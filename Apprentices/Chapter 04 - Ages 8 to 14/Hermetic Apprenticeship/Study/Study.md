Educational instruction in the institutions of Mythic Europe begins
around age seven. Future lawyers and clerics are taught the basics of
the trivium, the first stage of medieval learning. An apprentice may
also find himself behind a reading desk, as it is perfectly reasonable
for a magus to send his apprentice to a mundane teacher. Rather than
spend precious time teaching an apprentice Latin, Artes Liberales and
Profession: Scribe, many magi use a tutor. Many covenants have such
professionals on staff, scholastic instructors paid to teach Academic,
Arcane, and other Abilities.
