ReCo 4

R: Voice, D: Mom, T: Ind

By manipulating the target's stomach and groin muscles, this spell
causes the target to release his water, resulting in a (usually)
embarrassing situation. This spell does not target or create urine, so
if the target has recently urinated, the spell has no effect. This
cantation is unfortunately popular with many apprentices.

(Base 2, + 2 Voice)
