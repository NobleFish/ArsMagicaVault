ReMe 5

R: Touch, D: Mom, T: Ind

This spell instantly awakens a target from sleep to alert consciousness.
It does not work on someone unconscious from Fatigue-level loss or
continuous magic. It will awaken someone who was magically put to sleep,
as long as the magic that put the person to sleep has ended.

(Base 4, +1 Touch)
