House Ex Miscellanea does not have any formal Gauntlet procedures, and
each of the distinctly different traditions that fall under this House
has different methods of determining when an apprentice finishes her
training. They do adhere to the Code by not teaching the final
instructions of the Parma Magica to an apprentice until after 15 years,
but any sort of Gauntlet imaginable is appropriate for the varied
members of this House. Wind wizards might have to collect Auram vis from
a mountainous stream, necromancers might have to speak with the ghost of
a Roman senator, and diviners might have to forecast the time of their
own death.
