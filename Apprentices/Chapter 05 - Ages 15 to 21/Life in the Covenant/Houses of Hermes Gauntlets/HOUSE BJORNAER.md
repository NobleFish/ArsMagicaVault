Apprenticeship is slightly different for House Bjornaer magi, who
recognize their apprentices as full magi after they assume their
heartbeast for the first time, regardless of age or training. Some
Bjornaer magi will undertake mock Gauntlets after 15 years of training,
especially if they are working with an apprentice from another House who
is attempting a Gauntlet at the same time.
