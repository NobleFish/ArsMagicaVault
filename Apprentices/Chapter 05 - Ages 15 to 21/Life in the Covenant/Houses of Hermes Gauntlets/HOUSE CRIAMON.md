Passing a Gauntlet is merely a formality for House Criamon, whose
apprentices are accepted as full magi once they are asked the Riddle of
the Magus. This Riddle is a personal enigmatic puzzle for the Criamon
magus, who spends the rest of his career seeking appropriate answers.
Still, a Criamon apprentice could be sent to a foreign envoy — perhaps a
magic creature, another magus, or a talking brook — to receive this
Riddle.
