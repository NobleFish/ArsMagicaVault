*Minor, General*

The character is particularly reckless when it comes to a particular
Ability, chosen when this Flaw is taken. This Ability should be one that
is important to the character, or the troupe may veto the choice of this
Flaw. When she fails, she does so dramatically. Whenever she rolls a
zero when using the Ability, that zero is treated as a botch. She may
need to roll extra botch dice depending on the circumstances, but she
always suffers the effects of at least a single botch. This Flaw may be
taken more than once; each time, it applies to a different Ability.
