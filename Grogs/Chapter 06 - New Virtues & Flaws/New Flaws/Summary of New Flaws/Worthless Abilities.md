*Minor, General*

A time-lost character (see Chapter 7: Grog Templates) must have about 30
experience points that have been rendered valueless by her shift in
time. For example, she may have Area Lore of 3 for a city that no longer
exists, or her Etiquette score may have been rendered useless by
changing social structures. Troupes may permit companions to purchase
this Flaw multiple times.

In the example of the legionaries given in the Time-lost Witnesses
template (see Chapter 7), their wasted ability is their Organization
Lore. The army to which it refers no longer exists.
