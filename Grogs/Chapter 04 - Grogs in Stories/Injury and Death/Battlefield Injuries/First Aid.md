First aid can prevent a wound from worsening for a short period of time,
allowing the patient to get to a place of safety for the proper healing
of his wounds. When tending a patient's wounds, a chirurgeon can make a
First Aid roll against the Improvement Ease Factor of his patient's most
serious wound on the Wound Recovery Table (**ArM5**, page 179). If this
is successful, the patient is treated as one category higher with
respect to Activity While Injured (**ArM5**, page 178). The effects of a
successful First Aid roll last just one day, and cannot be repeated. A
botched First Aid roll provokes an immediate Wound Recovery roll, and
the patient receives no First Aid bonus. First aid can also start the
healing process (see *Art & Academe*, page 61).

**First Aid Roll:**\
**Dexterity + Chirurgy + Recovery Modifiers (see Art & Academe, page
54) + Magical Aid + stress die vs Improvement Ease Factor**
