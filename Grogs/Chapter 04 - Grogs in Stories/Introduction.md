# Chapter 4: Grogs in Stories

**Ars Magica Fifth Edition** is a game about magi first and foremost,
with companions also being important in generating and contributing to
stories. Grogs are usually an insignificant third in the order of
importance. This chapter is concerned with getting grogs more involved
in stories, and with making them more than just a bunch of Combat
Totals.
