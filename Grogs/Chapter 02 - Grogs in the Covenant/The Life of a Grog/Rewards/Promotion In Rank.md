The highest reward offered by most covenants is a promotion. A standard
grog can be promoted to custos, and a custos may be promoted to
sergeant, or the equivalent for servants and specialists. They may even
aspire to be captain, chamberlain, or steward one day. Increased rank
has several privileges — better food, better accommodation, better pay,
and often a better pension.
