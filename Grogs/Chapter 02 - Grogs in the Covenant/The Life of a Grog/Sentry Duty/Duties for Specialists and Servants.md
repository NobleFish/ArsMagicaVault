The working day of most specialists in the covenant is from forenoon
until eventide (approximately 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.). If they are entitled to
food provided by the covenant, they eat after the soldiers at dawn and
dusk, and with them at noon. Other than that, they toil under their own
recognizance, or under the command of their workshop master or the
steward.

Servants need to fit their duties around the other inhabitants of the
covenant, but tend to keep similar hours to the specialists. Kitchen
staff rise earlier since breakfast needs to be cooked and bread prepared
for the whole day. There is usually someone in the kitchen at all times,
even if it is just a scullion stoking the fires under the ovens and
guarding the stores against hungry soldiers coming off a watch.
Chambermaids must often be opportunistic about when they can gain access
to the magi's quarters to clean, and become experts at slipping in and
out without being noticed.
