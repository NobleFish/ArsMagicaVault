Every covenant has duties that are considered to be the most unpleasant
of all; these are usually drawn by lot, but are occasionally assigned as
punishment. Typically, emptying the latrine pits is the worst of all
jobs, but at a covenant, worse jobs can doubtless be found — feeding
Master Hygwald's familiar, removing the rotting corpses from Master
Lucas's laboratory, or accompanying Master Orsinio on a mission to the
Black Bog of Doom.
