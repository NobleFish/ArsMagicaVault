Being dismissed from the service of a covenant is a step rarely taken
lightly. Covenants value their privacy, and dismissing a grog from a
steady job is likely to leave that individual harboring a great deal of
resentment that could be used against the covenant. Of course, magi have
ways of securing silence, from simple threats and sham curses, to more
direct magical measures, such as mutilation of the tongue and
modification of the memory.
