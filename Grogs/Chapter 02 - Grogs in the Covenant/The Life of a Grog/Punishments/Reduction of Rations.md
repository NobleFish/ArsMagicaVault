Grogs usually receive basic but nutritious fare of good grain,
occasional meat or fish, and a daily ration of ale or wine. The threat
of being reduced to barley (rather than wheat), having the meat ration
removed, and/ or the ale exchanged for small beer (or worse, milk!) for
a month is a good incentive to avoid bad behavior. A character who
spends one or more season a year on reduced rations suffers a +1 penalty
to his Living Conditions Modifier; if he is on reduced rations for three
or four seasons, the penalty is +2.
