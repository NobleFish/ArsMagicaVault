Grogs usually have a section of the covenant set aside for them. This is
usually divided into servants' quarters, soldiers' quarters, and
specialists' quarters, more for practical reasons than anything else.
Soldiers in particular, with irregular watch duties, can easily disturb
the sleep of others. These quarters usually consist of a large room;
each grog is assigned a bunk and a chest in which to keep personal
belongings. Custodes and higher ranks occasionally have private rooms,
or at least bunks further from the drafts and closer to the fire.
Married covenfolk often have a separate room.

There is usually a single mess hall set aside for all grogs to eat
together, but because of watch patterns not everyone eats at the same
time.
