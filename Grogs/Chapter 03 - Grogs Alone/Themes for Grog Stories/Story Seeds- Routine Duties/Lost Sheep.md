The grogs sent to gather vis are confronted by a horde of angry
peasants. It seems that the last couple of times that the grogs have
passed this way, sheep have gone missing immediately after; this time
the villagers have been expecting the strangers, and have lain in wait
for them. It seems that, on each visit to the vis source, the grogs
disturb some sort of supernatural creature, which goes on a rampage
right after they have left the region.
