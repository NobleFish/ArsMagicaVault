Clues advance a plot, whereas obstacles hinder it. This catch-all
category consists of all the barriers that the characters must overcome.
These might be literal obstacles in the form of a mountain to be scaled,
a river to be crossed, or a fortress to be breached, or metaphorical
barriers such as earning the trust of the village, or finding the right
person to bribe. In general, obstacles should become greater challenges
to the characters as the story approaches its climax.
