This package assumes that the character has received academic teaching
at a parish school, cathedral school, or monastic school, or from a
private tutor. Characters who have attended a university receive
additional experience points from their Social Status Virtues (see *Art
& Academe*, pages 90–92). Characters attending a parish school can only
take the three-year package, since these schools only teach Artes
Liberales and Latin.

**FIVE YEARS (CATHEDRAL SCHOOL):** +15 Artes Liberales, +30 Latin, +15
Theology, +15 Civil and Canon Law OR Philosophiae

**FIVE YEARS (MONASTIC SCHOOL):** +15 Artes Liberales, +30 Latin, +15
Theology, +15 Medicine OR Philosophiae

**FIVE YEARS (PRIVATE TUTOR OR UNIVERSITY):** +15 Artes Liberales, +30
Latin, +30 any Academic Ability

**THREE YEARS (PARISH SCHOOL):** +15 Artes Liberales, +30 Latin
