The *turb* is a term used to refer to the fighting force of a covenant,
the sum total of its warriors. Some put the origin of the word with the
same captain that gave the Order "grog," who shortened the Latin
*contubernium* (meaning a squad of eight legionaries who shared a tent
and cooking fire). The word has become conflated with *turba*, meaning
"mob;" and this has become the word most commonly used in the modern
Order of Hermes to describe its soldiers.
