The word *grex* (plural *greges*), meaning "troop" or "company" (whence
vir gregis is derived) is occasionally used in a contemptuous manner by
magi, since it also means "herd" or "flock." Nevertheless, it is still
used to describe the group of grogs that accompany magi on expeditions;
it thereby has a meaning similar to unit or squad. A grex typically
numbers up to six individuals, and a turb might be composed of several
greges as permanent units who work well together, or each grex might be
assembled anew, according to the demands of the task ahead of it.
