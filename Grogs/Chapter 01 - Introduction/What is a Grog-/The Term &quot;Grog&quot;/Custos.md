A *custos* ("watchman" or "keeper," plural *custodes*) is a favored
grog, usually one with skills that set him apart from his fellows, but a
custos can also distinguish himself with great deeds or uncommon
displays of loyalty. Custodes are often set apart from the rest of the
covenfolk and awarded the privileges of a higher social rank, such as a
better provision of food and wine, a few pence more each month in wages,
or other minor benefits. In some covenants, grogs can be raised to the
rank of custos at the whim of the magi, while others have a formal
procedure for promotions. Custodes are usually equated to specialists
when building a covenant (**ArM5**, page 71) and determining its
finances (*Covenants*, page 63).
