There are various origin stories for the term "grog," which seems to be
a word coined by the Order of Hermes. The most commonly held opinion is
that it has its origins at Arae Flaviae, the first multi-House covenant.
At this covenant, the men and women who accompanied the magi when they
journeyed from home were called *viri gregis* (meaning "men of the
band," singular *vir gregis*). This became corrupted, so the story goes,
by a hard-of-hearing captain and the non-Latin speaking covenfolk into
"grog." The term was spread around the Order through the *liber gregis*
(see insert). Other terms entered the Hermetic vernacular at the same
time: turb, custos, and consors.
