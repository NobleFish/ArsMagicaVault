The autocrat is a mundane whom some covenants appoint to oversee the
smooth running of the covenant. He or she is usually the most important
of all the covenfolk, bar the magi themselves; the covenant of Arae
Flaviae even permitted their autocrat to attend meetings of the wizards'
council.
