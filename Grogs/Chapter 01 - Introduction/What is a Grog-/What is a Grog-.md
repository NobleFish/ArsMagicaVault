Equating "grog" with "soldier" is an easy habit to fall into, since the
principal use of a covenant's non-magus inhabitants is to defend the
magi against mundane threats. However, grogs can be any kind of minor
character that has a support role: craftsmen, scholars, and so forth.
This book presents a large number of ideas and archetypes that address
all varieties of grogs.
