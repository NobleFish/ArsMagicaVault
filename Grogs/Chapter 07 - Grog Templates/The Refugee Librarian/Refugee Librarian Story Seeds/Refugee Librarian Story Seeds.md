This particular option has enormous potential for story. Covenfolk
provide obvious witnesses to the events that befell a magus or a
covenant, and their testimony could be troublesome to the guilty. The
knowledge they bring with them also provides seeds into numerous
treasure-hunting opportunities, as new vis sources, auras, and regiones
are disclosed. And the prescient among the grogs may take such books and
devices as they can before finally abandoning their home.
