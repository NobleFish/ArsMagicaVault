The Confessor, as presented here, is a religious figure who steers the
character away from a path of sin. But as neither Islam nor Judaism
provide for anyone to intercede between the individual and their God,
the Confessor role is instead a secular confidante or trusted advisor.
The nature of the relationship may mean that a religious figure is still
appropriate, but with no implied doctrine present.
