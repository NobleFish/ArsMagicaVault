The Confessor may be anyone from an inexperienced young priest, through
a mature monk, to an elderly imam. But he tends to have high
Intelligence, Communication, and Presence characteristics. There is an
opportunity to build long relationships between a younger Confessor and
the magi of the covenant, and see those relationships grow throughout
the saga. The character can be used by the storyguide to confront
characters with their actions, and help the players explore their
consequences.
