*This is not the first time we have discussed these matters, maga Umbra,
and I fear it shall not be the last.*

A magus' soul is imperiled just as much, if not more so, by temptation
and obsession as any person's. The abstract allure of knowledge and
power provide new opportunities for sin to take hold and guide the magus
away from the righteous path. So it is important for the magus to make
peace with his maker and be absolved of sin. This is where the Confessor
comes in.
