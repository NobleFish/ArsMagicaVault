Grogs have a limited number of slots open for Virtues and Flaws and so
have a limited range of Supernatural Abilities open to them. Companions
and Mythic Companions may take more of these. They may also take Story
Flaws relating to the events, perhaps Plagued by Supernatural Entity, or
even Dark Secret, in addition to a Magical Air.
