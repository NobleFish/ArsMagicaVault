The ferryman most likely has had an athletic childhood spent out on the
marshes, so this package is used as the base. Abilities are then bought
to suit the character. Once play starts, the Role-bound Ferryman uses
the following Training Packages with alterations based on his situation
and Abilities:

**THREE YEARS (COVENFOLK):** +5 (Area) Lore, +20 Covenant Lore, +15
Craft: Boatbuilder, +5 Order of Hermes Lore

**THREE YEARS (LOCAL):** +15 (Area) Lore, +15 Folk Ken, +15
(Organization) Lore

**THREE YEARS (MAGICIAN):** +15 Magic Lore, +15 Premonitions, +15 Second
Sight

**THREE YEARS (SAILOR):** +5 (Area) Lore, +20 Profession: Ferryman, +5
Survival, +15 Swim

**THREE YEARS (SCOUT):** +15 (Area) Lore, +15 Awareness, +15 Stealth
