Time-lost characters have been personally selected by faeries to be
removed from time, then released, at a particular moment and in a
particular place, to cause interesting stories to be told. They have,
therefore, an unusual range of Virtues, and these may suit the
covenant's needs in a particular saga with an eerie exactness. Time-lost
characters may suffer a Delusion that they can return to their own time.
