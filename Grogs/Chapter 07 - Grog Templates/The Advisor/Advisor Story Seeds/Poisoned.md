The Tribunal meets and the Advisor lies dying, poisoned by a
non-Hermetic potion seemingly resistant to the simple healing spells.
Some of the magi race to find a cure for the poison while keeping the
Advisor's condition secret from their enemies. The others must conduct
Tribunal business and discover the would-be assassin, all without the
one man they have come to rely on.
