In Mythic Europe, only those from a moderately wealthy background have
the option to choose a trade for themselves; most peasants take on their
fathers' profession, or that of the master to whom they are apprenticed.
For someone from a humble background to become a Jack-of-All-Trades,
something else must have driven him from home and prevented him from
settling down. This might be as simple as a wanderlust, a thirst to see
the world and witness new experiences that drives him ever onwards.
Alternatively, he might make others uncomfortable due to poor hygiene,
odious personal habits, outspoken beliefs, or a general aura of
wrongness. Finally, he may carry some sort of curse or bad luck that
discourages anyone from wanting to become his friend, for fear of being
caught up in the backlash. This could be a very lonely or pitiful
character.
