Some say an army marches on its stomach. But an army of horses marches
very poorly indeed without proper hoof care, or if not professionally
shod. An expert in this field is a prized asset in any army, caravan, or
covenant. The Farrier is an important professional, needed wherever
horses are in use. He is more craftsman than animal wrangler. While
regular stable boys and groomsmen take care of the day-to-day tending
and the hard labor of fetching water and mucking out the stables, the
Farrier has more specialized skills. His primary area of expertise is
caring for horses' hooves and fitting horseshoes, as well as general
equine veterinary skills. The village blacksmith might have wrought the
shoe, and could shoe the horse, if need be. But the Farrier can adapt,
modify, and fit it optimally. Where many horses need care, a specialized
Farrier is of great value. Some people even attribute to a good Farrier
supernatural abilities; this may or may not be the case.
