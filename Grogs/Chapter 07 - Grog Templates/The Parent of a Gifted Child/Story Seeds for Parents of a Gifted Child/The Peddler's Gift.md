Working as a purchase agent for the covenant, the peddler-grog can deal
in all sorts of interesting trinkets, and would be particularly
interested in magical goods. With his own money, the peddler buys a gift
for his child from a mysterious man, thinking the Gifted child would be
happy with such a gift from the once-impoverished father. However, the
gift is in fact a device from an enemy, designed to annoy, scry, or even
put the covenant in danger. Instead of attacking a magus as intended,
the poor apprentice suffers the magical device's effects. If the peddler
does not have a Gifted child, then normal business transactions could
land the device in the covenant's hands, although full magi may be a
little more wary.
