Someone out in the populace is spreading the word about the evils of the
Order of Hermes. The words "child sacrifice" and "murder" are used. He
is building a following of torch-wielding peasants, and it is only a
matter of time before he turns on the covenant. There is also a rumor
that the peasants have captured a young mage. The magi and companions
are too obvious to try a rescue. The magus' mundane parent and the other
grogs must try their best.
