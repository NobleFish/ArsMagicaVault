*Your wine, my lady.*

Many important and vital jobs are done by insignificant mundanes, who go
unnoticed before the eyes of magi, lords, and clergy alike. These unsung
heroes rarely win a place in history. However, a conniving master can
put this virtual invisibility to much use. This bland and demure
maidservant can fill many roles.
