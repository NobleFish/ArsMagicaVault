Chapter 8 of the *Art & Academe* supplement provides rules for artists
in Mythic Europe. Production Artists use their Craft Abilities, such as
Craft: Stone Mason, Craft: Glazier, and Craft: Silversmith to produce
works of art that have a value far beyond that of a mundane example of a
craftsman's work.

This path opens up a whole new avenue for the Companion Craftsman, one
which can take him into the richest and most volatile levels of society.
It also opens up new rules for the creation of art and the acquisition
of new benefits.
