A companion Craftsman has access to the Touched by (Realm) Major
Supernatural Virtue, which in turn allows him to craft Wondrous Items
(as per the rules in *City & Guild*, page 70). These enchanted items are
more limited in scope than Hermetic enchantments, but they allow the
companion to develop his own career and reputation as a craftsman of
note. And as the character is unGifted, he or she may be more easily
accepted in society, opening up opportunities not available to magi.
