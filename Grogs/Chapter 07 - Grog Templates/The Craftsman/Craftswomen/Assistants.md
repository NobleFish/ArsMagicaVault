A Craftsman may employ a number of assistants equal to his Leadership
score, though he may always employ one apprentice regardless of his
Leadership score. As well as helping in the workshop, these assistants
may be the source of new grogs to replace the old over time, and may
initiate stories of their own.

Each assistant adds half his Craft Ability score to the Craftsman's
Workshop Total. If using multiple assistants, their Craft Scores should
first be totalled and then divided by two.
