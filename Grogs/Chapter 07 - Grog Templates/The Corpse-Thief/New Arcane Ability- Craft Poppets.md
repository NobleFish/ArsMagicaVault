The character can make effigies of individuals out of wax, straw, and
cloth that house an arcane connection and simultaneously act as a
sympathetic connection to the people they represent. A simple effigy is
good for one use, takes an hour to make, and requires a Dexterity +
Craft Poppets roll against an Ease Factor of 9. A permanent poppet takes
a week to make, and has an Ease Factor of 12. Permanent poppets can
accept a new Arcane Connection once the previous one has expired;
naturally, this must be to the same person. Unlike other symbolic
representations, a poppet made with this Ability may be used by any
person, not just the character who made it. **Specialties:** a
particular type of target — women, magi, dragons, kings, etc.
