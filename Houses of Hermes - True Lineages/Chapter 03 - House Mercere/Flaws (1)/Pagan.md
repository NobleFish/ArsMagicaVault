''Major, Story

You do not follow the teachings of the Church, and have never been
baptized. This tends to upset those in authority in Mythic Europe and
frighten common peoplewho learn of it. You do not observe Christian
holidays, and you try to avoid churchmen and the Dominion. You cannot
pretend to go along with society, however, because you believe that it
would displease your gods and incur their wrath — you might suffer grave
supernatural consequences if you take Communion or appear to worship
gods other than your own. You may begin with Magic Lore or Faerie Lore,
depending on the speciﬁcs of your faith. (This is not a Flaw in areas of
Mythic Europe with substantial pagan populations, but by 1220 the only
such areas are in parts of the Novgorod Tribunal.)''
