*In this text, the term “Redcap” refers to an unGifted member of House
Mercere. Gifted members are called “Mercere magi” or “Gifted Merceres,”
though note that all members of the House are considered magi for the
purposes of their Oath and the Code. The general term “Mercere” refers
to anyone, Gifted or unGifted, who belongs to the House.*
