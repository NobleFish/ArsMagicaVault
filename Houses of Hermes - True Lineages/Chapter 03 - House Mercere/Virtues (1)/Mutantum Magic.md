''Minor, Hermetic

You are descended from the Mutantum lineage, and can thus invent
formulaic spells and magic items that take advantage of Boosting,
Harnessing and Tethering (see Mutantum Magic, under Magic, above). You
may also take the Tamed Magic virtue, and half of your starting spells
may be “tamed” versions of common spells. Most characters with Mutantum
Magic belong to House Mercere and consider themselves Mutantes, and all
of them must be descended by blood from the Founder or one of his
ancestors.''
