''MERCURY’S BLESSING

R: Touch, D: Year, T: Ind, Level 25, Ritual

This ritual imbues the target with a kind of supernatural aura, similar
to Might, which improves the chances that children conceived and born
during its duration will have The Gift or heroic qualities. It is said
that this magic is a sign the gods are watching over the subject, and it
is often incorporated into wedding and fertility ceremonies of those
within the Cult of Heroes.

(Mercurian Ritual)''
