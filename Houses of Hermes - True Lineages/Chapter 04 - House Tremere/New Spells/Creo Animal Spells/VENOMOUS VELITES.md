R: Sight, D: Sun, T: Group, Level 45; Requisite: Rego

Creates a swarm of a hundred poisonous scorpions. The Rego requisite
allows the magus to command them to attack speciﬁc targets. If used in
combination with spells like “To Mark With Umbrage”, the magus can
command his scorpions to kill anyone on the battleﬁeld not bearing his
sigil. It is important for magi with weak Parmae Magicae to ensure they
bear the mark.

(Base 5, +3 Sight, +2 Sun, +2 Group, +1 extra effect from requisite.)
