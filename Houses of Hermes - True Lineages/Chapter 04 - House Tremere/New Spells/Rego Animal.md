<span id="THE UNFAITHFUL FAVOR"></span>THE UNFAITHFUL FAVOR

R: Touch, D: Sun T: Ind, Level 45

This spell commands a silk handkerchief to strangle an enemy for whom
the magus has an arcane connection. The handkerchief appears near the
victim and swiftly wraps itself about their throat. They take damage
according to the rules in Ars Magica until death or the destruction of
the handkerchief. Conscious victims can often avoid the handkerchief, so
this spell is usually cast at night. After the victim dies, the
handkerchief hides itself in their bedding, or up their sleeve.

(Base 25, +1 Touch, +2 Sun, +1 commands in addition to transport)
