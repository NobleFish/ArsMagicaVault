The Charioteer techniques are ﬂashy and intimidating. They subdue foes
psychologically. After a successful attack, the essedarius may choose to
do no damage and instead create the impression of a powerful and
dreadful attack that failed, but by the barest chance. The intention is
to frighten the foe into surrender, which may occur if they fail a Brave
check of 3 + 2 per Fatigue level the essedarius has declined to take
during this certamen. Magi ﬁghting in this style can force a similar
Brave check, but the target is 3 + 2 per fatigue level forgone in this
round of combat.
