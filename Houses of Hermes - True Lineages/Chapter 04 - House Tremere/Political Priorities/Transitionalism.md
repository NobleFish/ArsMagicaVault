House Tremere advocates changes to the Code that would permit Tribunals
to compel magi to assist the Order. Contributions to research covenants,
embassies to foreign magical groups and expeditions to distant lands are
currently voluntary. Miserly covenants often refuse to assist, knowing
that House Bonisagus will share any useful information with them. House
Tremere favors a common purse to pay for these things. They also believe
that the Order should be able to compel material assistance from every
covenant during war.
