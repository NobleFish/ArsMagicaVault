Magi accompanying Quaesitors for protection are normally provided with
letters of authority and play their part in the investigation. Powerful
magi who perform this role become known as Hoplites.

Many members of Houses Flambeau, Tytalus and Tremere value this role
highly, as they often get the ﬁrst opportunity to hunt down outcast
magi. The position is rewarding primarily to the Hoplite’s reputation,
but can be materially rewarding at well.
