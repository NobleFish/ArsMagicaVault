Fines are paid to compensate the Tribunal and the prosecution for the
time and effort spent pursuing the case; one third is given to the
prosecutor, two-thirds to the Tribunal coffers. Tribunal coffers are
kept by the Praeco and accounted for by the senior Mercere. In the case
of a March, the prosecutor receives one fourth of the outcast’s estate
(if the victim was also the prosecutor he receives one half in total).
By Grand Tribunal ruling the Tribunal coffers cannot beneﬁt from calling
a March. The remaining half of the estate goes to the inheritor.
