Over the years many magi have sought out clues to the location of the
thief’s tomb. None succeeded, but many came close and wrote of their
investigations. If a magus were to gather these tracts and piece the
clues together, he might be able to discover the tomb. However, to claim
the prize he must contend with an angry ghost.

Guernicus never wanted the tomb to be found, so many members of his
House would feel disturbing it to be an insult to their Founder’s
memory. Although not illegal, such an act will antagonize many
