By Grand Tribunal ruling, a Quaesitor in good standing can demand that
magi cooperate with an ofﬁcial investigation. Without giving up their
legal rights, a magus must assist an investigation if asked. Thus the
magus should answer reasonable questions related to the investigation.
He is never required to reveal legal magical secrets, but otherwise he
should answer honestly and without evasion. Failure to cooperate with an
investigation is a low crime.

Magi should also allow their servants to be questioned. Similarly, if
servants are commanded to evade questions or even lie, the responsible
magi can be charged.
