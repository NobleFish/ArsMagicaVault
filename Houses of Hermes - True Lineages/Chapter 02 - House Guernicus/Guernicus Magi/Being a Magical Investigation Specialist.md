Guernicus magi with a particular aptitude are often called in to provide
a brief service in support of a main investigation. These services
should not disrupt their study. Most have spells that allow them travel
magically and devices that enable Quaesitors to call on them as needed.
In particular, a specialist is often in great demand at Tribunal for
endorsing testimony.

The most experienced specialists conduct research that pushes the
boundaries of Hermetic Theory. One of the dreams of these magi is to
break the Limit of Time. Rumors that the Quaesitores have a spell that
can see into the past often circulate. If the researchers ever succeed,
they will not advertise it.
