This combination can be used to make spell traces harder to detect by
aging the trace. However, a spell to magically age a trace will leave
its own fresh trace; this is generally counterproductive. However, if
cast with Shroud Magic it can still prove useful. So far, no way has
been found of designing a spell that leaves no trace.
