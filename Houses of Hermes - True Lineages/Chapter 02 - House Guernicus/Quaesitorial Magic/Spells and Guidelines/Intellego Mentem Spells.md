<span id="EAR OF TRUTH"></span>EAR OF TRUTH

R: Per, D: Conc, T: Hearing, Level 30

The caster can tell if he is hearing the truth or not. If a speaker’s
Magic Resistance blocks the enhanced sense, the caster is aware of it as
an absence. Many Quaesitors develop a high level of mastery of this
spell and use it with the Acute Sense ability to great effect.

(Base 10, +1 Conc, +3 Hearing)
