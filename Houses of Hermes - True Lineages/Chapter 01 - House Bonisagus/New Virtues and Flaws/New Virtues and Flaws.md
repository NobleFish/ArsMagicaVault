Over the years House Bonisagus has included many members with particular
Virtues and Flaws. Since the House can select other magi’s apprentices,
youths who exhibit these powers are often adopted into the House.
However, this does not mean that Bonisagi are the sole recipients of
these boons. Virtues and Flaws marked with an asterisk depend on the
structure of House Bonisagus, and so are exclusive to the House. Others
are not.
