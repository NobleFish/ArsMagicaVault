*Perhaps the most obscure tradition of magi Ex Miscellanea is that of
the Mechanicals, also known as the Rusticani or “rustic magi.” These are
folk magicians who generally eschew interaction with the Order of Hermes
and its covenants, and instead live among the common people in villages
and other rural communities, whom they consider to be their covenfolk.
They have developed a form of magical craft that allows them to build
spells and enchanted devices from mundane objects, the effects of which
they share freely with their people — but they are disparaged by most
other magi for their reclusive and peasant-like ways, and besides
respecting the Oath, few of them ever acknowledge that they belong to
the Order at all.*
