*The Pharmacopoeians comprise a tradition of magi who can trace their
ancient lineage back to Crateuas: author of the first pharmacopoeia,
illustrator of the first herbal, and the original Root-Cutter. Their
magic relies heavily on the curative magic in herbs and plants, which
makes them some of the Order’s best healers.*
