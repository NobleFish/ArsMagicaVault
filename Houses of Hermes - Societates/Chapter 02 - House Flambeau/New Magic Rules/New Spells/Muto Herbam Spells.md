**Aegis of Unbreakable Wood**

MuHe 15

R: Touch, D: Sun, T: Ind.

Makes a wooden shield (or object of comparable dimensions) as strong as
iron, increasing its Defense bonus by +1. This is the best benefit magic
can provide without increasing the size of the shield. The weight (and
Load) of the shield are unchanged. It remains susceptible to fire, but
is otherwise nearly indestructible.

(Base 4, +1 Touch, +2 Sun)
