ReAq 10

R: Voice, D: Sun, T: Part

Freeze a circular portion of a body of water into solid ice. Anyone
wading or swimming in the water is trapped in the ice. Magic Resistance
does not prevent the ice from surrounding a character and holding her
fast. The area affected by the spell is the same size as a base
Individual: a roughly bowl-shaped volume about five paces across and two
paces deep at the center.

Since the ice is magical, it only inflicts cold damage if it penetrates
Magic Resistance. Rules for cold damage are given in ArM5, page 181; the
ice created by this spell inflicts a base damage of +1. Characters who
are completely encased in ice begin to suffocate (see ArM5, page 180).

A trapped character may break free of the ice by making a Strength roll.
Characters may also chop their way out of the ice using tools or
weapons, but this takes time. Magic, including Perdo Aquam or Creo Ignem
spells, may be able to free a character instantly
