Characters who are can’t see one another may not fight as part of a
trained group. Effective group tactics require the combatants to be
aware of what the others are doing.

An invisible character may serve as the vanguard of a group. If an
untrained group contains some invisible members, everyone in the group
gains an extra botch die per invisible character. On a botch, combatants
may blunder into an invisible character or even accidentally strike him
with their weapons. A group of allies cannot defend an invisible
character (see ArM5, page 173) unless they have some means of knowing
where he is.
