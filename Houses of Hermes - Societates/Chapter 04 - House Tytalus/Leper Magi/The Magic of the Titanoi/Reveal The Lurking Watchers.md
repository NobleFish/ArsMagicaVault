InVi 30

R: Per, D: Conc, T: Vision

This spell allows the caster to see any creatures of the Magic realm
within his field of view and identify them as possessing Magic Might.
The spell does not distinguish between what is spiritual and what is
not, so if a spirit has an animal form, the caster may mistake it for a
magical animal. The caster receives no information about the type of
creature except that which can be deduced from its appearance. This
spell must penetrate the Magic Resistance of the creature; if it fails
to penetrate a spirit’s Might, then the spirit cannot be seen by the
caster.

(Base 5, +1 Conc, +4 Vision)
