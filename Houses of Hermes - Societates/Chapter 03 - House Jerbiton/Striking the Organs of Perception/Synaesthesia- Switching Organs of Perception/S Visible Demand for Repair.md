R: Touch, D: Ring, T: Circ

This spell transforms the groans and squeaks of damaged pieces of
equipment into iconic species reminiscent of the magus’s sigil. This
makes it easier for magi to find the flaws in damaged parts, granting a
+3 bonus to Craft or Profession rolls.
