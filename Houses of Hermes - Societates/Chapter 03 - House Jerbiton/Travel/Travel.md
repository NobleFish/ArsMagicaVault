Jerbiton magi travel for pleasure, but also because it broadens a
magus’s repertoire without formal study. This is because a character
cannot create a thing she cannot imagine. Magi who travel see things
they have never imagined, allowing them to create or mimic a broader
range of things. Many Jerbiton magi travel frequently throughout their
lives, but arduous travel is best undertaken while young and vigorous.
House Jerbiton has two traditions which help to season young magi in
this way, fosterage and tourism.
