Mythic Europeans believe that the world’s history is evolving as
ordained by God, toward an outcome predicted in the Book of Revelations.
As first enunciated by Eusebius, the Christianized Roman Empire is the
tool of God, destined to prepare the world for the Kingdom of Peace,
which Jesus will rule after Armageddon.

When Rome fell different people adjusted their beliefs to suit local
circumstances. In the West, the role of preparer of the world was given
to the Church, and then to the Holy Roman Emperor. In the East, the fall
of Rome did not matter, because, decades before, its role had been
accepted by New Rome : Constantinople.
