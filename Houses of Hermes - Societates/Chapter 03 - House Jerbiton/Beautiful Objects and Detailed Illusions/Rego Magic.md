Magi can use Rego magic to do anything a mundane artist could do with
tools. The Finesse roll required is three more than the Ease Factor the
mundane craftsman would have to make.

The Ease Factor is also modified by the length of time it would take a
mundane artist to complete the work of art.

To do what a mundane artist could do in a day does not change the Ease
Factor. To do what a mundane artist could do in a month adds +3 to the
Ease Factor. To do what a mundane artist could do in a season adds +6 to
the Ease Factor. To do what a mundane artist could do in a year adds +9
to the Ease Factor.
