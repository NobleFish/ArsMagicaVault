Perdo Mentem spells allow magi to diminish the mental capacity of their
targets. This has a base level of 4, noted on page 150 of ArM5. The
example given is Trust of Childlike Faith, which destroys adult judgment
regarding truth and falsity. Hermetic magic can create many similar
spells, which diminish the target’s mental capacity in a host of other
ways. Using Trust of Childlike Faith as a template, characters skilled
in Perdo Mentem may inhibit a target’s ability to do any of the
following:

- Ignore irrelevant detail to find the core properties of a thing
- Articulate words
- Assess details without emotional bias
- Command muscles to complete learned movements
- Concentrate Deduce facts from observations
- Estimate the passage of time
- Form memories or learn
- Infer the properties of a thing from previous experience with similar
  things
- Integrate sensory information (which makes the character appear
  clumsy, as if inebriated)
- Read facial expressions
- Recover from nervous shock
- Write
- Understand and form words
- Understand simple mathematical concepts
- Understand speech
