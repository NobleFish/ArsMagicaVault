This is a big book, as it needs to be to explain all the rules and
background for **Ars Magica**. The first chapter, this [[Chapter 01 - Introduction|Introduction]],
provides a quick overview of the game, and explains the basic
die-rolling mechanic.

Chapter 2, [[Chapter 02 - The Order of Hermes|The Order of Hermes]], describes the order of wizards that is
central to the game.

Chapters 3, 4 and 5, [[Chapter 03 - Characters||Characters]], [[Chapter 04 - Virtues and Flaws|Virtues and Flaws]], and [[Chapter 05 - Abilities|Abilities]] contain all the information necessary to create a character. 

Chapter 6, [[Chapter 06 - Covenants|Covenants]], deals with creating the place where the characters live.

Chapters 7, 8, and 9, [[Chapter 07 - Hermetic Magic|Hermetic Magic]], [[Chapter 08 - Laboratory|Laboratory]], and [[Chapter 09 - Spells|Spells]], describe
Hermetic Magic. 

Chapter 10, [[Chapter 10 - Long-Term Events|Long Term Events]], covers things that happen to the characters over months or years, including character improvement, aging and death, and Warping by supernatural power. 

Chapter 11, [[Chapter 11 - Obstacles|Obstacles]], details the things that might harm the characters, most
notably combat.

Chapter 12, [[Chapter 12 - Realms|Realms]], explains the four different sorts of supernatural
power in Mythic Europe, the Divine, Faerie, Infernal, and Magical
Realms. 

Chapter 13, [[Chapter 13 - Bestiary|Bestiary]], provides a sample of creatures from each
of these Realms. 

Chapter 14, [[Chapter 14 - Mythic Europe|Mythic Europe]], provides hints on making
your game feel like it happens in the mythic world.

Chapters 15 and 16, [[Chapter 15 - Stories|Stories]] and [[Chapter 16 - Sagas|Sagas]], provides advice on using the preceding material to actually make a game that the troupe enjoys.
