The next step in creating a Hermetic magus is choosing his House. The
twelve Houses are described starting on <a
href="Ars_Magica_Definitive_Edition,_Chapter_Two:_The_Order_of_Hermes#The_Houses_of_Hermes"
class="wikilink" title="page 12">page 12</a>, and summarized in the
nearby table. Membership in a House grants a particular benefit at
character creation, which is listed in the table. A magus can only be a
member of one House.
