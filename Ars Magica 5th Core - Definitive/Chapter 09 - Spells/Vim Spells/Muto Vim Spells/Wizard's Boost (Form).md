R: Touch, D: Mom, T: Ind\
You cast this spell as another spell of a level less than or equal to
this one is cast. The effect of the other spell increases 5 levels in
power, but not past the level of the Wizard's Boost. The effects of the
extra 5 levels of power are for the storyguide to determine. A Wizard's
Boost may never affect a single spell more than once. There are ten
versions of this spell, one for each Hermetic Form.\
(Base effect, +1 Touch)
