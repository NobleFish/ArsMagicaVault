R: Per, D: Conc, T: Touch\
The caster can feel any magical means currently being used to spy on
him. The representation varies in a way that depends on the spell, but
it often feels like something touching the magus's back. For example, a
magus being spied upon with Enchantment of the Scrying Pool might feel
wet fingers, while a magus who is being tracked by The Inexorable Search
might feel a finger running over his skin. This spell detects the use of
Intellego spells of up to double the level of this spell. A special
spell is required because most Intellego spells are designed to be
subtle, and they are more subtle at higher levels.\
(Base effect)
