R: Voice, D: Mom, T: Ind\
A brilliant red flash explodes in the air where you designate. If you
target a person's face the target needs a Stamina stress roll against an
Ease Factor of 9 to avoid temporary blindness. If the target botches the
roll, he is permanently blinded. If temporarily blinded, he can try to
make a simple Stamina roll against an Ease Factor of 9 each minute to
recover. The flash is equivalent to looking straight at the sun. In Rose
of Jerbiton's version of this spell, there is a faint odor of roses in
the area where the flash explodes.\
(Base 5, +2 Voice)
