R: Arc D: Mom, T: Ind\
Gives you a hazy mental image of an animal you have an Arcane Connection
to. Wounds caused by an animal are an Arcane Connection to that animal
with a lifetime of a few hours. This spell can thus be used to find out
what was responsible for an attack. All spells cast by Chavin of Tytalus
can be recognized because they promote or advocate war or violence. In
his version of this spell, the claws and teeth of the beast appear more
dangerous.\
(Base 1, +4 Arcane Connection)
