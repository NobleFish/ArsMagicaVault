R: Touch, D: Mom, T: Ind\
Destroys an animal's vision. The blindness heals as if it is a Medium
Wound. The title refers to the hood used to cover a falcon's eyes when
it is not hunting. When cast by Asaron of Flambeau, this spell causes
the animals it targets to bear a faint silhouette of a flame in their
eves.\
(Base 20, +1 Touch)
