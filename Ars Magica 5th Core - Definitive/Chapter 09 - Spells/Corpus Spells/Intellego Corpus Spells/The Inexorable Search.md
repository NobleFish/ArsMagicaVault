R: Arc, D: Conc, T: Ind\
Determines the location of a specific person. To cast the spell you need
a map and an Arcane Connection. After casting the spell, you can move
your finger over the map at the rate of one hour per square foot of map.
When your finger passes over the person's location as represented on the
map, you sense the person's presence. (If the person is not in the area
covered by the map, no sensations result.) You can locate the person to
within a thumb's width on the map. A similar spell allows you to search
for a dead body (Tracing the Trail of Death's Stench).\
(Base 3, +4 Arc, +1 Conc)
