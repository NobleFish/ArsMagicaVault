R: Touch, D: Ring, T: Circle\
As Ward Against Faeries of the Water (ReAq Gen), but for faeries of the
air. The faerie cannot directly or indirectly break the magic circle,
nor can they use ranged attacks or magic to affect those within it. From
some angles at night, the ring may be seen as a violethued dome.\
(Base effect)
