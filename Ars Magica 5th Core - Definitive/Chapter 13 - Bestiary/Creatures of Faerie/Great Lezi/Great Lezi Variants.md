Gigantic fauns are found in the Basque country in Iberia. Once they were
much like other fauns, although even then they had striking red fur.
When humans won the right to till the country, these fauns became
agricultural faeries. They use their great height to plan agricultural
improvements, perform feats of remarkable engineering, and shout
warnings when predators approach. In the region they are called
Basajauns, but this is confusing because Basajaun is a large, wild faun
in the folklore of the surrounding peoples, who keeps house with his
wife and human slaves.
