Despite the Order's attempts to regulate it and make it a science, magic
remains an art. No matter how skilled a practitioner you are, and no
matter what precautions you take (short of not using magic at all), your
magic will sometimes get out of your control. From the danger of casting
a spell improperly to the danger of the enigmatic Wizard's Twilight,
magic is filled with hazards.
