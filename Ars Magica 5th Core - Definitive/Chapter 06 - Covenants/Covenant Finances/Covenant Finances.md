Covenants almost always need some source of mundane wealth to obtain
things like food and clothing. Even those that are entirely
self-sufficient have a source of mundane wealth — it just provides food
and clothing directly. It is entirely possible to play the game while
leaving these matters entirely in the background, but this section is
for troupes that want a bit more detail in the finances of the covenant,
so that they can tell stories about increasing its wealth, or about
dealing with threats to its mundane income. The *Covenants* supplement
has a lot more detail on this topic, and *City and Guild* provides more
general rules and background for craft and trading that covenants could
also use to make money.
