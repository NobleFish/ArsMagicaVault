Casting Tablets allow magi to cast Formulaic or Ritual spells that they
do not know (see page 263). Not all covenants have such things, and
those that do normally have them for spells that they do not need very
often. Some covenants cast their Aegis of the Hearth from a Casting
Tablet, but that is rather risky.

Five levels of Casting Tablets cost two Build Points, and use the same
level limits as Lab Texts.

Casting Tablet Cost: 2 Build Points per five levels
