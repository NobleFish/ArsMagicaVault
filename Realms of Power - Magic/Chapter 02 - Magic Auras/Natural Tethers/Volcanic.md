''Tether 1: A perfectly shaped, smoking volcanic cone that periodically
emits ash and lava, sufficient to be spectacular but rarely
catastrophic. An expanse of boiling mud and sulfurous pools. Tether 2: A
vigorous and dangerous volcano that is most active at the turn of the
seasons. A powerful, boiling hot geyser that spouts every seven hours.

As a result of Warping, inhabitants become immune to sulfur in the air
and water and can tolerate high temperatures. Suitable Minor Flaws are
Weak Sense of Taste or Weak Sense of Smell, and Sensitive to Cold(see
Chapter 4: Characters, New and Modified Flaws). The obvious suitable
Minor Virtue is an appropriate Lesser Immunity.''
