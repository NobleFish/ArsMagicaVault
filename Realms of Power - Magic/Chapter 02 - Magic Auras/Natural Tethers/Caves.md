''Tether 1: A magnificent cavern displaying many natural pillars,
stalagmites, stalactites, and other wondrous rock formations. A cave
with walls and ceiling encrusted with crystals.

Tether 2: A group of magnificent caverns linked by arches, tunnels, and
shafts. Extensive caverns with some tunnels that lead down a long way
towards the center of the Earth.

As a result of Warping, inhabitants become accustomed to the enclosed
environment and low light levels. Suitable Minor Flaws are Sensitive
Sight (see Chapter 4: Characters, New and Modified Flaws), a Fear of
open spaces, or Disfigured due to unnaturally pallid skin or
strange-looking eyes adjusted to poor illumination.''
