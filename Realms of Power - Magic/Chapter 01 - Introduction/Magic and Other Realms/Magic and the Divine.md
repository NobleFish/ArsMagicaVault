Most holy characters and Divine beings do not recognize Magic as a
separate supernatural realm, treating it either as an expression of
God’s gifts if used virtuously, or a tool of the Devil when used
sinfully. Magical beings are seen as part of nature, and described as
either blessed creatures if they serve God’s will, or corrupted beasts
if they act against him. Magical power is lessened in the Dominion, and
many magi believe this is because the power of the Divine is even more
perfect than that of Magic.
