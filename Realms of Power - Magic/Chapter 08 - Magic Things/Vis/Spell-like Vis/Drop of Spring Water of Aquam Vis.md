CrAq Level 20

R: Touch, D: Mom, T: Ind, Ritual

This Aquam vis takes the form of a drop of water from a magical spring.
If the vis ever comes into contact with the bare earth, a fast-flowing
spring forms on the spot. The spring does not form if it touches the
floor of a structure, but a spring is created upon any patch of ground,
even if it is solid rock.

(Base 5, +1 Touch)
