R: Per, D: Mom, T: Ind, Ritual

This Creo vis takes the form of a seed from a fruit tree. When planted
the seed grows instantly into a mature non-magical tree laden with the
appropriate fruit. The seed need not be planted deep in the earth; it
grows to maturity if covered by just a handful of dirt. But the tree
survives only if planted in an appropriate location and climate.

(Base 40)
