0 points, Init equal to (Qik–2), Terram

R: Touch, D: Sun, T: Ind

The elemental clubs at an opponent with its heavy limbs, requiring a
normal melee attack. If the elemental is at least equal in Size to its
opponent, a successful Crush attack can pin him to the ground, and the
elemental adds its (Might/5, rounded up) to its Grapple Strength to
prevent its victim escaping (ArM5, page 174). If the elemental
successfully grapples, then any equipment carried by its victim is
damaged; fragile items are destroyed, robust items are broken (and are
destroyed if affected again by this power), and metal items are damaged
(and are broken if affected again by this power).
