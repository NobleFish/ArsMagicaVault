Rego Ignem 20

R: Voice, D: Conc, T: Ind

This spell allows the caster to control the actions of a fire elemental
up to Size 0, if the Penetration Total exceeds the elemental’s Magic
Might. For larger elementals, versions of the spell with a Size modifier
to the Target are required. If an elemental is already under
supernatural control, the most recent spell or power used is the one
that commands it.

(base 5, +2 Voice, +1 Conc) \|}
