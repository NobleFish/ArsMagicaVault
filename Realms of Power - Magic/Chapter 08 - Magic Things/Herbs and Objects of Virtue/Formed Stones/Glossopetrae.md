A Stone Adder’s Tongue or Glossopetrae of Virtue contains 1 pawn of
Aquam vis and can be Enriched to become an antidote to liquid poisons.
The poisoned character loses a Fatigue level rather than taking a wound.
To be used, it must be put in the suspect liquid and left to stand for
at least two Diameters. Enrichment involves the capture of a poisonous
snake, which is milked for its venom. The stone is soaked in that venom
for a month, then washed every night for another month in fresh clean
rainwater that has been collected in a special bowl as it fell. The
stone should be kept in snakeskin. PeAq 20 (base 10, +1 Touch, +1 Part):
Lesser Power (20 levels, –1 Might (fatigue) cost)
