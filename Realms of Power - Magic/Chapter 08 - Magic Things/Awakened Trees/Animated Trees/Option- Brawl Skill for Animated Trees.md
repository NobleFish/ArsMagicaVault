For simplicity, troupes can simply assign a default Brawl skill of 3
(specialized in “branch”) to all animate trees. A more complex
alternative is to assign a different Brawl skill to each tree and
re-calculate its combat statistics accordingly. Under this rule, a tree
animated by a spell has a Brawl skill equal to the caster’s Finesse. A
tree animated by a spirit using Manifestation has the same Brawl skill
as the spirit, and uses the spirit’s Dexterity and mental
Characteristics (but the tree’s Strength, Stamina, and Quickness). \|}
