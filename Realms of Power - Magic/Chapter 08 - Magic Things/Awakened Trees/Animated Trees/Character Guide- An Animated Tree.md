- Magic Might: None, or activation cost of Manifestation (Herbam)
- Characteristics: Cun –3, Per –2, Pre –3, Com –3, Str +8, Sta +5, Dex
  –1, Qik –5
- Size: +5
- Season: Spring
- Virtues and Flaws: Clumsy
- Magic Qualities and Inferiorities: No Fatigue
- Personality Traits: Tree +3
- Combat: Branch: Init +5, Attack +7, Defense +1, Damage +10
- Soak: +15
- Wound Penalties: –1 (1–10), –3 (11–20), –5 (21–30), Incapacitated
  (31-40), Dead (41+)
- Abilities: Brawl 3 (branch)
- Natural Weapons: The weapon statistics for this tree’s branch are Init
  +10, Atk +4, Dfn +2, Dam +2. This makes its combat statistics work out
  exactly as listed in the spell Lord of the Trees (ArM5, page 139),
  when combined with its Brawl skill of 3.
- Vis: None

These statistics should be used for trees affected by spells such as
Lord of the Trees or Calling the Council of the Trees (ArM5, page 139),
or those animated by spirits. Under most circumstances, an animate tree
has no Might score. An animate tree can move its limbs and roots, and
can also uproot itself and shuffle about if the spell description says
it can. For this purpose, treat Manifestation as equivalent to the
spell, Freeing the Striding Tree (ArM5, page 139).
