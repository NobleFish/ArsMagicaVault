After a particularly fantastic event in which the characters used their
magic to solve a problem, a catchy song begins to spread through the
region attributing the success to a local nobleman instead of the
characters. It might still describe them, but in lesser roles such as
diviners and advisors to the lord. Other events are confused or even
completely wrong, and all supernatural elements are simply attributed to
either God’s will or the work of the Devil. This is an attempt by one of
the Warders of Mystery to limit the damage he believes the characters
have done to the realm of Magic, as they may learn when they investigate
who is trying to usurp their glory. \|}
