**Sail the Wind** 0 points, Init (Qik – 2), Auram

R: Touch, D: Conc, T: Ind

The character can cause winds powerful enough to lift him off of the
ground and fly him through the air. If more than one character has this
power, it is possible to lift and navigate a small boat, or even a ship
if there are three or more.

Cr(Re)Au 30 (base 5, +1 Touch, +1 Conc, +2 highly unnatural, +1
requisite): Greater Power (30 levels, –3 Might cost, +1 Init)
