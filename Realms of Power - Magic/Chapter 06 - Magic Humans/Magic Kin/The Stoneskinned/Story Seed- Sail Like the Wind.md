At a time when the characters are in a hurry to get somewhere fast, they
might encounter a ship’s captain who offers to ferry them there in a
quarter of the usual time for a very high price. If he has an inkling
that the party includes a magus, he might ask for a magical device, for
example. He is vague about the details, but promises that his ship can
easily make the journey at this speed. He advises the characters to
remain in their cabins for the duration of the trip, but any of them who
peer outside to see the source of the powerful winds they hear will
likely receive a shock as they realize they are soaring through the sky
several leagues above the earth on a ship of the People of the Clouds.
