Drowned Men must take the Major Supernatural Virtue: Transformed (Being)
(see Chapter 4: Characters). Often, Drowned Men develop personality
Flaws associated with the sea (Obsession, Fear, Compulsion, or Vow, for
example), but this is not compulsory. They also usually have the Major
Supernatural Virtue: Greater Immunity (drowning) (see ArM5, page 43) and
the Major Story Flaw: Servant of the Ocean (see Chapter 4: Characters).
