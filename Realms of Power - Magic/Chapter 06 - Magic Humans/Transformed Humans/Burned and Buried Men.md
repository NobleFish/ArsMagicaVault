A person may also be saved from immolation (by a magical creature of
Ignem), or from burial (by a magical creature of Terram), and so acquire
similar Virtues to a Drowned Man. There are fewer recorded incidences of
this occurring — perhaps merely because many more men drown than are
burned or buried alive. Hermetic scholars also speculate that it is
possible to be saved from death by entities that represent other Forms,
although no examples have yet been found.
