All giants must take the Major Quality, Gigantic; giants larger than
Size +2 should take it more than once. The giant must be designed with a
high enough Might score so it has sufficient Qualities to have the
desired Size. (Taking Gigantic multiple times can be costly).

Other common Qualities for giants include Virtues that grant strength or
toughness, improved combat statistics (especially Soak), and,
occasionally, powers related to earth and stone.
