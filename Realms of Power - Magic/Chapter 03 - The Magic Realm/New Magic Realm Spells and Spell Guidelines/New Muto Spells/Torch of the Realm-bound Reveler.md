- MuIg 25 R: Touch, D: Sun, T: Ind

For the duration of the spell, the target may affect fiery objects with
Magic Realm Magic in the mundane world, as if it had Magic Might
associated with Ignem. For example, he could use an Intellego Ignem
effect to see through a flame into the Magic Realm, and pass through it
to a microcosm of that flame. (Base 10, +1 Touch, +2 Sun)
