The word “salamander” comes from Greek. Magi use it as a kind of blanket
term for various beings associated with the Form of Ignem. The term
“Salamander of Virtue” specifically refers to a small lizard with
remarkable powers over fire.

A Salamander of Virtue is very cold by nature and seeks out any source
of heat: fires, or even warm-blooded creatures. When it finds a fire, a
Salamander of Virtue eagerly crawls into it and extinguishes it.

The body of a Salamander of Virtue, if dried by smoking it over embers
of alder wood, is Enriched into a Thing of Virtue. When touched to the
hands, heart, and forehead (a Necessary Condition), it grants Immunity
to Fire for as long as its possessor touches it.
