The covenant has a large population of animals that have been Warped by
a long association with the covenant. A minority of the animals have
unusual and useful powers, but the presence of monsters draws mundane
attention and suspicion to the covenant. If the population of
monstrosities is large enough, the animals may be farmed to provide a
source of Muto vis — this is represented by assigning an appropriate vis
source Boon to the covenant.
