If the Warping points gained by an animal are mostly due to Magic —
rather than Divine, Faerie, or Infernal influences — then it may acquire
the following Virtues and Flaws as it Warps, eventually acquiring a
Lesser Power when it reaches a Warping Score of 5. See Chapter 4:
Characters for details of these Virtues and Flaws.''

- Minor Supernatural Flaw: Warped Senses
- Minor Supernatural Flaw: (Form) Monstrosity
- Minor Supernatural Virtue: Lesser Power
- Major Supernatural Flaw: Hunger for (Form)
- Magic Major Supernatural Flaw: Bound to Magic
