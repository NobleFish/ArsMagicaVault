Three families of cats can trace their ancestry — through generations of
familiars and Warped, intelligent ancestors — to the familiars of the
Founders or their earliest apprentices. Each of these families, called
lineages, has an oral history and a unique culture. They have many
magical features that breed almost true, a feature unusual in magical
animals.
