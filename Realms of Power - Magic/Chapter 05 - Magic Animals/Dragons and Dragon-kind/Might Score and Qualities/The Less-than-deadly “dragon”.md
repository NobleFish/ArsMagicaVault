The characters are waylaid by Varkos the fire drake, who demands all
their treasure. They strike up a conversation with the “dragon” and
Varkos enjoys it so much he forgets to rob them. Later, the characters
learn of a young knight out to build a reputation by slaying the “dragon
of the mountains.” The knight has obtained a magical ointment to protect
himself from flames, so a battle between him and Varkos could be an even
match. Can the characters prevent a deadly fight?
