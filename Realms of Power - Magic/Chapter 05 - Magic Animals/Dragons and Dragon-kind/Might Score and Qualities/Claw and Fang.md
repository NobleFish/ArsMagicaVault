The weapon statistics for a dragon’s claws and teeth as are follows.
Dragons larger than Size +2 usually have large claws and/or large teeth.

|             | Init | Atk | Dfn | Dam |
|-------------|------|-----|-----|-----|
| Claws       | -1   | +2  | +3  | +2  |
| Large Claws | 0    | +5  | +3  | +4  |
| Fangs/Teeth | 0    | +3  | +1e | +1  |
| Large Teeth | 0    | +4  | +1  | +3  |
