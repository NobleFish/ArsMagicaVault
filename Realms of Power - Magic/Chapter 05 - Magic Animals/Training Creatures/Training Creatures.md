With the approval of the storyguide, any magical beast with an animal
intellect — that is, those with Cunning (Cun) — may be tamed, and then
used by another character as a mount, guardian, or companion as
appropriate. Taming an animal is a possible laboratory activity for a
magus, but mundane animal handlers and some magical characters can also
attempt to tame animals. Once an animal has been tamed, it can be
further trained and taught a number of tricks.
