This is an Intellego Mentem base 10 “discover the truth of a statement,”
but as the information comes from William’s mind, the total would also
have to beat his Magic Resistance, if any.

The question “Who stole my book?” would use the same base, but the
Target would vary depending on the number of individuals to be
investigated. A covenant with grogs and covenfolk would have an Ease
Factor of 30 (+1 Touch, +2 Group, +1 Size). A modest medieval city is a
Boundary Target, and has an Ease Factor of 35 (+1 Touch, +4 Boundary).
