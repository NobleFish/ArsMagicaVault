Overlooking the fact that using magic to answer questions about a fellow
member of the Order is a breach of the Code, the question is really, “Is
Thurydices still alive?” Since he is not present, the maga has no idea
where he is, she requires an Arcane Connection to him. An Intellego
Corpus level 4 allows her to “sense general information about a body.”
The Ease Factor is therefore 5 (adding one magnitude for Touching the
Arcane Connection).
