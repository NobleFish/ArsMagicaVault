Physical travelers cast spells as normal, responding to the Forms of
dream as if they had normal Hermetic Forms. (Storyguide consultation may
be required to agree upon Forms for some dream elements.)

Physical travelers can cast spells described as “cast in the real world
to affect dreams” even while in dream, as they are still real-world
people. This can make them much more powerful than spiritual travelers.

The aura of dream locations is determined as for spiritual travelers,
according to the dream-world environment.
