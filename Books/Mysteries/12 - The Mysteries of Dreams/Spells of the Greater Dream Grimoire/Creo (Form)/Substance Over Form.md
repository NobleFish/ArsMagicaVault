Cr(Mu)Te(Me) 20

R: Touch, D: Mom, T: Ind, Mystery Ritual

This spell can only be cast in a dream by a physical traveler and the
caster must use real-world vis for the ritual. The target object may
appear unchanged for now, but it has a reality that extends beyond
dream, so that if a physical traveler leaves a dream bearing the object,
it travels with him and appears in the real world. If the spell is cast
without real-world vis, the object vanishes as it is brought into the
real world.

*(Base 15, +1 Touch)*
