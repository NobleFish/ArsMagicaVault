InVi 30

R: Arcane, D: Mom, T: Ind

Reports all aspects of the astrological conditions (minute, hour, day,
sign, planets) at a point that the caster can see, or to which he has an
Arcane Connection. Identical conditions extend for some 500 paces away
from this point (100 standard Boundaries).

*(Base 10, +4 Arcane)*
