InVi 10

R: Touch, D: Mom, T: Ind

Measures the astrological time in the area around the magus. Knowing the
hour exactly subtracts 3 from the Ease Factor of the Astronomy roll for
an Astrological Duration spell.

Measuring the conditions at a single point is sufficient, as identical
conditions (suitable for Astrological Durations) extend for some 500
paces away from the point (standard Boundary area x 100). Increasing the
size of the spell Target serves no purpose, as the measurement area is
limited by changes in the astrological conditions, rather than by the
magus’s spell.

*(Base 5, +1 Touch)*
