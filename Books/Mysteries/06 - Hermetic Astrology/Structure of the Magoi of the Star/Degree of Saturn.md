This is the first degree of Initiation, and members traditionally wear
black gowns, emblazoned with stars and moon symbols for rites. To be
Initiated requires a knowledge of Artes Liberales at 4 or greater,
reflecting a sound knowledge of astronomy and the mathematical ability
to construct astrological charts. The Initiate is taught how to overcome
planetary influences, and how to use them to his advantage. The Virtue
Initiated is Planetary Magic.
