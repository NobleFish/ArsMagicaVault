The signs of the Zodiac fall into several categories, some opposing
others. Signs are accorded correspondence with the four elements (fire,
water, air, earth), four humors (choleric, phlegmatic, sanguine,
melancholic — corresponding to the four elements), three temperaments
(cardinal, fixed, mutable), and two genders.
