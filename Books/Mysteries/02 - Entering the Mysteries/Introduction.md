# Entering the Mysteries

*Now let us enter within the ancient crypt, where the mysteries of the
maggots await. You have come so far blindfolded, stripped of power,
stripped of prestige, and stripped of dignity, clothed only in your
shroud. Within await the Great Secrets, secrets even death could not
hold! Yet I must ask you — are you worthy to behold them?* *— Diana of
Ex Miscellanea, a Mystagogue of the Disciples* *of the Worm*

*This chapter describes how one is Initiated into a Mystery, how a
Mystery Cult is born, what the benefits are to its members, and the
bitter truth that power always has a price.*

*The price of power can take many forms. The time a magus spends
pursuing the mystical rites of his cult, learning its secrets, and
preparing for Ordeals, Sacrifices, and Quests is the most obvious price.
An often overlooked but no less real cost of membership, however, are
the adventures and stories required to progress in the paths of the
Mysteries.*
