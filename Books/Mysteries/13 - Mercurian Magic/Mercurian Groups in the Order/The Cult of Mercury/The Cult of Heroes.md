As described in Houses of Hermes: True Lineages, the Cult of Heroes is a
small non-Mystery group comprised of worshipers in the Cult of Mercury.
They believe the great heroes of antiquity still walk the Earth.
Heracles, Arthur, Beowulf, Gilgamesh — they say these legendary figures
and others like them were descended from the gods, and the cult members
believe that they can be born again. The Children of Hermes (see Chapter
14: Mystery Cults) is another group of mystery cultists who subscribe to
this idea and believe that their leader is actually Hermes, the Greek
form of the god Mercury, reborn. \|}
