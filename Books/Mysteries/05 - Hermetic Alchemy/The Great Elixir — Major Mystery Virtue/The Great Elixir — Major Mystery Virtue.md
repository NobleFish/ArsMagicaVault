The ultimate goal — the Elixir of Life — achieved so far by very few
alchemists is to transform themselves into beings of magic, truly
immortal, although at the cost of the loss of their humanity. This
Virtue teaches a method that is itself a form of Mystery Ordeal,
creating and consuming a Great Elixir potion.

The magus must have both the Virtues Unaging and the Lesser Elixir.
