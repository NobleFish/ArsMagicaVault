The magus who succeeds in creating the Greater Elixir ceases to be
human, and becomes an immortal, alchemical being of the Magic realm,
with a Might score determined by the Great Elixir potion. He may be
slain, but he will not die of lesser wounds, nor age or Warping.

The transformation is complete and irrevocable, and cannot be dispelled;
for example, by trying to dispel the Great Elixir focus — indeed, the
focus no longer exists, having been consumed and digested by the magus.

See Appendix A: Immortal Magi for description and rules for immortals
and immortal magi in particular.
