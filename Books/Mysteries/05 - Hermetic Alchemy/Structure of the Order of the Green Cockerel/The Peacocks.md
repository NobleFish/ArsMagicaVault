This fourth degree represents the process of conjunction, as the now
purged and spiritually purified Initiate is rejoined to the Green
Cockerel , welcome once more amidst the alchemical community. The planet
Venus governs this degree. The metal copper is associated with the
Peacocks, and the Initiatess robes bear the splendor of all the colors
of the rainbow. They are now expected to serve as active members for the
cult, and learn some of the secrets of the Great Work. They are
Initiated in the Virtue Hermetic Alchemy.
