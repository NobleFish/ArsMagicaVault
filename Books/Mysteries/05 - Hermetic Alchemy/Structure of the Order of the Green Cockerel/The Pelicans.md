The sixth degree is the process of distillation. It is symbolically
associated with the Moon, the metal silver, and the color red. Now one
learns the true secret of distillation as represented by Initiation into
the Virtue Philosophic Alchemy, and takes on the role of Mystagogue and
earthly representative of the Order of the Green Cockerel, acting as
intermediary between the true spiritual master who has completed the
Great Work and the alchemical brethren, mundane and esoteric.
