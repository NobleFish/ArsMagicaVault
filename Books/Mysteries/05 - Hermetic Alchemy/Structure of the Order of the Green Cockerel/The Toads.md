The first degree represents the first process: calcination, associated
with Saturn, the color black, and the metal lead. The unrefined
probationer is exposed to the heat of the Initiation, and learns of the
invisible sun, and the influence of the planets. Regarded with awe by
mundane alchemists they meet, owing to their great knowledge, Toads are
the lowest members of this secret society, their role largely that of
lab assistants, as yet unversed in the mysteries. Quests for rare
ingredients or alchemical literature are common at this degree. The
Initiate learns the Virtue of Planetary Magic.
