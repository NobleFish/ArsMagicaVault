The magus becomes a pure, perfected, unchanging immortal, divorced and
cut off from the mundane world. The new spirit may be seen in the
heavens as a nova stella, or new star. When he first ascends, he shines
brightly, to announce his presence to the world, but over time his
brilliance may fade to reflect his relative status among the other
heroes. It is said that the greatest heroes and gods from the time of
myths ascended to form entire constellations, but no new constellations
have arisen in known time.

Such ascended spirits form some of the heroes in the Daimonic bestiary:
It is now possible to summon the magus.

The magus’s talisman, if he has one, becomes a part of his immortal
being, a source of power from which he can invoke effects — just as he
could in life. (This is true whether the talisman is a normal object, or
his spirit or body — see Chapter 8: Hermetic Spirit Magic, Inscription
on the Soul and Spirit vs. Body Enchantments.)

The magus’s mortal body is left behind as a useless empty shell. Any
enchantments still on the body are dispelled and any remnant vis is
consumed by the ascending spirit. The body remains an Arcane Connection
to the Daimon spirit. Most cultists take some small portion, such as a
bone, as a relic to use when summoning their Daimonic leader; but they
often destroy the larger part of the body lest it fall into enemy hands.
