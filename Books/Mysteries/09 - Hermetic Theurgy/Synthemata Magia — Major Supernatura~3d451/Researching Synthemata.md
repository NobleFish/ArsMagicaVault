A magus may also try to research the synthemata of a being he has
previously encountered in person. He can do so even if he failed to
intuit its synthemata.

By fasting and invocation, over the course of an entire season, the
magus attempt to research the synthemata. He learns the synthemata on a
success in a roll of Perception + (Realm) Lore + aura modifier against
an Ease Factor of the being’s Might + name hiding (no aura modifier):

If the magus has a sympathetic or Arcane Connection to the being, he may
use this in a similar manner to those with Hermetic synthemata:
calculate the “multiplier” as in ArM5, page 84, and add the score as a
bonus to the synthemata research roll.

Failing this roll does not prevent future attempts. Botching the roll
may still attract the attention of the being, but hopefully the
covenant’s Aegis of the Hearth can ward out the offended spirit.
