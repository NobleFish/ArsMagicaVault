This Virtue lets the magus ally himself with a ghost or other
incorporeal spirit as a spirit familiar, using rules similar to those
for binding an animal familiar. A magus may only have one familiar, be
it animal or spirit — he cannot ally with a spirit if he has a familiar
already. If he had, but lost, a familiar, he can later ally with a
spirit as his spirit familiar.

Spirit familiar is also known as genius umbrae (shadowy guardian spirit)
or (by those with knowledge of ancient traditions) the Greek name
parhedros.

The Lab Total for binding a spirit familiar is the same as an animal
familiar: any appropriate Technique and any appropriate Form. As with
animal familiars, a Technique or Form is appropriate if it corresponds
with the spirit desired as spirit familiar. The Technique of a spirit
matches the Hermetic Technique of their best powers. The Form of spirits
is usually obvious, as most spirits have powers with effects similar to
Hermetic Forms: almost any spirit corresponds to Vim; ghosts may
correspond to Mentem or Imaginem, or even Corpus; elementals correspond
to their elemental Form; plant and animal spirits are often tied to
living bodies, but there are some that are incorporeal, perhaps
inhabiting a sacred grove.
