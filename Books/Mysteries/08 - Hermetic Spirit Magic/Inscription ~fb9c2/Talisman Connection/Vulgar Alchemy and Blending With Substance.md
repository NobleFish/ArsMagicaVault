Vulgar Alchemy (see Chapter 5: Hermetic Alchemy, Vulgar Alchemy) allows
the magus to discover new Shape and Material items. However when first
discovered, they only apply to Vulgar Alchemy enchantments of charged
and lesser enchanted devices. To use such an item in Blending With
Substance requires the magus to experiment further until he has
incorporated the new item fully into Hermetic theory. (That is, when he
is able to write a tractatus about the bonus item, according to the
Vulgar Alchemy rules.)
