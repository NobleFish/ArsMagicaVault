Certain places under the influence of the Church have been noted in the
Alpine Peripheral Code as sites where even minor violence will be
adjudged interference. The most important of these are the city of
Constance and the monasteries in the Passes of Saint Bernard, each vital
to the redcaps and favored to some degree by House Jerbiton. Some magi,
particularly members of House Criamon, prefer to rest in chapters during
journeys, so that they do not accidentally alarm churchmen with their
appearance, Blatant Gift or unusual beliefs.
