Each tribunal has developed a pattern of judgments that are not followed
in the other tribunals. This pattern of judgments, along with social
pressures and historic events, create traditions which make each
tribunal's culture unique. The Alpine Hermetic culture deliberately
holds itself aloof from the general Hermetic culture, so it has
developed many unique features.
