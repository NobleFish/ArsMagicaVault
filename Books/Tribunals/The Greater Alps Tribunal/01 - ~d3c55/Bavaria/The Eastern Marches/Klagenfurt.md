Refounded in 1161, this Carinthian town was the site of one of
Herakles's lesspublicized labors. The villagers were unable to drain the
marshes nearby because of the lindworm, a dragon with a taste for the
flesh of virgins. Fortunately Herakles wandered through the area and
dispatched the creature, thereby making all the virgins in the vicinity
quite grateful. The locals keep the skull of the lindworm to this day.
