The territory of Burgenland is divided between two tribunals, with its
northern plains part of Transylvania. The southern and central area is a
thin strip of wooded hills that lie at the very edge of the Alps.
Dividing the land types, the Burgenland is also traditionally the border
between the lifestyle of civilized Europe and the plains of Hungary. The
territory is named "land of castles" because its central and southern
areas bristle with fortifications. Each successive wave of Eastern
invaders, once they settle down to Alpine agriculture, puts more bricks
on the walls of Burgenland.

The settlement of Eisenstadt effectively marks the Tribunal border. The
redcaps use an exchange point west of Eisenstadt, a small regio that
contains a marble statue of the Trivia, the threefold goddess of the
roads.
