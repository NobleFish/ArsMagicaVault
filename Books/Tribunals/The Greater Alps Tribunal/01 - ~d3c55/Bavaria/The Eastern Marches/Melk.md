The abbey at Melk was originally a castle of the Babenbergs, but was
passed to the Benedictines a century and a half ago. The monks here are
great collectors and cataloguers of knowledge, and their library is
excellent. The abbey reports directly to the Pope, without intermediary
bishops, and persistent rumor links it to a special school for Hermetic
magi who take monastic and military vows, dedicating their lives to the
war against demons.
