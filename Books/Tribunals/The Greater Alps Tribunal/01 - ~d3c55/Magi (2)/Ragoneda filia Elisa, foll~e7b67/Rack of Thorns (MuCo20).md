A white rose +2

The ribcage of the target warps and develops thorns of bone along its
inner surface. These puncture the lungs on the next inhalation, and then
disappear, leaving the target with pincushion injuries throughout the
lungs. Fatal internal hemorrhage usually follows. A character who,
forewarned, holds their breath needs only make a Stamina roll of 6+ to
not gasp in pain when the thorns are created. Those without forewarning
who wish to hold their breaths need to make a Stamina roll of 9+ or tear
their lungs.

(Design: Change a limb +10, increased range +5, reduced duration –10.
Doing something inside the body +15, as per "Lungs of water and death".)
