A piece of hard candy (+1)

The Nagging Curse cracks the enamel of one of the target's teeth, so
that they suffer distracting pain. This is represented by a lost fatigue
level, which cannot be recovered without magical healing or the
extraction of the tooth. Only one fatigue level can be lost due to this
spell.

(Cause pain +10, increased range +10)
