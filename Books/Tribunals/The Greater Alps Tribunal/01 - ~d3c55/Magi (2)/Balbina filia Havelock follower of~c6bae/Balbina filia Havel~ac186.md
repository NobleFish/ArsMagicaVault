Balbina's name means "stammerer" which accurately describes her
affliction, a curse that prevents her speaking correctly while casting
spells. Trained in House Verditius, she is more traditional than most,
because even with casting tools she has difficulty formulating spells,
and finds her stammer embarrassing. Balbina is in charge of maintaining
the odd devices her housemates have constructed here over the years,
most significantly the overwheel that the covenfolk use to provide power
for grinding, pumping water, circulating air and winching.
