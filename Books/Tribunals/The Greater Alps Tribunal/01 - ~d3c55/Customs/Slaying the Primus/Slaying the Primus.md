Primacy is an onerous duty. The selectee agrees to delay their ascension
into Twilight, to help others comprehend the Enigma. As each primus
nears Twilight, the Criamon gather, and then move in procession, through
the labyrinthine halls of Twisting Shadows, to the eighth level of the
regio. After a brief ceremony, which summons the ghosts of the previous
primi, a magus from another House ritually murders the current primus.
Why they ask an outsider to perform this function is unclear, but the
Criamon consider the offer of this role a mark of deep respect.
