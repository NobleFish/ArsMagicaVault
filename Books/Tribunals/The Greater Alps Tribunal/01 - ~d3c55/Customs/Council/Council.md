This covenant isn't ruled by votes, but by consensus amongst its
members. In the forming of consensus, the thoughts of the wisest magi
hold the greatest weight. The covenant, in practice, is ruled through
the charisma of its senior members. Sometimes, magi who have come to The
Cave seeking Enigmatic Wisdom are asked to participate in Council.
