As the center of House Criamon, the customs of the Cave of Twisting
Shadows are elaborate and bizarre. Since they occasionally request the
assistance of a magus from another house, their rituals are well
documented, if ill understood, by the other Houses. This may be
deliberate, since the frequency of requests for observers increased
markedly slightly before the Schism War. Books about Enigmatic Wisdom
and House Criamon Lore also proliferated at that time.
