Although the Cave is the center of Criamon cosmology, the House sends
its youngest members away from it. They hope these young, vigorous,
open-minded magi will be able to bring new insights back to the House,
when they periodically return for new riddles. Emigration also protects
magi from complex mystical experiences that they are unable to integrate
into their world-view without greater life experience.
