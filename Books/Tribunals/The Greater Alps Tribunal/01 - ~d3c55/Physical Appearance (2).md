The covenant lies in a mostly-artificial cave system behind a minor
waterfall in the Bavarian Alps. Certain skilled vivisectionists claim
that the covenant's floor plan is disturbingly similar to the network of
blood vessels that splay about the human liver, but the current
occupants claim this design predates Hermetic habitation. A recurring
stellar motif is found on the ceiling of the covenant complex's tunnels.
The constellation might be Ursa Major, but if it is, then some of the
stars are misplaced. Again, the Hermetic inhabitants have no idea what
the meaning or function of this pattern originally was, although they
have enchanted it to provide illumination.
