In *The Mysteries*, options were given for including mystery cults in
Ars Magica. This book assumes a low mystery setting. For high mystery
settings you should increase the number and size of the covenants in the
Alps. The Mysteries also presented secretive organizations, which
characters could join, that taught new styles of magic. The magi
presented here are suitable for a low-mystery saga. In a high-mystery
saga, the statistics given here reflect only their public façades.
Although the book is low-mystery, hints of secret groups that could
flower into mystae are scattered throughout the text.
