#### About the Author

Timothy Ferguson lives in Biloela, Australia. During the completion of
this book he earned three degrees and lived in three different cities.
It's traditional for librarians in Australia to spend a couple of years
banished to obscure hamlets in the Outback, where there is so little to
do that he might write another book, or finish another degree.
