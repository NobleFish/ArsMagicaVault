Constance is the main city on Lake Constance and regulates trade from
Bavaria into the Alps. Its merchants and bishop, who is the ruler here,
are extremely wealthy. Some commentators claim that Constance is the
richest bishopric north of the Alps, but it is hard to be certain if
this is true. What is clear is that when the bishop is of decisive
character he plays a significant role in both secular and ecclesiastic
politics.
