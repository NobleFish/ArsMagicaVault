This town lies southwest of Bern. Berhtold IV, a Zähringer, founded it
in 1157. The House of Savoy continues the frenetic castle building in
the area around it, erecting new fortifications at Gruyeres,
Estervayer-le-Lac, and Morat during the thirteenth century.

Local legend says that if you stay up the night of Saint John's Eve you
can catch the seeds of ferns. These are fiery, luminescent and golden.
Peasants say servants of the devil are forced to give you a bag of money
in exchange for them. Similar myths are found elsewhere in the Alps,
linking fern seeds to wealth. Hermetic magi believe fern seeds contain
Ignem vis, and call them "the blood of the Sun".
