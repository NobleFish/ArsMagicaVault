The main settlement of this area is the town of Chur, once a Roman
colony. It has had a bishop for eight hundred years, but its new
cathedral was finished in about 1150.

Much of the territory of Rhaetia lives in a state of *faustrecht*, "club
law," where rights go to the strongest. The area is scattered with
castles, filled with nobles whose territories are insignificant. The
owner of each claims sovereignty and rights over nearby peasants,
terrorizing sustenance out of them. Many settlements in Rhaetia have
independent entries.
