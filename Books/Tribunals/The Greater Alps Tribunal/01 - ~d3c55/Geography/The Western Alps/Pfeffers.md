This is a Benedictine monastery founded in 713, near which are a series
of hot springs. Since these springs are recessed in a crevice, patients
are lowered into them using long ropes. Since lifting the patients out
at the end of each day is so burdensome, it is traditional to leave
patients in the heated wells for a week at a time, lowering food to them
occasionally.
