A society dedicated to the cultivation of the grape, called the Abbaye
des Vignerons, is centered on Vervay. Every fifteen to twenty years the
society sponsors a festival to celebrate viticulture. The finest
grape-grower is crowned and presides over a procession and festival, and
the most beautiful girl in the village is given a large dowry and is
allowed to select the husband of her choice. The society acts like a
guild, rewarding skilled growers with medals and pruning shears. Bronze
tools were a traditional sacrifice to the goddess of the Rhaetians, so
this festival may be very ancient.
