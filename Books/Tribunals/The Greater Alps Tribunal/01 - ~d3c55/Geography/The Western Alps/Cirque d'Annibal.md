Close to the entrance of the Alps from the Burgundinian side through the
little pass of Saint Bernard, is a field in which stands a vast circle
of ten, ill formed monoliths. Hannibal waited for stragglers from his
army here, and held a council of war before marching on into Italy.
Members of the Order say this site is ill-omened, filled perhaps with
the spirits of those who died on the march from Carthage. As the
descendants of the Romans, they believe, their presence angers the
fallen. The more melodramatic myths say the shadows here fuse into a
demonic shape, part bronze, part man, but mostly elephant.
