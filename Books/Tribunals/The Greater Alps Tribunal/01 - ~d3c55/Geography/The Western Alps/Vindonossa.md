Between Baden and Basel lie these ruins of a Roman fortress that
defended Helvetia from the German hordes across the Rhine. Within its
fallen walls is the castle of Hapsburg, from which that noble family
takes its name. Bishop Werner of Straussburg, ancestor of the Hapsburgs,
constructed it in the 11th century. A scion of the family, although not
the most senior, lives here, and his ancestors rest on the family
ground.
