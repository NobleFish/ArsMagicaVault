One regio that is still accessible belongs to a dwarf king, Laurens, who
rules a land of roses. Laurens made war on his neighbors to kidnap a

Following lengthy detention, and a daring escape, Laurens traveled home
to the Catinaccio. He noted that the roses marked the entrance to his
realm, and made him vulnerable to his enemies. Laurens cursed the roses,
commanding them to hide by night or day, giving them the appearance of
stone. At dusk, however, when the sun lies low across the mountains of
