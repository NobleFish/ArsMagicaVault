There is a ruined Temple of Mercury near Mount Pilatus. It is of intense
interest to Hermetic historians, who try to link it to the three
Founders whose *domus magnae* adjoin the Alps. Thus far their
cogitations are inconclusive. The temple was used as the meeting place
for tribunals before the Midsummer Fair became popular. It remains
hidden by magic items that generate illusions, but is not currently
inhabited by magi, or used regularly. It would be an excellent site for
a chapter house.
