A fortified town near Geneva, Lausanne was settled by the survivors of
Alemmanian attacks on the Roman-settled town of Lousonna. In either
locale their function was to ensure Roman possession of the approaches
to Saint Bernard's Pass. Its inhabitants are in the process of
rebuilding their cathedral, slowed by the enormous demand for laborers
and masons bought about by the castle building mania.
