These are two large, flattish rocks that emerge from Lake Léman,
slightly south east of the city of Geneva. Hermetic myth says that the
members of House Díedne used to perform human sacrifices here, but that
may be a distorted folk memory of the Romans who may have sacrificed to
Neptune upon them. Gargantua, a giant, threw the largest of these stones
into the lake. He is credited with digging Lac Léman and the creation of
many of the hills and troughs around Geneva.
