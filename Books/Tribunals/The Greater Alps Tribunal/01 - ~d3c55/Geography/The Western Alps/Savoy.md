Those sections of the tribunal that lay in the Kingdom of Burgundy are
in the territory of the house of Savoy. The Covenant Where Journeys End,
an ancient covenant whose magi are predominantly members of House
Merinita, claims many of the vis sources in the area, but most of the
other Alpine covenants have at least one chapter house here, primarily
to gather vis. This region has been ill-omened for the Order, as
Hermetic settlements here were destroyed during the Schism War and the
purge of House Tytalus. Journeys End was severely damaged during the
civil war within House Merinita, and some predict that the next crisis
that shakes the Order will be presaged by the destruction of a Hermetic
settlement in this area. Hermetic magi of a less mystical bent point out
that there was no warning from this area about the corruption of House
Tremere, or the rise of the Shadow Flambeau.

Savoy attracts Seekers interested in the various ruined Hermetic
settlements, and the relatively stable chapters here are often the
proving grounds for magi of middle age, or retirement quarters for
ancient or addled magi. Savoy, and its ruler, are described on pages
24–27 of *A Medieval Tapestry*.
