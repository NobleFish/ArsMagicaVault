This is the site of the battle, which, in A.D. 69, ended Helvetian
independence from Rome. This site is not generally haunted, but the
remains here can be used to call up the ghosts of fallen soldiers. Many
ghosts have reported that religious figures, who sound like primitive
magi, were killed in the battle, and magi occasionally excavate in this
area, seeking the skulls of these ancient wizards. Extended stays,
especially sleeping on-site, stirs the spirits of the field to recreate
their battle, with any Hermetic magi taking the role of the fallen Roman
priests. A magus who survived the spirit battle would know where the
skull he seeks was buried, but the groups of magi who have attempted
this have all died, and been dumped on the edges of the field. The
ghosts, apparently, don't want the newly dead magi haunting them.
