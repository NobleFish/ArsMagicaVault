Kalliope, a magus of middle years, is the latest successor to an
intermittent line of librarian-magi active at Valnastium. Similar lines
exist in a few other covenants and they form a small, interacting
community. Most covenants leave librarianship to non-magicians, and even
in Valnastium, Durenmar and Verdi, which most often have
magicianlibrarians, menial, training and research tasks are left to
mundane assistants or apprentices.
