This covenant has 22 members. Most Criamon return here periodically, as
part of their riddle quests, or to join the Central Clutch. Eight of
these members live in the mundane area surrounding the Cave, members
purely in a legal sense. Criamon magi given here fit the House's
archetype only loosely. This is to provide variety, allowing storyguides
to remove those they dislike.
