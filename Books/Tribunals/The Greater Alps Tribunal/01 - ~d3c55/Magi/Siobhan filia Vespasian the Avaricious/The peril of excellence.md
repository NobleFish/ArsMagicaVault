Each Criamon magus decides whether to pursue excellence in his or her
field of study. Some, like La, believe they will never personally find
the solution to the Enigma, so they throw themselves deeply into their
field of specialization, searching for clues they can pass on. Others
seek personal enlightenment. These magi find excellence in any field
suspicious, because the field of study they pursue can become a
distraction from the greater goal. They illustrate this with the story
of a nobleman who learned how to play chess to impress his king, but
spent so long considering board positions that he was distracted from
the intrigue of the court, and so retired to a monastery after rivals
took his lands. The Central Clutch members tend toward those who embrace
excellence, which is seen as a sacrifice.
