The covenant is not visible from the mundane world. A gateway exists,
but is hidden by illusions and an icy lake. The regio which has an aura
of 6 — replicates the topography of the mundane world. Long, low houses
of blue-white stone line terraces that work down toward vineyards that
border the snowline. Further down the slopes are orchards, then cropped
fields. To those with magical sight, virtually everything appears to be
warped by the covenant's aura, the occasional human an exception. The
covenant has no obvious central structure, because the magi's
laboratories are burrowed deep into the core of the mountain.
