Since most Alpine apprentices are welcomed into Hermetic society in
March, February is the month in which they are tested. See *Houses of
Hermes* for details of apprentices' gauntlets. Alpine archmages usually
declare which challenger they will receive for the year in this month.
The two sets of events became tangled together because of a pun in the
tenth century, which has been sanctified by time.
