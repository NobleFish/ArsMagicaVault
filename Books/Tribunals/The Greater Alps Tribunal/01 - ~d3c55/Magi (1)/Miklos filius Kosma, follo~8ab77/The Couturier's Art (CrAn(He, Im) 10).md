R: Touch D: Sun/Instant T: Individual.

Spell Focus: Dressmaker's pins +1

This spell creates an entire set of clothes, complete with accessories.
Since it cannot create metal, it mocks up metal pieces using animal
bones covered with reflective colors.

(Design: Creates leather/wool +10, uses requisites to add colors and use
plant based fibers.)
