R: Touch D: Sun/Instant T: Individual.

Spell Focus: A silver needle +1

This spell magically repairs an item of clothing. It can't replace metal
pieces, but can simulate them using metallic dyes.

(Design: Creates leather/wool +10, uses requisites to add colors and use
plantbased fibers.)
