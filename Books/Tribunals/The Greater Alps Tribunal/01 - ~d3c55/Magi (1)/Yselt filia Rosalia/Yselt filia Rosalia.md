An ice-distorted, faerie-blooded maga, Yselt rarely leaves the Covenant.
Leader of both the Council and its community, she is a sort of stern
aunt to her followers. Yselt's sigil is the icicle. Her distortion is so
intense that some of her sodales worry she might be transformed into a
faerie by her Final Twilight, assuming the role of the absent Queen.
Since this might enhance the distortion of the covenfolk, twisting them
into the shapes of the Queen's slain servants, Yselt might eventually
need to be exiled.
