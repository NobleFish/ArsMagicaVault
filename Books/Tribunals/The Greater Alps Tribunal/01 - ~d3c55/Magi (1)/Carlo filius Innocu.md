Carlo was born of parents both distorted by the Aura of Ice, and like
many others so bred, he has the features of the barbegazi, the gnomes
that served the Queen before Hermetic magi seized the regio. Other magi
have difficulty determining where his faerie powers begin and his
Hermetic magic ends. Many believe that he makes hand gestures when using
faerie powers as a private jest.

Carlo's sigil is icicles forming in his beard, a feature that gives this
class of gnome its name. Like other barbegazi, Carlo has huge feet, with
which he can tunnel through snow, or upon which he can slide rapidly
across ice. He can survive being buried in snow, can travel by tunneling
through it, and can summon blizzards to bury himself and his enemies.
Other magi are unsure whether these powers are Hermetic or faerie in
origin. Barbegazi are described in more detail on page 105.
