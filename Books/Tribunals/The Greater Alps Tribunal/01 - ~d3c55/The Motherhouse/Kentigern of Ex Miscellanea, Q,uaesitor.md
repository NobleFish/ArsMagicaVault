A member of a beast-mage tradition from Hibernia, Kentigern's magical
affinity is for dogs. He controls a pack of faerie hounds that prowl
about the Midsummer Fair, growling at people that they think smell
suspicious. A skilled tracker, he hunts magical animals through the
blizzard, and air elementals through the sky. When a quarry is too
powerful for him, he invites magi from the nearest covenant to assist.
