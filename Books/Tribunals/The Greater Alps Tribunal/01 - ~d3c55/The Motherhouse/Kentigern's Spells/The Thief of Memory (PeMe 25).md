R: Eye/Sight D: Sun/Instant T: Individual

Spell Focus: Burning a forget-me-not (+3)

This spell removes all memory of a single individual, location or event
from a person's mind. The target will often be aware that they cannot
remember the individual or event. For example, if made to forget being
robbed, they will still know that their money is missing, and will be
aware that they do not recall where it has gone. This spell was
originally created to suppress the manifesting Gifts of untrained
children, or to remove their memories of home. The Quaesitores use it to
tidy up after Hermetic criminals.
