*Call the Basilisk (CrAn(Re) 50)*

R: Touch; D: Sun/Perm; T: Ind.

Spell Focus: A toad +1 or being in the parts of the Alps where basilisks
are unnaturally common +1

This spell creates a basilisk (see the *Medieval Bestiary* Revised
Edition, page 95). These dangerous beasts are relatively common in the
Alps, especially near Basel and Vienna. Kentigern developed this spell
so he could study them safely. Given that they only hatch from an egg
laid in a dung heap he thinks that perhaps a magus is making them, and
wants to be prepared to deal with the situation. The prescription for
making basilisks is simpler in the Alps than elsewhere: in other parts
of Europe they say that the egg needs to be laid by a rooster and sat on
by a toad while a certain star is in the sky.

(Design: Creates a small magical animal 35, Might 50 = minimum level)
