Chosen for her capacity for stealth and defensive violence, Adrasteia
roves the borders of the Tribunal seeking wrongdoers to smite with the
iron fist of justice. She spends most of her recreational time tracking
raiders who cross the Tribunal borders to plunder the vis sites
recognized as Alpine in the Peripheral Code. Since she's only allowed to
batter wrongdoers caught in the act, she lays elaborate traps and
constructs detailed ruses that tempt the greedy to err. She always takes
at least two hoplites with her, so her ambushes are violent and
effective.

Although her techniques wouldn't be accepted elsewhere, she's yet to
receive a formal rebuke. The powerful covenants in the Alps wish there
were more hoplites wandering about smiting uppity Rhinelanders. The
Primus of Guernicus is aware that Adrasteia may become a problem, but is
hesitant to withdraw her to Magvillus for retraining. He sees her as a
stabilizing influence: she dissuades Alpine magi from taking up
vigilantism or counter-raiding into the Rhineland, as Adrasteia sees
counter-raiders as equally legitimate targets.
