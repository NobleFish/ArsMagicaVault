Larta magi are not merely exceptional artists, but phenomenal virtuosi,
unique talents of the age. In the various disciplines from which they
come, each is the ultimate practitioner of their generation, or seems
likely to become so. It's this hint of power that prevents true rancor
from developing over the adoption of Larta magi into House Jerbiton.
Further, the Verditius, those Bonisagus who want to study their
techniques, and those Merinitans who like their work support them
politically.
