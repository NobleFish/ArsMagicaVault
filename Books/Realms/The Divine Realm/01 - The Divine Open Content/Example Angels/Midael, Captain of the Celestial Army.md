**Appearance:** Midael appears on earth in the guise of a knight or
warrior. His angelic nature is clear from his luminous white skin and
his shining eyes.

Midael is one of the captains of the celestial host, lead by Michael. He
is the angel invoked by David in Psalms 34 and 35, and is the defender
of those persecuted by evil men, often appearing as a knight or great
warrior to protect the innocent or righteous from dishonorable foes or
wicked assailants. He has great martial prowess, but his is also a canny
warrior and has defeated many opponents with greater skill or power than
he by wit and cleverness.
