Benjamin of Tudela wrote the *Sefer ha-Masa'ot* (*The Book of Travels*)
to chronicle his journeys from Navarre to Jerusalem. Anyone studying
this book may gain the specialty "Jewish communities" in this Area Lore,
which helps to locate Jewish communities across the Mediterranean and
the Levant. Written in Hebrew, copies can be found in larger Jewish
communities.
