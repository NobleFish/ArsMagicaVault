Tractatus Dream Interpretation (Quality 8), Tractatus Judaic Lore
(Quality 8), Tractatus Rabbinic Law (Quality 8)

**Author**: Compiled from various sources by the 2nd century AD.

**Availability**: Any synagogue or yeshivah.

**Description**: The *Ketuvim*, or *Writings*, is a disparate work of
wisdom, poetry, prophecies, and history and as such is treated as a
collection of tractatus.

The *Ketuvim* is the final section of the collected *Tanakh*, or Hebrew
Bible.
