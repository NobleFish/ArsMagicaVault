Summa Infernal Lore (Level 5, Quality 8)

**Author**: Anonymous Jewish magicians. **Availability**: The private
libraries of *Ba'al Shem*, Kabbalists, and the *Ba'al Ov*.

**Description**: A book of theurgistic magic that purports to list the
names of angels, demons, and other spirits. This is an unusual work in
that it eschews the expected ritual purity for witchcraft-like
practices.

**Special Rule**: Studying the *Sword of Moses* for a season can be
treated as a source of insight for researching and integrating the
extinct magical form of Canaanite Necromancy (*Ancient Magic*, page 30).
