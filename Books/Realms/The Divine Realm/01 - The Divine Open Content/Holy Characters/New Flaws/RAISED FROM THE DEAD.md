*Major, Story, Supernatural*

You died, and were brought back to life through a holy miracle. You
begin with at least three Warping points, plus one Warping point for
every year that has passed since you were resurrected, and you
automatically receive another Warping point every year you continue
living. You also have a level 4 reputation in the area where the miracle
occurred. You do not remember what happened to you while you were dead,
although you may have virtuous impulses that you cannot explain.
