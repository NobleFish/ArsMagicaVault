*Minor, Supernatural*

You do not belong to a holy tradition, as your practices and beliefs are
too unusual to be classified by an overarching philosophy. You may still
learn Supernatural Abilities from other holy characters who are willing
to teach you, though always with a penalty to your Advancement Total —
you do not have any favored Abilities. You must have True Faith to take
this Flaw.
