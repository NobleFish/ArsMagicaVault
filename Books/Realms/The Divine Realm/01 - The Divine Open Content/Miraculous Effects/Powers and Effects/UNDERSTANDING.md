Characters with Understanding can learn hidden information, often about
the future or the Divine Will. This information will often be difficult
to understand, include additional misleading information, and will not
always take into account sinful or mundane possibilities. This is
because while this Power gives unique insight into God's Will and the
future, it is impossible for the mortal mind to comprehend things the
way that God sees them. The more you explore a subject with your holy
Understanding, the less focused the information will become; and
revisiting the same questions will bring more confusion than wisdom.
God's ways are not ours, and we cannot ever fully grasp His designs.
Thus, reckless glimpses into His plan can break and even destroy the
fragile human mind.

**Meditation** is the only Method that allows this sort of prophetic
knowledge. Mistakes that lead to tragedies of hubris with Understanding
usually involve false, harmful or even abusive information. You might
perceive events that never happened, revisit scarring and painful
memories, or learn dangerous secrets that you did not wish to know.
