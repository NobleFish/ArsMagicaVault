# Chapter One: Introduction

Welcome to *The Divine*, the first of the *Realms of Power* books for
**Ars Magica**™, dealing with the Divine realm and its earthly
institutions and followers.

The Divine encompasses the highest and most sublime powers in Mythic
Europe. It gives strength to the three great faiths of Christianity,
Islam, and Judaism, and is the source of Mythic Europe's ubiquitous
Dominion and countless miracles. It houses the Heavenly Host, gives
sanctuary to the saints and other holy spirits, and rewards the virtuous
with followers and True Faith.
