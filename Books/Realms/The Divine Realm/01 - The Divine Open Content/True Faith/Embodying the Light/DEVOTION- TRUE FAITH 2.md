Devotion is an infused quality, wherein the soul perceives the
perfection and truth of the Divine radiance.

When a character has a True Faith Score of 2 he gains the Premonitions
Minor Virtue (**Ars Magica** 5th Edition page 47). Furthermore, the
character may dispel any supernatural (magical and Infernal) illusion or
Faerie glamour, no matter its level, by spending a Faith Point.
