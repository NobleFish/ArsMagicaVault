Botching a prayer roll results in the temporary loss of all Faith Points
for a number of weeks equal to botches scored. A character without Faith
Points who botches a prayer roll will not have any prayer (or invocation
or intimidation of a saint) answered for a number of months equal to the
number of botches scored. A botch may also provoke a tragedy of hubris
(see Holy Powers) if appropriate.
