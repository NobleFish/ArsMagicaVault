There are many theories among Hermetic researchers as to the
relationship between the Divine and the other realms. The four realms
are a division recognized only by the Order of Hermes; Christian
theologians, Muslim philosophers, and Jewish rabbis would consider the
classifications laughable or offensive, while heretics and hedge mages
could have other divisions or explanations. What is certain to Hermetic
thinkers — a certainty that forms one of the foundations of Hermetic
Theory — is that the Divine is the font, or the highest, of the realms
(as discussed in **Ars Magica** 5th Edition, page 182).
