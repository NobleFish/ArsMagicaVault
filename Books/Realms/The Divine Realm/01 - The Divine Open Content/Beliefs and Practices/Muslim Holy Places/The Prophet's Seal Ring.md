When the Prophet intended to write a letter to the ruler of the
Byzantines, he was told that those people did not read any letter unless
it was stamped with a seal. So, the Prophet got a silver ring — as if I
were just looking at its white glitter on his hand — and stamped on it
the expression "Muhammad, Messenger of God."

— The *Sahih* of al-Bukhari (d. 870)
