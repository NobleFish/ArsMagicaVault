As will have become apparent, in some respects women have a less
favorable position under Islamic rule than men — their share of
inheritance is less, and they can be divorced easily. In some
traditions, the testimony of a woman is regarded as having half the
value of that of a man, and the seclusion and veiling of women is also
encouraged. However, women have the same property rights as men, and in
general the law instructs that they should be treated with respect and
kindness. It should also be noted that the extent to which the strict
letter of the law is followed varies from place to place. As has been
described, some women rose to positions of great importance in Muslim
society. In rural areas, in particular, the veiling and seclusion of
women is not commonly practiced, and they take a more active role in
everyday life.
