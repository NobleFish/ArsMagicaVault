**Divine Might:** 25

**Places where baraka may be invoked:** Tomb in Cairo

**Powers:** *Acknowledgment of Evil, Resist Temptation, Grant of
Serenity, The Dutiful Shepherd, Humility of Caesar*

The founder of one of the four major schools of mainstream Islamic law,
Muhammad ibn Idris al-Shafi'i studied and taught in Arabia, Egypt and
Iraq. He died and was buried in Cairo in 820.
