Angels, the messengers of God, are the most omnipresent servants of the
Divine. Theologians have calculated the number of angels to exceed some
two hundred and sixty-six million, although the exact number can never
be known and may be even more, since every living soul has its own
guardian angel. Angels are the caretakers of humanity and the world.
They are made in imitation of God and far surpass humans as intellectual
and virtuous beings. They are radiant and exist in abundant
communication with Him. It is the role of the ninth Angelic Hierarchy to
turn humans towards the Divine, to elevate their spirits, and care for
their souls. When their charge dies, they comfort him and lead the soul
to its reward (or punishment).
