Kabbalah is the Jewish form of the Invocation Divine Method and can be
used in the same way. In addition, it is the mystical understanding of
the Divine forces of creation and provides the knowledge through which
the Kabbalist forms and brings life to a golem (see the Jewish Mysticism
section later).

**Specialties**: golems, specific Holy Power (Supernatural)
