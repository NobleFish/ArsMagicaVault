*Minor, Social Staus*

Appointed by the community council, the gabai is the local tax
collector. It is his responsibility to ensure that Jewish taxes, payable
to the community on such things as wine and meat, are collected from all
adult males.

The gabai has a –2 negative Reputation of "Tax Collector" in your his
community. This Flaw is compatible with any other Free Social Status
Virtue.
