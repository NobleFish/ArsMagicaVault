*Major, Supernatural Ability*

Your character begins with Dream Interpretation 1, a Supernatural
Ability that represents his ability to divine the truth behind prophetic
dreams. The value of dream interpretation is well understood in Jewish
society and, given its Biblical associations, it is an accepted form of
divination. This Ability may be used in place of Meditation as a Holy
Method when activating holy powers (see Abilities).
