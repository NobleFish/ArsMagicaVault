*Minor, Social Status*

Your character is an ordained Rabbi, a teacher and wise man of the
Jewish community. He is afforded the respect due from all within his
community, who look to him for leadership and guidance. As a rabbi, your
character is expected to teach students, to study and write upon the
law, and to pass judgment upon cases brought before the the Jewish
court.

The rabbi must take the Educated (Hebrew) Virtue to provide the required
Academic Abilities. This Virtue is only available to male characters.
