*Free, Social Status*

The shamash is the servant of the synagogue and responsible for its
well-being. He must ensure that the synagogue is clean and in good
repair that it is available for all the community. He is also
responsible for waking the community early each morning so that they can
attend prayers and for making announcements concerning services, births,
marriages, and deaths.

As a shamash, the character must have the Educated (Hebrew) Virtue.
