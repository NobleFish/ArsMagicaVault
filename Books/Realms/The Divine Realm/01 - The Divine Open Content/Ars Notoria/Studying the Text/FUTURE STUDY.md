Ars Notoria is an Ability and may be increased through study as normal.
Glosses of the basic text exist, both summa and tractatus, written by
practitioners for use by their fellows. Generally, these texts do not
contain new rings of Solomon, but are personal observations and
commentaries on the existing rituals. While these glosses are useful in
increasing the understanding of the Notory Art, they are inadequate for
learning the Ability initially.
