Christian grogs probably attend church services regularly, as often as
is permitted by their Hermetic masters. This is perhaps because they
were born in a religious parish and have family who expect them to come
home, or because they are pious and care for their immortal souls.
Fighting for magi may be a difficult vocation to explain to their
confessors, and this may be a source of interesting stories should the
covenant come into conflict with the Church.
