*Major, Supernatural, Story*

You do not follow the teachings of the Church, and have never been
baptized. This tends to upset those in authority in Mythic Europe and
frighten common people who learn of it. You do not observe Christian
holidays, and you try to avoid churchmen and the Dominion. For whatever
reason, you cannot pretend to go along with society — you might suffer
grave supernatural consequences if you take Communion or appear to
worship Christ. You may begin with either Magic or Faerie Lore,
depending on the specifics of your faith.
