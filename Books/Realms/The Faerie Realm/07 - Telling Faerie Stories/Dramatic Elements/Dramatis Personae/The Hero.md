The hero is the one who sets off on adventure. The hero might be a
seeker-hero (looking for a prize) or a victim-hero (forced into
adventure by the actions of the villain). In many stories in the Faerie
Realm, the characters collectively take on the role of the hero,
although this is not always the case.

Examples: Odysseus (in The Odyssey); Samson (in the Book of Judges);
Es-Sindibad (“Sinbad”).
