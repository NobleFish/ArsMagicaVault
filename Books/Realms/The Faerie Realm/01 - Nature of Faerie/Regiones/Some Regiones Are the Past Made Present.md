Many humans see faeries as linked to the past. This is particularly
apparent in areas where the dominant group displaced another tribe to
gain its land. In part, this is because when one nation invades another,
they often bring alien supernatural forces that subdue the indigenous
faeries. In areas where invasion occurred, some faeries, rather than
flee or accept secondary status, instead enter exile and recreate the
lands and customs of the displaced tribe, so that their stories can
continue undisturbed.
