A Redcap is selling Gifted children to members of the Order as
apprentices. He seems to have a regular supply of them, although they
all have Faerie Blood (of the Ettin variety). He is shadowing an ettin
who is stealing Gifted children, and then stealing the children in turn
from the faerie and selling them on, heedless of the Code that specifies
that he must not molest the fae. A magus who receives one of these
children may have to cope with the enraged ettin when she tracks down
“her” child.
