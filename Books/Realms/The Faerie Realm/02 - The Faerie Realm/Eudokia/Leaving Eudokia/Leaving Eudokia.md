At the completion of the various challenges put in the way of the
characters, it is customary for the Threshold faerie who is mediating
the story to appear once again and send them back to the mortal realm
with its Spirit Away power. This may not be as obvious as the sudden
appearance of a faerie — the characters might just find the way out of
the labyrinth that formed the Guardian in the first place, or they may
follow a will-o’the-wisp over the hill and back home.
