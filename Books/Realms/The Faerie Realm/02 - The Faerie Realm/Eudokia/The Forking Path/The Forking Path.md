A character typically embarks upon the Forked Path if he strays into a
Faerie Aura while on the verge of some major life change. The character
might be about to experience dramatically altered circumstances — such
when he’s about to be married — or else he may be experiencing a
personal threshold — such as adolescence, menopause, or senescence.
Consequently, experiences in Eudokia tend to be solo affairs, although
characters undergoing a similar change occasionally have such ad
ventures together. An example might be a groom and the rest of his stag
party, or a cohort of Hermetic apprentices about to take their Gauntlet.
