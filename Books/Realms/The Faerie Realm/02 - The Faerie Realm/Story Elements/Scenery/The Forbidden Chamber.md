There is a room in a castle that everyone is forbidden from entering. It
has an immensely strong door but no lock. On occasion, strange noises
are heard from behind the door. Alternatively, it could be locked, and
the key held by the castle’s owner. Or, the chamber could instead be a
chest, or a question that must not be asked.

Path of Chance: The room contains either fabulous treasure or a hideous
secret. Disobedience might be signified by a transformation in the
interloper, or the sounding of an alarm.

Path of Destiny: The inevitability of getting into the forbidden chamber
is central to the quest. This may require the quester to obtain the key
from its owner. The characters may not like what they find inside.

Forking Path: This is a simple choice between curiosity and obedience.
It may be made harder if cries for help are heard from beyond the door.
If curiosity is not stronger, then perhaps chivalry is.
