The sun shines bright on a green and pleasant land. Plants are at the
height of growth, and animals are well fed and content. Bees drone among
the flowers, and the air is still and fragrant.

Path of Chance: In the Summerlands pies and sweetmeats grow on trees,
the rivers run with mead and milk, and every need is catered to. It is a
respite from hardship, and also a trap for the self-indulgent.

Path of Destiny: Summer is symbolic of bounteous times, of incipient
action, and of vigor. It is in the Lands of Summer where one is forever
young.

Forking Path: The luxury of summer lulls one to sloth, but it is a
season of action and adventure. Does one take a muchneeded rest, or
spurn the seduction?
