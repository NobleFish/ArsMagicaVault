A dark and foreboding wood, where little light reaches through the
canopy. Strange noises haunt the forest, along with half-seen, swiftly
moving shapes. Alternatively, the trees could be dead, and all sounds
are swallowed.

Path of Chance: A forest can hide all sorts of ne’er-do-wells,
mysterious hermits, magical trees, and fierce beasts.

Path of Destiny: The traditional abode of the hag, the Ironwood is
filled with her wolfish children.

Forking Path: They say there is nothing to fear except fear itself. How
true is that when one is lost among demonic-looking shadows and
sharp-fanged critters?
