Snow covers the ground, rime-frost coats every surface, and icicles
depend from horizontal surfaces. The breath steams in the frigid air,
and the heat is sapped from the body.

Path of Chance: Winter is a passive guardian for the snow-bound castle,
or a prison for those within. It is a challenge to be met, and overcome.

Path of Destiny: Winter represents sterility and patience. Nothing grows
and nature holds its breath before the promise of spring. It serves as a
pause before the action, the lull before the storm. In the winter, the
wicked queen ruled supreme, Demeter ceased her search for her daughter,
and the men were softened for Ragnarok.

Forking Path: Many rail against the chill of winter with fire and furs,
but is it a fight that can be won? Perhaps it is better instead to
submit to the inevitability of nature.
