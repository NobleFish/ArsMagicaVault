A beautiful maiden imprisoned in a tower, pit, or dungeon. An essential
companion actor is her guard — typically an ogre, a dragon, or a hedge
of thorns. Her parents have placed her in this situation, for complex
reasons of their own.

Path of Chance: This is a straightforward rescue mission.

Path of Destiny: The obstacles must be overcome and the princess rescued
in precisely the right manner. Alternatively, the quester could be the
princess, or the guard who must fail to stop the hero.

Forking Path: Have the characters stopped to think as to why the
princess is imprisoned? Perhaps there is a very good reason.
Alternatively, this could be a test of obedience — is it not a father’s
right to dispose of his children as he sees fit?
