Two lions, indistinguishable from each other. They are impressive and
fearsome creatures, but are as playful as kittens, and friendly. They
have the ability to speak, and ask for assistance in a minor task (such
as removing a thorn, or rescuing their son from a pit). Alternatively,
this could be any predatory animal.

Path of Chance: The lions offer friendship and aid.

Path of Destiny: The questers must rescue the lions from peril to ensure
that their help is bought for a later part of the quest.

Forking Path: Not everything that is frightening is actually a cause for
fear. Refusing the help of the lions will hurt their feelings, but
characters might be too suspicious of ulterior motives to take what is
offered freely.
