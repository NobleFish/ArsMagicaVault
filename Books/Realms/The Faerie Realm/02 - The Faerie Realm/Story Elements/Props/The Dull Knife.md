An unremarkable blade, of crude manufacture. Its blade is so dull it can
barely cut butter. Alternatively, this could be any tool that is unable
to perform its function due to a defect.

Path of Chance: In the contradictory way of Faerie, sometimes the
dullest knife in the world is the only thing that can cut the
uncuttable.

Path of Destiny: The knife becomes rusty to indicate when another is in
peril. At such a signal, the quester must ride to rescue his partner.

Forking Path: As a gift, the Dull Knife is useless. Should this be
interpreted as an insult on the part of the giver, or will the character
accept it with grace?
