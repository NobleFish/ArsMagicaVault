To alter its role so that it gains additional powers, a faerie requires
several contributing factors. It requires the potential to change within
its glamour, represented by the cognizance Virtues. It also needs
sufficient mystical energy to change, which is stolen from humans and
represented by Pretense points. Finally, it needs a human collaborator
to provide the creativity required to design the changes in its glamour.
