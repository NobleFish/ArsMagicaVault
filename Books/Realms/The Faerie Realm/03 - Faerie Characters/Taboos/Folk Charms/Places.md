Flaws based on places must seriously inconvenience the character. Some
faeries are so tied to their home that the rest of the earth acts as a
Traditional Ward against them. They can travel foreign lands provided
they do not touch the earth, which burns them. Some vampiric faeries
rest in coffins lined with the earth of their homeland to allow their
Might to regenerate. Characters tied to their home with a Sovereign Ward
simply cannot leave, and are unsuitable as player characters in most
sagas.
