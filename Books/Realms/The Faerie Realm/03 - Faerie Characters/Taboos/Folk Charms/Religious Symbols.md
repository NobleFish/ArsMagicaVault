Faeries with this taboo flee church bells, but this is unusual in urban
faeries. The Dutch, for example, believe that all of the oldest church
bells in their country were made by faeries, as presents for early
missionaries. A surprising number of English faeries are dedicated
Christians, and yet one English saint was able to destroy a faerie bower
with a bottle of holy water, disrupting its glamour so that it looked
like a charnel pit.

Many of the most powerful faeries cannot enter the Dominion, because God
has not invited them in and they are unable to trespass. Certain
Merinita magi dream of a day when they can get a bishop to do this, on
God’s behalf.
