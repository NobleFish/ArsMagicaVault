Most hybrid forms are simply human forms with animal elements grafted
onto them. These elements may be simply cosmetic — in which case they do
not change the character’s scores — or they may represent Minor Virtues.
The physical anomalies of the bodies of faeries are not Flaws unless
they limit the faerie in stories. As an example, the serpent’s tail that
a faerie has instead of legs is not a Flaw if she spends most of her
time in Arcadia or her cave in the hills, since it’s only a detriment if
it is going to terrify humans that she’d prefer were civil.

Some faeries have animal parts that they use for combat. This uses the
Brawl Ability and the statistics on the following table. Minimum
Strength, Load, and cost do not apply to these weapons. Having natural
weapons does not, in itself, require a Virtue.
