Favored Abilities: Empathy, Ware, Weal, Woe

There are many sorts of faerie-touched men and women who typically live
on the fringes of society. Called “wise men” and “wise women” by
villagers who turn to them when troubled, they are also forced to live
apart from the community because of their strange ways and their
intimidating powers. Their strengths are in keeping the supernatural at
bay and protecting those in their charge, and through their exertions
they can mend or injure those who they think worthy. It is folk like
this that the magi of the Order of Hermes often imagine when they use
the term “hedge wizard.”
