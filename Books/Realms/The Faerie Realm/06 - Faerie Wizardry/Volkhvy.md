Favored Abilities: Evocation, Ceremony, Conjure, Grant

Far beyond the reach of the eastern arm of the Christian Church are
people who have never heard the Gospels and who still keep the old pagan
ways — Russian faerie wizards known as the Volkhvy. Their great rites
are legendary, where everyone in the community comes together to entreat
their faerie gods for aid, usually through glamours and blessings that
reflect the harsh wilderness in which they live. They typically possess
great conviction because of these extreme conditions, becoming
especially self-confident, though of course this Virtue could come from
their faerie rites as easily as their personal fortitude.
