Mythic Europeans live in an agrarian society. They live and die by the
strength of their harvests, and this concern draws faeries. The courts
of summer and winter, in many areas, fight battles on the Equinoxes.
Summer defeats winter, and then later in the year, is in turn defeated.
The members of each court are dressed as appropriate for their season.
The faeries of winter often seem harsh, while the faeries of summer are
happy, and it is easy for the untutored to mistake these two sides for
good and evil. Players may be forced to intervene if the seasonal
battles are disrupted. This is because the faeries involved believe they
are vital for agriculture, so their roles force them to destroy crops
with unseasonal frost or droughts.
