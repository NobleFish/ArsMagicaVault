Spring is a time of natural vitality, and faeries seek out the pleasure
this gives to humans. The faeries of the spring are lighthearted, and
heartening. Some seem to be able to feed from the natural magic of the
world, and not be interested in humans at all. The season of harvests is
both a season for joy and happiness that draws faeries, and of banefires
to keep curses away.
