Lakes seem bottomless, and may link to the lands of the dead. The
surfaces of lakes are reflective, so what lies beneath the lake can
reflect what lies within the viewer. A character wading into a lake is
stepping through a mirror, and into is own reflection. A well is a dark
hole that leads to the underworld. It’s a source of water, which is
life, but at its depths any manner of thing might lurk. Children,
particularly, should be kept away from wells, because you never know
where their shafts might lead, or what might be watching from the
depths, that the children might draw up.
