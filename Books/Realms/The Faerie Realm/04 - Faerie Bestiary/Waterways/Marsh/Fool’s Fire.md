Appearance: This faerie looks like a distant, flickering flame. It
dances away from those who seek it, drawing them further and further
from aid.

Fool’s fires are tiny lights, also called will o’ the wisps, that guide
lost travelers into dangerous terrain. Some fool’s fires act as lure on
behalf of more powerful faeries. In some German areas they are thought
to be the ghosts of people who have moved border marking stones.
