Rivers are the lifeblood of medieval cities, and many were worshiped as
goddesses in pagan Europe. The most powerful of these was Danu, who was
the goddess of the Danube, the Don, the Deipnier, and many other places.
The courtly faeries of Ireland, called the Tuatha de Danu, are her
children. Rivers are guarded by merfolk, and by nymphs.
