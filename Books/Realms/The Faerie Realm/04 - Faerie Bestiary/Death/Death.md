Just before death, the spirit flares as it flexes free of the body. This
attracts the presence of many different types of faerie. This section
does not concern itself with faeries who kill to feed on the fleeing
spirit. Instead, it details faeries who wait for the person’s natural
time of death and interact then, or with faeries that seem to cross to
the mortal world from the lands of the dead.
