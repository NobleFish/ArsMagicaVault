Nuckalevee: The Irish Nuckalevee is like a flayed centaur that lairs in
the sea, and forages for human victims ashore. Its breath causes plague,
and it is terrified of fresh water. It represents the division of
between potable and salt water. The point on rivers that is closest to
the coastline, but where the water is still potable, is often the site
of settlement, so the nuckalevee is a dire threat. The point where
freshwater turned back the salt was also significant in the folklore of
the pre-Christian Celts.
