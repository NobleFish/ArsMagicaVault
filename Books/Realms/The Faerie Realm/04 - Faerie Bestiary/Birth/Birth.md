Humans, just after birth, are bundles of tremendous vitality. Faeries
can harvest this potential in several ways. The most direct way of
harvesting the vitality of a child is to kill and eat it. This method is
rare, but so horrible that it is widely famed. A related way to harvest
attention is to refuse to harm children protected by a particular
ritual, and accept the performance of the ritual as worship. Some
faeries steal and try to raise children, who then provide an endless
harvest of attention if they survive. More beneficent faeries bless
children at their birth.
