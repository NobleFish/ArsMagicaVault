Serpents with gemstones in their faces, that lack the transformation
power of the vouivre’s eye, are found in the forest of Luchon, at the
foot of the Pyrenees in France. Characters harvesting the vis from these
serpents do not realise that they are the children of a vouivre. When
she bathes, one of her surviving children informs her that they have
been decimated by magicians, and she seeks vengeance. This calls all of
the hedge wizards of the area together, because they want to take
advantage of the vouivre’s distraction by attempting to open the doors
of her subterranean lair and take the treasures there. Terrible
questions, after this dispute is settled, remain. Will any remaining
serpents grow into vouivres? And what manner of creature was their
father? \|}
