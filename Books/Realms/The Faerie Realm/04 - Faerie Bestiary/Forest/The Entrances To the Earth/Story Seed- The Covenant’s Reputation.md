A covenant that develops an evil reputation in surrounding areas
eventually attracts faeries who represent that evil. Evil is often
framed, in medieval storytelling, in terms of the Infernal, so faeries
that have the distinctive folklorisitic marks of demons may begin to
interact with all of the communities that surround the covenant, raising
the ire of the Church and the Quaesitores. Characters cannot fight these
demons in the conventional way: there are always new faeries to fill
vacant roles in a popular story. The characters need to find symbolic
acts that change the perception of the covenant in the minds of
surrounding people. \|}
