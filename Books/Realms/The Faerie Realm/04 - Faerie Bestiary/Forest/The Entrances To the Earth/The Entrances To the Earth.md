Mythic Europeans have a mixed relationship with caves. They are useful,
for shelter and for storage of goods, but they are also dangerous
places, on the verge of a different world. In parts of Italy, people use
caves to store wine and cheese, and when they are ready, the grotto
becomes a tavern. In Roman times, many of these taverns were guarded by
statues of Cerberus, the guardian of the gate of Hades. Caves are like
tombs: revels within them may lead to contact with creatures that guard
the border of the deep earth, or the land of the dead.
