In a world where demons might be hiding under any stone, and the
Dominion protects the homes of God’s people against their infernal
designs, sin is a recognizable phenomenon that actually stains the
transgressor’s soul. In order to play characters who sin, and describe
the plots of tempters who entice people to do evil, players must first
understand exactly what sin means in Mythic Europe.
