*True to the fears of the bishop, some of the Orléans heretics escaped,
and fled to safe houses where they were able to hide from those seeking
to punish them for their crimes, and continue teaching their dangerous
ideas to their disciples. One of their descendants, a priest, is
suspected of practicing this heresy and was captured by a witch-hammer,
but somehow managed to escape before the inquisition began. He arrives
at the covenant and asks for sanctuary. He is* *cagey about his past,
but if his story is uncovered, he claims that the heretics of Orléans
were unfairly persecuted. He does not deny their heretical beliefs,
which promote a very skeptical view of God and the universe, but claims
that the Church has manufactured the evidence of their devil-worship
because it fears the truth of their message. Of course, if allowed to
stay, he continues to preach his heresy to all those who will listen.*
