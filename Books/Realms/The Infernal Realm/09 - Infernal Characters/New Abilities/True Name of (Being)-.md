This is not an actual Ability, but rather a placeholder for knowledge
gained from studying Infernal Lore. By learning a being’s True Name, the
character can affect it as if she had a permanent Arcane Connection to
it. She learns this by studying the appropriate (Realm) Lore, which
teaches her the True Name once she has gained 5 experience points, and
also gives her a free experience point in that Arcane Ability. See
Chapter 4: Infernal Legions, True Names for more information. (Special)
