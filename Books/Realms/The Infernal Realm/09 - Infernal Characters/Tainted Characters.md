Demons may meddle with the creation of children to draw them under the
demons’ influence. The attention of the demon taints the child, which
may make it appear to have inherited some of the demon’s properties. The
ill-educated, including the tainted person, might ascribe these
properties to demonic blood. Demons often tell tainted people that they
share a blood kinship, as a form of emotional manipulation. Tainted
children grow into tools, which demons employ in their schemes.
