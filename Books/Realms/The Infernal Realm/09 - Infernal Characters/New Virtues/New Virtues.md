#### Ablating

Major, Goetic Art

When combined with Summoning, Ablating allows a sorcerer to leech away
the power of a demon or other spirit for his own. This Virtue opens the
Ablating Art for him at 0, the mechanics of which are found in Chapter
11: Ars Goetia.
