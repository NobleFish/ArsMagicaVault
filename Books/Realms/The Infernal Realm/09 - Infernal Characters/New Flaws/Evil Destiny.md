Major, Story

The Infernal realm has taken an interest in the character, but he does
not know how or why. Perhaps one of his Powers is actually Infernal, or
demons are plotting against him, or he is convinced that he is being
stalked by something evil.

He cannot discuss this openly for fear that he will be accused of
infernalism, but must be always on his guard against the dark forces
that he knows will come for him one day.

The storyguide will use this Flaw to subtly work the Infernal realm into
your character, leaving you unaware of the specifics. You might have one
of the other Flaws in this chapter, or a combination of several Minor
Flaws, or even a Flaw that changes periodically. The character could
simply be Plagued by Demons or Susceptible to the Infernal. Through the
course of the story you may eventually discover what his Flaw is, but
until then you are only haunted with the knowledge that something about
him is not right.
