Minor, Supernatural, Tainted

One or more of the character’s Abilities was taught to her by a Watcher
(see Chapter 6: Demons as Deceivers), or came through some other
corrupted source of study. Use of a corrupted Ability for a selfish or
sinful action receives a +3 bonus to the roll. Succeeding in a roll
because of this bonus (that is, when without it the roll would have
failed) means that the character immediately acquires 5 experience
points in that Ability. However, uses of the Ability that are neutral or
selfless receive a –3 penalty, and if you fail the roll because of this
penalty, the character loses 5 experience points in the Ability.

Any use of a corrupted Ability is an unholy act, which can be sensed by
Divine Powers. You may only take this Flaw once, though you can choose
to have it affect multiple Abilities if you wish.
