Major or Minor, Personality

The character seeks to undermine religion whenever possible, and
generally tries to avoid the Dominion. He might sin in public, teach
misinformation about the tenets of the faith, or show obvious disdain
for holy things. He is not necessarily an infernalist; it could be that
he simply despises organized religion, dislikes the Divine realm, or
does not believe in God. He might even be persuaded to attend services,
though he does not take any of it seriously and is likely to disrupt the
ceremony with his discomfort and ignorance.
