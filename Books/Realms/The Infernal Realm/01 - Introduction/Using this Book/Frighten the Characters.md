Simply put, the Infernal is terrifying. Horrors gather beyond the
boundaries of the covenant, and in the world outside, demons can be
anywhere and everywhere. They can even seep past defenses through
insidious means such as Personality Traits, curses, and, of course, the
stain of sin itself. Dread and fear can dramatically improve a story
when appropriate, and the Infernal realm certainly makes for a good
scare.
