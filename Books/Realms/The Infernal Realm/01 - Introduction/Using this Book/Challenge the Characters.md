As a source of antagonists, the Infernal realm is unmatched. It is
fairly easy to build an adventure simply on the strength of a demon that
takes an interest in something near and dear to the characters. Demons
can play politics, meddle with people’s heads, and cause an incredible
amount of damage when they put their minds to it. If the characters do
not have a means for “dealing” with demons, they will quickly realize
how badly they need one.
