Magi are often the driving force behind a saga, and their quest for
greater power and more magic can lead them into corruption, for no
supernatural powers are as easy to gain as infernal ones. Many may seem
like they would be relatively little trouble — distasteful, yes, but
distaste is easily overcome. Magi may stumble upon the Infernal while
searching for vis, or even studying their magical Arts. Other
characters, too, even grogs, may see the Infernal realm as an easy way
to improve their abilities and perhaps give them an even footing with
their Hermetic masters.
