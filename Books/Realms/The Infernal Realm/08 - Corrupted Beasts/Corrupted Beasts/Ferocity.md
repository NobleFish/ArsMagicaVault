Minor General Virtue, animals only

Like companion and magus characters, the animal has a Confidence score
of 1. However, it can only use Confidence in situations where its
corrupt nature is triggered, such as when hunting children or attacking
a priest. Describe a situation which activates the Confidence of the
creature; in such circumstances the creature has 3 Confidence Points.
Some creatures have this Virtue without it being derived from an
Infernal source.
