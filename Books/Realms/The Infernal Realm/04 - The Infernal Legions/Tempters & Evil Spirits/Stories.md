- A personal Tempter may feign friendship towards her mark in order to
  lure her into sin. The very jealousy engendered by the demon might be
  its downfall when her victim sees her “best friend” talking to
  another.
- A terrible disease affects a village, but all physicians and
  herb-wives who try to approach are waylaid by evil spirits. Why should
  these demons want to prevent the disease being cured? How does this
  win more souls for Hell?
