Some magi decide to push the boundaries of the Code and seek out demons.
They have many different reasons: for sport, for revenge, or perhaps to
be the one who strikes first. The Order seems to have no official
position on demon-hunters, as there have been Peripheral Code rulings
both for and against them, and most Tribunals prefer to evaluate their
actions on a case-by-case basis. Generally speaking, it is a Hermetic
crime to aggravate demons, though destroying them is considered a
laudable act.
