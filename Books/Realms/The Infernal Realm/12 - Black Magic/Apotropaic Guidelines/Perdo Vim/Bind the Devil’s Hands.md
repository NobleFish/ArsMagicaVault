PeVi Gen

R: Touch, D: Sun, T: Room

This spell hinders the operation of Infernal Powers in the room it
affects. All Casting Totals of maleficia are reduced by (the level of
this spell – 15). Other Infernal Powers which rely on a Supernatural
Ability are similarly reduced. A demon is required to spend (the
magnitude of this spell – 3) extra Might Points to activate any of its
Powers, although Powers which normally have no cost are unaffected. This
spell is often used to imprison demons or infernalists.

*(Base effect, +1 Touch, +2 Sun, +2 Room)*
