PeVi 5

R: Voice, D: Sun, T: Ind

This spell, if it penetrates Magic Resistance, removes 5 Might Points
from a demon. This is a painful experience, and can be used to force
compliance much as one would when whipping a recalcitrant animal. It is
therefore one of the more dangerous spells to be known by a member of
the Order, and incurs heavy suspicion of diabolism.

*(Base effect, +2 Voice)*
