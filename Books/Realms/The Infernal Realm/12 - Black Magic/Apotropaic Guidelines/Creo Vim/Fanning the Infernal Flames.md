CrVi Gen

R: Voice, D: Sun, T: Ind

This spell magically restores some of a demon’s Infernal Might, adding
(the spell’s level – 10) to the demon’s Might Pool as long as the effect
penetrates the demon’s Magic Resistance. These additional Might Points
cannot exceed the demon’s Might Score.

This spell is known to exist among infernalist magi, and is only
mentioned to students of the Infernal so that they may know what to
expect.

*(Base effect, +2 Voice, +2 Sun)*
