This special ability may only be taken for spells that target spirits:
demons, ghosts, faerie shades, or other incorporeal beings with a Might
score.

The caster may add one of his scores in Summoning, Commanding, Binding,
or Ablating (whichever of these four Arts is most appropriate to the
effect of the associated spell) to his Casting Total whenever he casts
this spell. He also adds his (Hierarchy Score x 5) to his Penetration
Total when he casts this spell on a demon. The storyguide should also
subtract the demon’s (Hierarchy Score x 5) from the result in such
cases.

This ability taints the spell by association with the Goetic Arts, and
it always appears to be an Unholy Power when subjected to Divine or
Infernal Powers of investigation.
