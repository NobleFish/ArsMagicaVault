Carolus of Tytalus unwittingly uses three pawns of Vim vis sordida and
five pawns of regular Vim vis opening an item for enchantment. His first
invested effect is 20th level, and thus uses up 20 of the tainted
levels, forcing an experimentation roll. Should his player roll a zero
on the Experimentation table, he must roll six extra botch dice (three
for each of the two pawns). The second effect he invests is also 20th
level, but as this uses up the last ten tainted levels in the item,
another experimentation roll is made. A botch on this roll requires only
three extra botch dice. Further enchantments do not use tainted slots,
so enchantment proceeds as normal.
