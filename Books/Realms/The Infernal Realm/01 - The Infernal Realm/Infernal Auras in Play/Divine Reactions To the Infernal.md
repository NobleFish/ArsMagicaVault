Beings who belong or are affiliated to the Divine realm most commonly
feel disgust if they draw upon the source of their power while within an
Infernal aura. Their senses are more attuned to the signs of corruption,
and they may see objects spotted with blood, suffer a foul taste in
their mouths, or hear the faint cries of tortured souls that indicate
the filthy nature of the place into which they have strayed. Any
character whose faith is strong and is affiliated to the Divine realm
receives a +3 bonus to all Perception rolls that would reveal the
corrupt nature of the aura. However, this insight may not always be a
positive matter, as such characters are more likely to be affected by
the horror of the sin about them.

While the unveiling of manifest evil may be a harrowing experience,
those whose faith is firm can feel it forming a shield against the
corruption of an Infernal aura. Those with True Faith are able to see
through the falsehood around them and transcend such petty concerns,
knowing that the power of God far surpasses the power about them. While
still affected by the general effects of an Infernal aura listed above,
characters with a Faith Score of at least 1 are immune to the additional
effects imposed by different varieties of Infernal aura (see below).
