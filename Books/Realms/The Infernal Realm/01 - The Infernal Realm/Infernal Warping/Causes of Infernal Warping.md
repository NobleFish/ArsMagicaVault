Infernal warping may result from the practice of sin or from exposure to
powerful Infernal forces such as strong auras or Infernal sorcery. Of
course, Warping Points can also result from magical, Faerie, and even
Divine sources, but a character suffers the effects of Infernal Warping
if the majority of Warping Points that contribute to this level of
Warping have been through Infernal influence.
