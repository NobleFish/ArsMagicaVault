Incantation/Psychomachia, Level 10

R: Eye, D: Conc, T: Ind

The caster can recognize whether a person is more evil or good by
sensing his most powerful emotion. He looks him in the eye as he casts
the maleficium, and becomes aware of his dominant Personality Trait, or
another emotion by which he is currently influenced.

*(Base 4, +1 Eye, +1 Conc)*
