Debauchery/Psychomachia, Level 45

R: Voice, D: Sun, T: Group

Everyone in a group of people is seized with a powerful emotion,
specified as part of the spell, which is usually sinful in nature. They
each gain an appropriate Personality Trait at +3 for the duration of the
effect. Until it wears off, these people become much more receptive to
sins associated with the trait, and may in fact behave completely out of
character in pursuit of their desires.

*(Base 15: +2 Voice, +2 Sun, +2 Sin)*
