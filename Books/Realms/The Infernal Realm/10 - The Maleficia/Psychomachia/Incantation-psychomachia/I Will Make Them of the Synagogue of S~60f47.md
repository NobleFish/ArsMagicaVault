Incantation/Psychomachia, Level 50

R: Voice, D: Sun, T: Group

The caster speaks a simple command to a group of people, which they must
obey for the duration of the effect. Examples: “Attack anyone who comes
through here,” “Journey to the covenant of Semita Errabunda and capture
the woman Isabelle who lives there,” or “Bow down and worship this idol
until the legate arrives.”

*(Base 20: +2 Voice, +2 Sun, +2 Group)*
