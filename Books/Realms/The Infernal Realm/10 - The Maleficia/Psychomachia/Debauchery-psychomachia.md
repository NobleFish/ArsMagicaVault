General  
Tarnish an Infernal aura with a value equal to (the magnitude of the
effect + 3), +6 maximum.

Level 2  
Make a minor change to a target’s emotions (transforming sadness to
despair, for example).

Level 3  
Make a major change to a target’s emotions (transforming resolve into
paranoia, for example).

Level 4  
Increase or decrease a character’s Personality Trait by one step, to a
maximum of +3 or –3.

  
Completely change a target’s emotions (transforming anger into euphoria,
for example).

Level 5  
Increase or decrease a character’s Personality Trait by one step, to a
maximum of +4 or –4.

  
Give a target a temporary Personality Trait at +1.

  
Give a target a Warping Point.

Level 10  
Increase or decrease a character’s Personality Trait by one step, to a
maximum of +5 or –5.

  
Give a target a temporary Personality Trait at +2.

Twilight.
