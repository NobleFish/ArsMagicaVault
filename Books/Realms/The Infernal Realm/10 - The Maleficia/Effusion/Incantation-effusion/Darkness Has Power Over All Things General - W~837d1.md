Debauchery/Effusion, Level 20

R: Sight, D: Conc, T: Ind

This effect gathers severe winds, powerful enough to lift a person
through the air and carry him through the sky at high speed. The
character must concentrate upon these winds in order to control them,
and she cannot do this if they fly out of her sight. Most infernalists
cast this effect on themselves, winging through the night to reach their
evil gatherings, though it is said that they must not speak the name of
Christ while aloft, or they will lose their support and immediately fall
to the ground.

*(Base 4: +3 Sight, +1 Conc)*
