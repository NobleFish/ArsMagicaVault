A cage of change is an enchanted item that is intended to rapidly Warp
an animal, causing it to become monstrous by using an effect similar to
The Enigma’s Gift (ArM5, page 157). This is done in the hope that the
animal may then be slain and harvested for Muto vis. Using a cage of
change is considered an unpleasant act by most magi, and many Bjornaer
and Merinita magi consider the device abhorrent.
