The Cockerel of Virtue benefits farmers through its crowing, which
drives away evil spirits. A mundane cockerel normally crows to herald
the coming of dawn, so the Cockerel of Virtue’s cry terrifies spirits of
darkness through the Law of Sympathy. A variant effect for the
Cockerel’s crow may be found on page 29 of Houses of Hermes: Mystery
Cults. Cocks fight one another for dominance. The winner is the proudest
of all creatures and struts regally around the barnyard, but the loser
becomes dejected (temporarily gaining the Personality Flaw, Depressed)
and does not crow. The comb of a Cockerel of Virtue can be Enriched by
stretching the skin over an amber bead which must then rest on
consecrated ground for a period not less than 12 Sundays. When worn
around the neck, the wearer gains Second Sight with an Ability of 3.''
