A character with Animal Ken (ArM5, page 62) can speak to animals,
including some magical beasts. Such a character can attempt to reason
with any animal (regardless of whether it is tamed or not) using his
Animal Handling Ability, and so convince it to perform a task. This does
not result in a tamed animal, merely an animal that has decided to obey
a particular instruction. Animal Ken adds to taming totals, and the
character may issue more-complex commands to a tamed animal. The animal
is no more likely to obey these commands, but it may allow the character
to better utilize an animal as a scout or informant. Another important
use of Animal Ken is to convince an animal that is balking at performing
some dangerous task that the task is not dangerous after all. \|}
