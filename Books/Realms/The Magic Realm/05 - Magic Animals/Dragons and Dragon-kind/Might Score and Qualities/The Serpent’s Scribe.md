Polymathes the draconic librarian has acquired a few Hermetic books over
the years, and has decided it’s time to expand his collection. A true
connoisseur, he wants only books of high Quality. He is certainly not
willing to trade away anything from his own collection, but he would let
someone come to his lair and copy a book in exchange for a new tome as
payment. If the copyist does a good job, Polymathes decides to employ
that person (indefinitely) to copy and trade more Hermetic books. Being
a dragon, he does not phrase this as a request. \|}
