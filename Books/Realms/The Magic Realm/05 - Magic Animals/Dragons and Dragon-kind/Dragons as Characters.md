While the classic dragon is much too powerful to use as a player
character, one can create a fairly weak dragon using the rules in
Chapter 4: Magic Characters, suitable for use as a grog, companion, or
magus-equivalent character — or as a character’s Magical (Being)
Companion. For dragons meant as non-player characters, it’s not
necessary to strictly obey the Ars Magica Fifth Edition
character-creation rules; for instance, there’s no need to make sure a
mighty dragon’s Virtues are all properly balanced with Flaws. Dragons
are supposed to be powerful.
