Legends speak of human wizards who have unlocked the secret of
immortality and achieved ascendency to the Hall of Heroes, becoming
Daimons of the Magic Realm (see Chapter 7: Spirits). The process of
becoming an immortal spirit is called apotheosis. It is the ultimate
goal of several mystery cults. The mystical groups that pursue
apotheosis, along with rules for the transformation itself, are detailed
in The Mysteries: Revised Edition (pages 82-86).
