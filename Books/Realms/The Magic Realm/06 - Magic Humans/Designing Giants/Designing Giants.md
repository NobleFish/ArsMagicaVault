Technically, any being of humanoid form, but larger than human size,
could be considered a giant. This chapter is mainly concerned with
gigantic beings who do not fall into any other category: human-like
giants, as opposed to large elementals or the like. Giants get their
enormous Size from the Gigantic Quality (see Chapter 4: Magic
Characters, Major Magic Qualities).
