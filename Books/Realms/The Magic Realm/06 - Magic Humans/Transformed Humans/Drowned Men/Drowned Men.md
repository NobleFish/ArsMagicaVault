Sometimes when a sailor drowns at sea his body passes into a magical
regio and is eventually returned to the world of living — perhaps
washing up on a foreign shore unconscious but alive. It is unclear
whether these sailors have actually drowned and then undergone some kind
of resurrection, or whether they were rescued at the very last moment
prior to drowning by some magical force. In either case, Drowned Men are
irreparably changed by the experience and are now creatures of magic.
