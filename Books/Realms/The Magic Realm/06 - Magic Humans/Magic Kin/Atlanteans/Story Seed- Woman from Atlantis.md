Word is brought to the covenant of a noblewoman caught in the ocean by
fishermen living in a nearby town. The woman is very surly and
uncommunicative, although she does stridently proclaim that she is
Pavania — a princess of Atlantis — and that simple fishermen are beneath
her. Pavania is aware of The Gift, although she has not heard of the
Order of Hermes, and if she learns of the magi’s powers she will demand
that they remove from her “the taint of the surface world.” In return,
Pavania says that she will guide the magi to her home, the sunken city
of Atlantis, where they will be richly reward by her grateful father,
the King of Atlantis. Do the magi believe her? How do they remove “the
taint of the surface world?” Even if Pavania is telling the truth, who
(or what) is the King of Atlantis and what would he consider a “reward?”
