When a famous figure in the Order of Hermes dies suddenly (Vinaria, the
former Prima of House Merinita, for example), her body reverts to the
form of a tree. In her will, she admits to being a Loamwalker and
explains what this entailed. Her words imply that there are other
Loamwalkers in the Order, as she warns the magi to mend their ways. The
matter is brought to an emergency Tribunal to force these other
tree-people hiding in their midst to reveal themselves. A trusted Redcap
known to the characters — perhaps Ash, from The Broken Covenant of
Calebais — admits to the characters that she is another of these trees
in human form, and asks them to help shelter her from morepowerful magi
who would use the threat of war to force her to reveal her secrets. \|}
