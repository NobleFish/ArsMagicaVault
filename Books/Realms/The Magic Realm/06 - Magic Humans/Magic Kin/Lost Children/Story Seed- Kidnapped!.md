While traveling through a nearby wood, a player character attracts the
notice of a Lost Child, though he does not know it. This adolescent
falls in love with the character, and begins to follow the group
closely. Being magical, the child is not interested in the niceties of
courting or wooing, but instead engineers an abduction during the night,
carrying off the character with whom she is infatuated. When the other
characters awake, they realize they are missing one of their number and
must follow the trail into the forest to recover their friend.
