Finding passage to the Magic Realm from the mundane realm is in itself a
great discovery, for there are only three ways by which travelers can
move from one to the other. These same methods can also allow travel
within the Magic Realm, and can sometimes magically transport a traveler
between regiones and other places associated with Magic in the mundane
realm, causing the person to pass through the Magic Realm only briefly.
