- ReVi 20 R: Per, D: Mom, T: Ind

The length of the caster’s journey through the Twilight Void is reduced
by three steps from his initial Speed Level. With a Speed Level of 6 the
caster would return to the Magic Realm after a year, and this spell
would increase his Speed Level to 18, meaning that he emerges after only
a day. (Base 20)
