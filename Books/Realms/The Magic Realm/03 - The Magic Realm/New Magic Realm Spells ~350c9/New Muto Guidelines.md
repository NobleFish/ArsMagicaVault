Magi use the Art of Muto to change the way that things interact with the
basic nature of the Magic Realm.

Level 5: Change the Form of a boundary or vestige. (Requisite)

Level 10: Associate a target with the Magic Realm, as if it had Magic
Might of the same Form as this effect. This means it can affect things
of that Form with other Magic Realm effects.

Level 15: Cause a target in the Magic Realm to become perceptible as a
vestige to travelers in the Twilight Void. Many features of the Magic
Realm, but not all, have this property naturally. OR Cause a target in
the Twilight Void to become visible as a vestige in the Magic Realm.
