*The prosperity and condition of flora and fauna in and around the aura
may reflect its strength. The sizes of flocks and harvests may increase
if the aura strengthens, and animals and plants may become more perfect.
Any* *other natural resources gathered in the aura — such as water from
a spring or well, or gemstones from a mine — may also increase in size,
number, or perfection. Such prosperity may be reversed in the case of a
weakening aura.*
