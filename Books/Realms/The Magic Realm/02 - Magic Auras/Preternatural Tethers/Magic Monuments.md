*Ancient buildings or constructs that are strongly associated with Magic
might constitute a tether. Examples include temples, menhirs, stone
circles, pyramids, cave paintings, and parts of ancient covenants. As a
rule of thumb, these places can be given one point of tether score for
every full century that they have seen active use. For example, a
standing stone at the covenant of Durenmar, which has been used for more
than four centuries, has a tether score of 4.*
