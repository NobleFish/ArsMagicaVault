Spells of a specific Form can command any spirit tied to that Form. For
example, Ignem can command any fiery spirit. An Arcane Connection is
always needed to summon a spirit.

|              |                                   |
|--------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Level 5**  | Control an Airy Spirit of (Form). |
| **Level 15** | Summon an Airy Spirit of (Form).  |
