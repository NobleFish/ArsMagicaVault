*Wecelo is an expert fisherman able to navigate Lake Brienz better than
anyone the villagers can remember. As a young man, he was among a group
of boatsmen lost in a storm on the lake. Perhaps because he drew the
fancy or the pity of the lake spirit, he was the only one saved from
drowning. Ever since, he has felt a great longing to stay by the lake.
Wecelo is likely to be sought out by anyone seeking passage across (or
into) the lake, or who is interested in its denizens, fish or
otherwise.*
