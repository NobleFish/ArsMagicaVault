Spirits typically have one to five supernatural powers, and this section
details many of the most common ones. General guidelines for powers can
be found in ***Chapter 4: Magic Characters**''. Some of these powers are
similar to those available to the Forest Spirits in Guardians of the
Forests: The Rhine Tribunal. The details given here revise and update
those descriptions. These powers may also be used for creatures other
than spirits. Naturally, any power that affects another being must
penetrate its Magic Resistance. Powers that are similar to Ritual spells
permanently reduce the spirit’s Might score as well as Might pool; Might
Score can be restored according to the mechanism detailed in***Chapter
4: Magic Characters**''.
