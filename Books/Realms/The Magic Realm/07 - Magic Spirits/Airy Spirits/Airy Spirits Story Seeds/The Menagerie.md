The Airy Spirits of creatures and plants are relatively weak, but what
would happen if a multitude of them worked in concert? A magus decides
to test this idea, and begins collecting florae and fauni from across
the countryside. Soon, however, even the local villagers begin to notice
a dampening of vibrance, as if an undefinable gloom hangs over the land.
Meanwhile, how long can the captive spirits survive away from their
natural places, and what effect does the magus hope to achieve with
them?
