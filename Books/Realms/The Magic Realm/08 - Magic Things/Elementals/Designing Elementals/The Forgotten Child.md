A magus uses Incantation of Lightning (or a similar spell of 6th
magnitude or greater) in a Magic aura, which inflicts the last Warping
point needed to rarefy an elemental. Unaware of what he’s done, he moves
on. The elemental he has unwittingly created causes no end of trouble
for the locals. Eventually the magus discovers his error, and must put
right what he has wrought.
