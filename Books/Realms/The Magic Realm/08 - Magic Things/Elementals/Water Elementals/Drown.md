0 points, Init equal to (Qik – 2), Aquam

R: Touch, D: Sun, T: Ind

After successful melee attack, the elemental can engulf a target whose
Size is smaller than its own and attempt to drown him, necessitating a
roll to avoid deprivation (ArM5, pages 180-181). The victim can attempt
to escape from its clutches each round using the Grappling rules (ArM,
page 174).

effect): Greater Power (30 levels, –3 Might cost, +1 Init)
