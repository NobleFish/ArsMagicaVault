A Toad Stone of Bufonite of Virtue is a dull gray stone containing 2
pawns of Intellego vis that, when Enriched, has a Lesser Power to detect
poison. It turns pink or dull red when in contact with poison.
Enrichment requires that the stone be removed from the head of a Toad of
Virtue while it is still alive. The stone must then be placed on red
cloth until it is set into a suitably fine item of personal jewelry or
into a drinking vessel or platter. The whole process should take about a
season. InAq 15 (base 3, +2 Sun, +1 Touch (Target), +1 continuous
effect): Lesser Power (15 levels, –2 Might (fatigue) cost)
