The intense blue of the Periwinkle of Virtue grants Venus’s Blessing.
The hopeful in love must find enough perfect flowers among those that
have been trampled by rutting deer to form a garland, which is strung
around the neck of the dominant stag. After ninety days, the sole
remaining flower of this garland has been Enriched. The recovered
garland also grants the impetuousness of youthful love that shows no
inhibition in pursuing its object of affection (that is, the Reckless
Flaw). Left unenriched, Periwinkle of Virtue is worth 1 pawn of Mentem
vis.
