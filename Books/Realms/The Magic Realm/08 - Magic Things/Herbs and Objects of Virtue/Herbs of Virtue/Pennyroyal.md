The pennyroyal is a creeping plant similar to mint, with clusters of
tiny purple flowers. The Pennyroyal of Virtue contains 1 pawn of Corpus
vis. Known as Heal-All, the star that is ruling when this sovereign herb
is Enriched determines which ailment it cures: Mars protects against
flux; Venus against pox; Mercury against kidney stones; Jupiter against
fever; and Saturn against falling sickness. The herb must be painted
painstakingly with the clearest of resins from a lightning-struck pine;
each week for a season a new layer of lacquer is added. A simple brush
of the herb over the heart or brow will confer this Greater Purifying
Touch. Let the healer be warned, however, for devils will seek to undo
the work he has wrought with this herb (actually a Delusion).
