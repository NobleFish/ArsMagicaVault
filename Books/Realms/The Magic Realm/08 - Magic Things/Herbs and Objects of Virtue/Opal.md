An Opal of Virtue contains 2 pawns of Terram vis. If suitably Enriched,
it provides the Minor Heroic Virtue Sure Traveler (see Houses of Hermes,
True Lineages, page 107) but also produces a minor Personality Flaw. The
Flaw should be chosen by the storyguide, in consultation with the player
if appropriate, and might well be something that will irritate fellow
travelers. Enrichment involves taking the item to the highest point in
the vicinity, where the wizard has a clear view in all directions, for a
season.
