An Enriched piece of Jet of Virtue provides the Minor General Virtue
Puissant Stealth (ArM5, page 48). The Enrichment process involves
leaving it concealed for a season in a place where people gather
frequently, changing the hiding place once a month. The Virtue is lost
if anyone other than the owner sees the jet. Before Enrichment, Jet of
Virtue contains 1 pawn of Perdo vis.
