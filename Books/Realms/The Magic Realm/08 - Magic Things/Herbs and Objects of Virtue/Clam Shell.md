The Clam Shell of Virtue, when Enriched, confers the Minor General
Virtue Tough (ArM5, page 49). To benefit, the owner must wear or carry
the shell. The Enrichment process could require that the shell be put in
the torrent of a powerful waterfall for a season. A shell that survives
the battering is proven Enriched. Before Enrichment, a Clam Shell of
Virtue contains 1 pawn of Terram vis.
