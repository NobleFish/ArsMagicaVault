There is a formed stone that is bent over, like a body crippled with the
pains of arthritis, called Clach Crubain of Virtue. It contains 1 pawn
of Perdo vis. When suitably Enriched, carrying the stone close to one’s
person confers the Minor Supernatural Virtue Lesser Immunity (arthritis)
(ArM5, page 44). The Enrichment process requires one to obtain a measure
of pure oil, probably by crushing freshly picked, unblemished olives or
by pressing oil-rich nuts. Alternatively, oil from an animal source may
be used if the origins are special enough. The oil should be rubbed all
over the stone and allowed to dry off in sunlight strong enough to warm
the stone. The process must be repeated three times and should take
about a season to complete.
