As the covenant begins to harvest too much vis from a magical forest,
the trees and forest spirits notice a slight weakening of the local aura
and take exception to the magi’s activities. The trees begin to awaken
on their own and hinder travelers in various ways: lowering their
branches to block trails, placing their roots where passers-by will trip
over them, and so forth. If the magi escalate the conflict by using axes
or fire, the Florae or genius loci protecting the forest animate the
trees in self-defense.
