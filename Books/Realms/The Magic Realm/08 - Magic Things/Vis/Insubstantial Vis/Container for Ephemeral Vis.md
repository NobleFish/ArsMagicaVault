ReVi Level 24 Pen 0, 12/day

R: Voice, D: Mom, T: Ind

This device is an enchanted seashell. The holder may use it to capture
or release insubstantial vis in auditory form 12 times per day. If the
seashell contains insubstantial vis and is held to the ear, the sound of
the vis may be heard. The same effect can also be enchanted into a
crystal to retain luminescent vis. The more vis the device holds, the
brighter it glows or louder it sounds.

(Effect: Base 10, +2 Voice; Modifications +4 12 uses per day)
