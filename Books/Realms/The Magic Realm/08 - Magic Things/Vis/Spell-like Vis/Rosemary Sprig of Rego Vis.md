ReHe Level 56 R: Touch, D: Moon, T: Str, Ritual

This Rego vis takes the form of a sprig of rosemary. If the vis is hung
above the main entrance of a home, it protects all within the dwelling
from faerie creatures of the wood with Might 10 or less. Hanging the
rosemary above a door completely depletes the vis. The effect has a
Penetration total of 11.

(Base 10, +1 Touch, +3 Moon, +3 Structure, +11 levels Penetration)
