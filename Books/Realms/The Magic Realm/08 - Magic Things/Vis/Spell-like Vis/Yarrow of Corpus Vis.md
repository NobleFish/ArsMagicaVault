CrCo Level 40

R: Touch, D: Mom T: Ind, Ritual

This Corpus vis takes the form of yarrow, which is also known as
soldier’s wort. When the leaves of the plant are crushed and applied to
a character’s wounds, they heal completely. The plant only heals
physical injuries and has no effect on Wound levels caused by disease or
poison, nor does it restore missing limbs.

(Base 35, +1 Touch)
