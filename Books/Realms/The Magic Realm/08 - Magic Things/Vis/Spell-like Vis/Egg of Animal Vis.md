CrAn Level 30

R: Touch, D: Mom, T: Group, Ritual

This Animal vis takes the form of a leathery snake egg. If the egg is
warmed to the temperature equivalent to a warm spring day for one hour,
fully-grown poisonous snakes emerge from the egg. A simple die + 14
determines the number of snakes that emerge.

(Base 10, +1 Touch, +2 Group, +1 Size)
