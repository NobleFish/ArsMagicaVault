The vis described above is more valuable than the typical forms of vis
characters encounter. A vis source that provides extraordinary or
dedicated vis should rarely produce more than a few such pawns per year.
In transactions involving special vis, the vis is proportionally more
valuable than normal vis. To determine the trade value of extraordinary
or dedicated vis, divide its cost in Build Points by 5. If an
extraordinary pawn of vis would require 20 build points, it is worth
four times as much as a normal pawn of vis, which only requires 5 build
Points.

The build points for extraordinary vis are variable depending on the
special powers of the vis. Vis that mimics Ritual spells is more
valuable than vis that simply mimics a normal spell, and vis that
provides merely cosmetic or annoying effects should cost little or no
more than normal vis.
