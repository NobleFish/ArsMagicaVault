InHe Level 14 Pen 0, constant effect

R: Touch, D: Sun, T: Group

The mistletoe grows on the oldest tree in the forest. If harvested
properly it contains Herbam vis. Anyone carrying the sprig of mistletoe
gains an intuitive understanding of the forest in which the mistletoe
grew. This grants a +3 to all nature-related rolls when the character is
in the forest.

(Effect: Base 1, + 1 Touch, +2 Sun, +2 Group; Modifications +1 2 uses
per day, +3 environmental trigger)
