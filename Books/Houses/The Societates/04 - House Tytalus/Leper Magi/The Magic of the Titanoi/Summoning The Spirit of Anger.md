ReMe 40

R: Arc, D: Conc, R: Ind

This spell calls a spirit of wrath to the magus’s current location, if
he has an Arcane Connection or knows its full name, and overcomes its
Magic Resistance. The name can be a non-magical name, and need not be a
magical True Name. A wrathful person serves as an Arcane Connection to
any spirit of anger; if he has a Major Personality Flaw such as
Wrathful, then the spirit has a Magic Might of 30. A Minor Personality
Flaw attracts a spirit of Might 20. Otherwise, the spirit has a Might
equal to (5 x Angry Personality Trait). Casting this spell infl icts a
Warping Point on the source of the anger, unless it is the caster
himself.

Similar spells exist for summoning the spirits of other emotions.

(Base 15 \[see *The Mysteries Revised Edition*, page 28\], +4 Arc, +1
Conc)
