MuIm 10

R: Touch, D: Sun, T: Ind

This spell is a variant of Aura of Ennobled Presence, except that it
causes the target to appear more guileless, gullible, and naïve. The
character gets a +3 to rolls to deflect suspicion or convince others of
his innocence. In a debate, the target receives a +3 to defenses based
on Guile. To those affected by the caster’s Gift, his apparent innocence
and unblemished honor appears sanctimonious and self-righteous.

*(Base 3, +1 Touch, +2 Sun)*
