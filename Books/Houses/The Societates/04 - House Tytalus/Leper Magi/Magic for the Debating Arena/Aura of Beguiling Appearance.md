MuIm 10

R: Touch, D: Sun, T: Ind

This spell is a variant of Aura of Ennobled Presence, except that it
causes the target to appear more friendly, conciliatory, and credible.
The character gets a +3 on rolls to win trust and make friends. In a
debate, the target receives a +3 to Defense Totals based on Charm. The
social penalties caused by The Gift are not negated by this spell; the
caster merely seems to be suspiciously over-friendly.

*(Base 3, +1 Touch, +2 Sun)*
