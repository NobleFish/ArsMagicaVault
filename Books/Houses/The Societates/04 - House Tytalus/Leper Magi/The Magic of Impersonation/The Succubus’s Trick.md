MuCo 5

R: Per, D: Sun, T: Ind

This spell causes a female caster to adopt male physical
characteristics. A similar spell (The Incubus’s Trick) turns a male
caster into a woman. Eye color, hair color, hairstyle, stature, build,
and similar characteristics are not changed by this spell, so anyone
familiar with the caster’s original appearance can easily recognize her
after the change (although the enchanted maga is sometimes instead
assumed to be her own “twin brother”). The names of these spells derive
from demons of lust who can change their gender.

*(Base 3, +2 Sun)*
