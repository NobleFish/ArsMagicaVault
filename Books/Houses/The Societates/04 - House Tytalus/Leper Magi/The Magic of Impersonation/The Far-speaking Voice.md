CrMe 20

R: Arc, D: Diam, T: Ind

The caster can deliver two minutes of conversation directly into the
mind of the person to whom he holds an Arcane Connection, although the
target cannot communicate back. This spell is often placed within an
enchantment (either a charged item or one with an effect which expires)
with the caster’s own Arcane Connection, allowing a spy to communicate
with him.

*(Base 3, +4 Arc, +1 Diam)*
