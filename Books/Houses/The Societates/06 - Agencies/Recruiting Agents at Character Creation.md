Some Virtues and Flaws give characters agencies prior to play. These
include Social Contacts, Mercenary Captain, Outlaw Leader, Close Family
Ties, and Landed Noble. An agency acquired in this way is created by
selecting agents from the Resistance of Agents Table, then paying agency
points for each equal to the desired Bond Strength plus the Resistance
Strength from the table. The player has 12 agency points to spend. Every
agent must cost at least 1 point, and have a Bond score of at least +1
