*Those wizards who do not (or cannot) adopt Hermetic magic upon joining
the Order are encouraged by the Lineage of Pralix to consider training
an apprentice in both the Hermetic and non-Hermetic traditions, and a
Pralician maga often volunteers to assist with this process in return
for the apprentice’s assistance in the laboratory. She begins by opening
the Arts for the Gifted child, but before he is taught any Hermetic
magic, he is inducted into the magic of his non-Hermetic parens. A
typical arrangement for the rest of the apprenticeship is that for every
season the Pralican spends teaching Hermetic magic to her colleague’s
apprentice, she gets two seasons of the apprentice’s assistance in the
laboratory. At the end of the apprenticeship, the apprentice is not too
dissimilar to the average member of House Ex Miscellanea in the Ars
Magica Fifth Edition rulebook, although he may be biased towards his
non-Hermetic parens and suffer from Minor Hermetic Flaws such as Weak
Parens.*
