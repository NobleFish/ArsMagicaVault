*The Lineage of Pralix has a number of spells that are targeted directly
at exotic magic. They have developed spells directed at suppressing,
altering, and dispelling shapeshifting magic, curse magic, spirit magic,
and so forth, which are more effective than general purpose spells such
as Wind of Mundane Silence. Their initial encounters with hedge wizards
tend to be hostile, and they like to be well prepared against any
aggressive actions.*

*Apart from their obvious fascination with exotic magic, the Lineage of
Pralix have no defined magical interests, and each member is left to
pursue whatever she desires. Her self-imposed duties often see her
journeying deep into dangerous territory to face beings of unknown
power, so she often knows spells that assist travel and provide
protection.*
