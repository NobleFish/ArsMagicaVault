*In ancient Thrace there lived a poet and musician beloved of gods and
men. He is the root of dozens of myths and hundreds of stories. He was
an artist, augur, and Argonaut. Orpheus was a master musician and
magician, said by some to have been taught these twin arts by Hermes
himself. His followers suggest that this makes him the first user of
“Hermetic” magic in history.*
