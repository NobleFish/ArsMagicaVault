*Simple Duties: Cleansing or protecting the jinni’s home, guarding a
human family to whom the jinni has become attached, retrieving stolen
wealth, writing a poem in praise of the jinn, providing rare commodities
such as frankincense or rubies.*

*Onerous Duties: Fighting wizards who seek to enslave jinn, freeing
slaves from captivity, playing a jest on an important local figure,
delivering the heart of an enemy, procuring a unique item such as a book
written in Solomon’s own hand.*
