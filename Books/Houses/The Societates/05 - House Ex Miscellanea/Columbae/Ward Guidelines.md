*Here are the various guidelines for warding the different Forms in Ars
Magica Fifth Edition. All of them are presented at Range Touch, Duration
Ring, and Target: Circle, since Warding always uses those same
parameters.*

*Since Hermetic wards must penetrate a being’s Magic Resistance to be
effective, it is a good idea to note the caster’s Penetration Total in
addition to the level of the spell. Even with spells that are designed
to affect mundane things, it is possible that the character could
encounter a special type of target that can resist the effect. For
example, a spell designed to ward away animals will not affect a
Bjornaer magus in animal form unless it penetrates his Parma Magica.*
