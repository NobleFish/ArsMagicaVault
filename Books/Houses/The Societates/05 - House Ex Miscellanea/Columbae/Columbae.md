*The swynwyr (pronounced “SWINweer;” singular swynwr, “SWIN-oor”;
feminine singular swynwraig, “swin-OOR-aig;” plural swynwragedd,
“swin-oor-RAG-edth”) are Welsh hedge wizards well known for their skill
at magical wards. Because this requires them to leave markings wherever
they go, they have become known within the Order of Hermes as the
Columbae, literally, “pigeons.”. In some cases this nickname is
affectionately meant, while in others it is clearly derogatory. They are
also sometimes called the “ward-makers” or the “white-nailed,” the
latter term in reference to their use of chalk with most of their
spells.*
