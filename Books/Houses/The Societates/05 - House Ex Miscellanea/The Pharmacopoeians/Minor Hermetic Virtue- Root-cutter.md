*The Root-Cutter Virtue is a Minor, Hermetic Virtue. A character with
the RootCutter Virtue is able to collect and prepare magical ingredients
from plant, animal, and mineral components that reduce the vis cost of
healing Rituals. The magus may reduce the vis cost of a healing Ritual
by his score in Mythic Herbalism, but the cost may never be reduced by
more than half.*

*Specialties: healing wounds, curing diseases, resolving aging crises.
(Hermetic)*
