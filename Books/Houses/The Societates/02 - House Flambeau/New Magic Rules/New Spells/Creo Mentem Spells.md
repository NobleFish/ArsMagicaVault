**Heart of the Lion**

CrMe 15

R: Eye, D: Sun, T: Ind

Instill a person with indomitable courage, increasing his Brave
Personality Trait by +3. This may, but does not always, lead to acts of
recklessness. If you cast this spell on your grogs, be advised that they
will lose their fear of you as well as their fear of the enemy.

(Base 4, +1 Eye, +2 Sun)
