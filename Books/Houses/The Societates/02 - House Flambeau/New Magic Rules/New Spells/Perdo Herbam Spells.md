**Tree Falling in the Forest**

Pe(Re)He 35

R: Sight, D: Mom, T: Ind

Instantly cuts down a tree, making it fall in the direction the caster
designates. With a successful aiming roll, you can make it fall on a
living creature. Damage is up to +18 for a mature tree, and the victim
or victims may also be pinned by the tree’s weight. When used as a form
of attack, this spell bypasses Magic Resistance.

(Base 5, +3 Sight, +2 size, +1 Rego requisite)
