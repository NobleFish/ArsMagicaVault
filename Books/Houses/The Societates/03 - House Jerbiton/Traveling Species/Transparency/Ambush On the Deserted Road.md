PeIm 20

R: Touch, D: Ring, T: Circle

This spell destroys all of the species that the group of things within
the Circle emit in a single direction. It can — for example — make a
magus’s traveling party invisible from the direction of another group
they wish to ambush. The base level of this spell includes an adjustment
that accounts for the magus’s ability to gently move the direction of
effect, so that as the enemy group passes by, they cannot spot the
hidden party. Other people, for example the members of the magus’s
company, can still see each other, because the species reaching their
eyes are unobstructed. The transparency requires a Perception + Finesse
roll against an Ease Factor of 9 to be convincing.

(Base 5, +1 Touch, +2 Ring)
