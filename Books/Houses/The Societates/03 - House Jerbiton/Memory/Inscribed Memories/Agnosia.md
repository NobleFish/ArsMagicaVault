PeMe 10 R: Eye, D: Sun T: Ind

This spell destroys familiarity with a category of object represented by
a single noun. The victim of this spell cannot recognize instances of
the thing, although she is that aware such things exist. If confronted
with a representative object, the victim cannot identify or use it. This
spell does not destroy Abilities, so if the character is placed in a
position where she uses an object without conscious monitoring, her
Abilities return. This can be alarming for the spell’s target, since she
cannot consciously use the tool to repeat her inadvertent actions.

(Base 3, +1 Eye, +2 Sun) \|}
