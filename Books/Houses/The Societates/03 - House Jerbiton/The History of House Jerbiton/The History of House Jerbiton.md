Jerbiton was born in a remote village in the Alps, but taken as an
apprentice by a maga from the Imperial East. His tradition’s ancestors
are the cosmopolitan magi from the cities of Greece. Jerbiton was sent
as an emissary to the forming Order, but for many years the other
members of his tradition did not join it. House Jerbiton still feels
that the Eastern Empire is its heartland, and has been stunned and
angered by the fall of Constantinople in 1204. A new generation of
apprentices, trained after the pillaging of the greatest of cities, is
about to enter magushood. They represent a new way of being Jerbiton
magi.
