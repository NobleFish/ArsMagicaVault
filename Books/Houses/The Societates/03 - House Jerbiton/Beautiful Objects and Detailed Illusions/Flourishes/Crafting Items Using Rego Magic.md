The table below is designed to assist players of magi to create spells
that make items from raw materials, using the Art of Rego. These levels
are correct for all spells that make items of Individual size, at Touch
Range, with Momentary Duration. The beauty of these items depends on a
Finesse roll, described in an adjoining section.

| Raw Material                | Level |
|-----------------------------|-------|
| Bone (animal)               | 3     |
| Gemstone                    | 4     |
| Glass                       | 3     |
| Leather (tanned)            | 1     |
| Metal                       | 4     |
| Stone                       | 4     |
| Timber (dried and prepared) | 1     |
| Wood (unprepared timber)    | 5     |
| Wool (prepared)             | 1     |
| Wool (raw)                  | 3     |
