A magus creating a natural object using Creo magic always makes a
functional version of that object, provided the spell is not botched.
The magus may use an Intelligence + Finesse roll to add detail, making
the creation particularly beautiful or suited to a task. An illusion of
an object, natural or otherwise, always looks like a functional example
of the object, but the magus may make Intelligence + Finesse roll to add
detail.

When creating artificial objects, the same level of Finesse is required
as when using Rego to make them from raw materials.
