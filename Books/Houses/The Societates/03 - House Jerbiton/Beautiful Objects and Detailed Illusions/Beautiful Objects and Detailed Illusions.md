The training in the mundane arts given to some magi assists them when
they are creating intricate magical effects. Usually, Hermetic magi form
a vague mental image of their desired effect. Their magic then fills in
many of the details of the finished shape, based on the Form of the
creation. The magus then consciously alters the outcome, represented by
the use of the Finesse Ability. The final product is also infused with
the magus’s sigil.
