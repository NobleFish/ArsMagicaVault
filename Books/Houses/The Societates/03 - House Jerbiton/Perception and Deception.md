The Founder was skilled in magic that altered perception. His tradition
continues, alongside those of many others who have joined the House
since its beginnings. Perception is a complex process, which makes it
fragile, and easily manipulated, at each of its stages, by magi. The
process of distorting the perceptions of others is called deception, and
members of House Jerbiton claim to be its masters, even though skilled
practitioners of deception can be found in many Houses.
