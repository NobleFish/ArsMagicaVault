Jerbiton magi believe that it is the duty of magi to dress in a way that
allows other people to recognize, and respond to, their status. This
prevents magi from being treated rudely by accident, making life more
pleasant for everyone. It also allows magi who do not wish their status
to be discerned to hide more easily, by removing the symbols associated
with magi.
