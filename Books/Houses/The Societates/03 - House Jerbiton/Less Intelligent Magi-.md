Sagas vary concerning the prevalence of The Gift. In sagas where The
Gift is rare, and mundane hostility to magi is acute, House Jerbiton’s
magi may select apprentices with serious Flaws, provided they have the
Gentle Gift. As an example, the usual Intelligence score for magi
described in earlier supplements is +3. An Intelligence score less than
+3 reflects a premium placed upon Gentle Giftedness by masters in House
Jerbiton. As a counterbalance, these apprentices are often healthier and
more charismatic than their sodales from other Houses.
