To many magi, a familiar is the closest relationship they will ever
have. Separation for a familiar is analogous to separation from a wife
or child. The emotional impact of this punishment should not be
underestimated.

The cords connecting magus and familiar must be cut. This is an
extremely painful and emotionally scarring experience for both parties.
Quaesitors have a ritual spell that achieves this and it is the
Presiding Quaesitor’s unpleasant duty to perform the ritual if requested
(the convicted magus must provide the vis).

Alternatively the process can be achieved in a season of lab activity.
To verify this, the magus needs to submit to magical investigation at
the next Tribunal.

After the cords have been cut, both magus and former familiar ﬁnd each
other’s presence too much to bear. A familiar normally ﬂees into the
wilderness and avoids all future contact with magi. The former familiar
cannot normally be rebound.
