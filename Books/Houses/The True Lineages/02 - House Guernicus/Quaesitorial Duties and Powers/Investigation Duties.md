A Quaesitor in good standing has a duty to investigate serious
complaints formally presented to him. It is up to the Quaesitor to judge
which complaints are serious. He may ask for more compelling evidence
from a complainant before embarking on a full investigation.

He is also entitled to at least three seasons every year to pursue his
own studies or attend to covenant responsibilities. This entitlement can
be accrued and so a Quaesitor may make himself unavailable to
investigate complaints for extended lab work. If he is unavailable he
can pass the investigation to another or simply give the complainant the
authority to investigate.
