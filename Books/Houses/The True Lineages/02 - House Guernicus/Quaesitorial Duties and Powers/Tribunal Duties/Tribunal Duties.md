Quaesitors perform a number of functions at Tribunal. The most important
is the Presiding Quaesitor, but there are other roles. The Presiding
Quaesitor has two advisers, who are consulted on all signiﬁcant
decisions. Quaesitors can also be asked to endorse testimony, which
attests to its truthfulness.
