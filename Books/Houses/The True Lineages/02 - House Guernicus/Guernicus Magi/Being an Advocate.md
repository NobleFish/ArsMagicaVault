Some Guernicus magi feel that they can serve the Order best by acting as
a legal advocate. These magi take cases where principles feel threatened
or are otherwise unable to conduct their case. Their fees are generally
affordable even for poor magi.

They also work to resolve disputes out of court, acting for one side but
able to broker agreements between otherwise extremely hostile parties.
Ideally Guernicus advocates work to bring peace and security to the
Order. They stand between the weak and the strong and ensure justice is
done.
