Occasionally a Guernicus magus finds his magic and temperament more
suited to martial magic than investigative work. This predisposition is
well respected in the House, as it recalls the passionate spirit of
Guernicus. Quaesitors may well feel themselves under threat during an
investigation, and in such cases they can call on allies to add magical
muscle to the inquiry. If a magus acquires a reputation for taking on
this duty, he will become generally known as a Hoplite.

A number of Guernicus magi are able at both investigation and combat.
These Hoplite Quaesitors are often sent to the more lawless Tribunals,
well-suited to their robust investigations.
