Originally there was only one Tribunal in the Order. However, in 773 AD
this Tribunal constituted the regional Tribunals and the Grand Tribunal.
Scholars of the Code of Hermes refer to the original Tribunal as the
First Tribunal. The First Tribunal met in 767 AD to form the Order and
in 773 AD to form the regional Tribunals and the Grand Tribunal. The
distinction between the First Tribunal and the Grand Tribunal is a
legally important one.

However, this importance is lost on many magi. It is common practice to
count the meetings of the Grand Tribunal from the earliest First
Tribunal, rather than the meeting of 799 AD. Quaesitors who take issue
with this are often accused of semantic pedantry. \|}
