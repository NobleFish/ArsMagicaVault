Many Quaesitors employ worldly-wise custos to act as eyes and ears in
the mundane world, to complement the diligent Redcaps. Many are
merchants who ply their trade about the Tribunal.

Those who supply specialist equipment and materials needed by Hermetic
magi are often recruited by Quaesitors to keep an eye on their clients.
This is done in the greatest of secrecy and the merchants are under
strict instructions not to raise any suspicion. They merely pick up turb
gossip and note the things they witness. If asked directly the merchant
will say he is under the protection of his Quaesitorial master. When
confronted Quaesitors will simply point out the beneﬁts of ensuring
merchants serving the Order are under Quaesitorial care.

Occasionally, but rarely, a mundane agent has been placed within a
covenant as a spy. If a covenant is known to screen its recruits, the
subject’s memory is altered to defeat standard Intellego Mentem
screening. This use of magic is covered under investigation immunity.
