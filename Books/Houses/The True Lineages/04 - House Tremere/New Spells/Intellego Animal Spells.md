<span id="TO SEE AS OTHERS SEE"></span>TO SEE AS OTHERS SEE

R: Arcane, D: Sun, T: Ind, Level 40 Requisite: Rego

This spell allows a magus to read the thoughts of an animal, and give it
directions. This allows the magus to indirectly sense the animal’s
location, using it as a spy. The magus’s perceptions are ﬁltered through
the mind of the animal, so they focus on those stimuli that the animal
ﬁnds most vivid. Dogs ﬁnd smell vivid, so the magus’s perceptions, when
using a dog, will focus on odiferous substances. A magus reading the
mind of a magpie ﬁnds that they ﬁxate on reﬂective surfaces.

(Base 5, +4 Arcane Connection, +2 Sun, +1 extra effect from requisite.)
