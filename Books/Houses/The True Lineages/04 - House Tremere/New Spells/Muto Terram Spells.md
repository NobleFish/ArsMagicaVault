<span id="A WINDOW OF SINGULAR DIRECTION"></span>A WINDOW OF SINGULAR
DIRECTION

R: Touch, D: Ring, T: Part, Level 20

This spell, created by the Architects of Tremere, makes a circle of wall
transparent, from one side only. It was developed in the early years of
the Order, to allow magi protected by temporary fortiﬁcations to target
their foes.

(Base 4, +1 Part, +2 Ring)
