<span id="LOVE’S UNFAITHFUL WITNESS"></span>''LOVE’S UNFAITHFUL WITNESS

R: Touch, D: Conc, T: Ind, Level 35

This spell is used to question jewelry about its wearer’s activities.
The sympathies of the largest gemstone determine the personality of a
piece of jewelry. Rubies tend to be bloodthirsty, garnets sanguine and
so on, as represented by the Materials Table in Ars Magica (page 110).
Unadorned silver is treacherous and unadorned gold is pious, judgmental
and accusatory.

(Base 25, +1 Touch, +1 Conc)''
