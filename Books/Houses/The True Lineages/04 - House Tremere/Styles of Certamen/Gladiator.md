The School of the Swordsman is the style of the Founder Tremere,
reﬂected in the basic rules. It is a balanced, simple style, for which
many teachers are available. Rumors persist that there are a series of
secret techniques for this school, allowing dedicated Followers to can
spend vis to greater effect than other magi. The Tremere treat these
rumors as an attack on their integrity — if other magi lost faith in the
certamen system, it would disadvantage the House. The shared illusions
of the School of the Swordsman tend to be humaniform and are heavily
inﬂuenced by the sigil of the magus.
