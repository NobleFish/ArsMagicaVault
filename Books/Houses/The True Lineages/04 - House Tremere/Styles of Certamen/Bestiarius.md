Employed only by members of House Bjornaer, the techniques of the School
of Beasts allows them to use their Heartbeast score instead of their
Finesse score during duels in the Muto form, or involving their own
bodies. In Muto Corpus or Muto Animal duels, they can add twice their
Heartbeast score to their Resistance Total. Bjornaers who dabble in the
Bestiarus technique add their Heartbeast score to their Resistance.
