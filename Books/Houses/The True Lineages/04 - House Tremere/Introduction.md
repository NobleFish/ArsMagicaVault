# House Tremere

*Words fail. He failed. Enough! He is gone. He wanted to be feared, but
for love of him we are all now hated. We must begin again, exhausted and
friendless. This is his legacy: hatred, and us. You will not love or
fear me as you loved or feared him, but in this place, beyond mortal
dissimulation, I tell you this: we are all he has left for any of us.*

*— The Eulogy for Tremere, spoken by Primus Albanus, beyond the Gate to
Hades*

Symbol: Mars incised with a square

Motto: Voluntas vincit omnia. (The Will conquers all.)''

Members of House Tremere assume that the world is chaotic and dangerous.
They strive to control events and prepare for inevitable, future
battles. They cultivate inﬂuence and acquire resources to respond to
emerging crises. They settle internal arguments with non-fatal duels.
Tremere magi are pragmatic, dutiful and courageous.''
