Tremere magi expect to carry their own gear, so they prefer small
magical items, crafted out of expensive materials. Many members of the
House believe that Tremere invented the wand as a more manageable
version of the staff. Tremere magi like rings, for their portability and
because they can be comfortably worn while sleeping. Pouches that allow
a magus to carry a campaign’s worth of food on his belt and ﬂasks that
draw water from nearby clouds are popular. Sacks that reduce the
bulkiness of bedding and camping gear are valuable.
