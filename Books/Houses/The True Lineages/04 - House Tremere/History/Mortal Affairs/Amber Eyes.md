The Tremere returned sight to over a thousand blinded Bulgars with a
single ritual, since this was the least expensive method. The new eyes
the Bulgars grew were colored amber, the sigil of the maga who cast the
ritual. Many of the covenfolk in Transylvania have inherited this eye
color, which deepens with Warping. Children with these eyes are often
named for the blind bard Thamyris, and are more likely to be homosexual
than other characters.
