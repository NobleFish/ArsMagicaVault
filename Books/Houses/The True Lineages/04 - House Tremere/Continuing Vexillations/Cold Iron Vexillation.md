Task: To settle disputes with faeries to the advantage of the House.
Each member of this vexillation has faerie blood, is a competent
soldier, and a skilled diplomat. It maintains a small force of
auxiliaries, trained to ﬁght faeries.

This vexillation is always led by a masked ﬁgure called the Epicurean.
Presumably the mask has changed wearers over time, but in Arcadia, the
Epicurean is, in a very real sense, a continuing entity.
