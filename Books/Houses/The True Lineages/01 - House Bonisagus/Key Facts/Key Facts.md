**Population**: 81 (52 magi Bonisagi, 29 magi Trianomae)

**Domus Magna**: Durenmar in the Rhine tribunal.

**Prima**: The archmage Murion. An unscrupulous schemer and staunch
conservative, Murion leads the House along an ambitious path that will,
if successful, re-establish House Bonisagus’s power among the Houses.
**Favored Tribunals**: The Rhine and Roman Tribunals, followed by the
Iberian and Provencal.
