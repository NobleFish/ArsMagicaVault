The House has two councils of magi charged with maintaining the smooth
functioning of the House. The ﬁrst is the Colentes Arcanorum, a group of
magi Bonisagi who are responsible for the collection, compilation, and
dissemination of magical lore, theoretical practices, and arcane
breakthroughs. Equally important are the Tenentes Occultorum, a group of
magi Trianomae who ensure that those same secrets stay within the Order
of Hermes. They are also the magi Trianomae who hover around the Order’s
borders, eagerly searching out other magical powers and practitioners.
