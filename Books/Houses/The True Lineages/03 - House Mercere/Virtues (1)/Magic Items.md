*Minor, General*

*You begin with 25 more starting levels of magic items than you would
otherwise, and the rate at which your items are improved is increased by
one level per year. This is probably because of your exceptional
devotion to the House, or because you have inherited a number of items
from other Redcaps. You must be a Redcap to take this virtue, and you
may take it more than once, though no single effect in any of your items
can be greater than Level 30.*
