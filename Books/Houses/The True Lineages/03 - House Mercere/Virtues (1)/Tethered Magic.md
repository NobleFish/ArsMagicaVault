''Minor, Hermetic

You can pass control of your non-Ritual spells to others, just as if
they were the caster, “tethering” the magic to them for the spell’s
duration. You may also tether a spell to an object, which can then
transfer the spell to an appropriate target when it comes into range.
This can even be done whenever you activate an effect in a magic item.
However, a side effect of this sort of magic is that all of your spells
and the effects of any magic items you activate are arcane connections
to you.''
