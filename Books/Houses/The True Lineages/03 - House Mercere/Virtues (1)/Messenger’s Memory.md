''Minor, Heroic

You can remember, word for word, any short messages you have heard or
read in the last year. You must concentrate when you ﬁrst read or hear
the message, but afterwards you can recite or copy it exactly, and you
can even recall personalized details like inﬂection and handwriting,
though you may not be able to perfectly reproduce these nuances. While
you might recall excerpts of longer works, like an hour-long speech or a
book, it is impossible for you to memorize more than a few choice
passages.''
