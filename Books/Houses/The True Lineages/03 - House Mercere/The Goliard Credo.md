*I believe — in dice I well believe, That got me often bite and sup, And
many a time hath had me drunk, And many a time delivered me From every
stitch and every penny. \[...\] To drink and wench and play at dice Seem
to me no such mighty sins... Never man I know descended into Hell for a
game. Ask thou something else of me... \[...\] I believe in wine that’s
fair to see, And in a barrel of my host More than in the Holy Ghost The
tavern is my sweetheart, yea, And Holy Church is not for me. \[...\]
Amen. Priest, I now am through with’t. Through with life. Death hath its
pain. Too much... Too much... This agony — I’m dying. I to God commend
you. I ask it of you — Pray for me.* *— From the Goliards’ “Credo au
Ribaut”*
