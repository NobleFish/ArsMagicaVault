''(PHYSICAL CHARACTERISTIC) OF THE HEROES

R: Touch, D: Mom, T: Ind, Level 60, Ritual

Each of the four variations of this ritual permanently increases one of
the subject’s physical Characteristics (Strength, Stamina, Dexterity or
Quickness) by one, to no more than +5. During the ceremony, the subject

one step closer to human perfection.
