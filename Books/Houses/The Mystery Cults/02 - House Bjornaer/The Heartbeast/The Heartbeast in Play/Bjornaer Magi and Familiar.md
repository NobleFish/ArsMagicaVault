The final consequence of being Initiated into the Outer Mystery of House
Bjornaer is the inability to bind a familiar. The Ritual of Twelve
Years, like other Mysteries of Initiation, leaves a scar on the soul of
the Initiated magus; Birna, having no personal desire to bond a
familiar, considered it to be an acceptable loss when she invented the
ritual. Any magus who has ever bound a familiar, even if it has since
died, cannot successfully complete the Ritual of Twelve Years, and so
cannot join the House.
