The domus magna of House Bjornaer is Crintera, situated on the island of
Rügen in the Baltic Sea, just off the northern coast of Germany. Its
permanent members are the six members of the Bjornaer Council. The
covenant proper is situated in a regio in the middle of a magical
forest. As well as the six huts of the Bjornaer Council, the top level
of the regio has a large meeting area where the Gathering of Twelve
Years is held. More information about Crintera and the members of the
