- **Birna:** Gothic witch and Founder of the House.
- **Hérisson:** Founder of the Harmonist philosophy; unappreciated in
  his time.
- **Salmo:** Primus following the Schism War who refocused the House in
  a more traditional Hermetic mould.
- **Urgen:** Previous primus, a direct and sometimes violent magus who
  resigned in favor of Falke.
