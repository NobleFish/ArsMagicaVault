Minor Virtue, beasts only

Like companion and magus characters, a beast with this Virtue has Confi
dence points. However, its Confi dence points may only be used in
situations where its natural animal ferocity is triggered, such as when
defending its den or fi ghting a natural enemy. For each instance of
this Virtue, describe a situation that activates Confi dence for the
species in question, and take 3 Confi dence Points and a Confi dence
Score of 1 to use when those circumstances are met.
