Mental Characteristics of Creatures

A creature may have the Minor Virtue Increased Characteristics one or
more times (particularly appropriate for predators), just like a human
character. The Characteristic points gained from this Virtue may be
applied to any of the physical Characteristics. These points should be
spent before applying the adjustments for Size. See step 3, below, for
adjustments to this Virtue.
