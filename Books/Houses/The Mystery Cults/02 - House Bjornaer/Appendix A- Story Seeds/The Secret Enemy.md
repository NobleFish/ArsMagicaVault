The Gothic shapeshifters gain a charismatic chieftain who unites some of
the tribes under one banner. This leader becomes sufficiently motivated
to try to eradicate House Bjornaer, possibly due to actions performed or
witnessed by the player characters. These people do everything in their
power to wipe out every last one of the Bjornaer followers, even at the
expense of their own lives. They are magically weak compared to Hermetic
magi, but they are physically strong, highly loyal to each other, and
fanatical in their task. More about the Pomeranian witches can be found
