These are the Mysteries that were allegedly discovered by Quendalon, and
which were responsible for the dramatic change in focus for his House.
They concern faeries and the Faerie realm, making it possible for magi
to travel through Faerie, summon faeries from Arcadia into the physical
world, and to actually become faeries themselves.
