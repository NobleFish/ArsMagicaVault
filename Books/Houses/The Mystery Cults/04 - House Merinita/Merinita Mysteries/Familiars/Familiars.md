Merinita introduced the ritual that allows a maga to bond with her
familiar, and many of her followers have developed a special affinity
for them. In fact, within the House it is far more respected to take a
familiar than it is to train an apprentice, and those who do the latter
before the former tend to be looked down upon by their peers. Merinitae
who are interested in this direction might take a Major Magical Focus
with Familiars, which applies to all spells and laboratory activities
that affect familiars, either their own or those belonging to other
magi.
