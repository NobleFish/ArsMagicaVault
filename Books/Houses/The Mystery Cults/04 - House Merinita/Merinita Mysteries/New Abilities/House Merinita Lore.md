This is the Ability that Merinitae use to Initiate their House
Mysteries, and thus it is the one thing that they all have in common. It
represents knowledge of all the secret rites, mysteries, and legends
associated with the House, including all three schools of Faerie Magic
Mysteries, and even the nature Mysteries taught by Merinita to her
followers before she disappeared. Magi who wish to Initiate the Inner
Mysteries should have at least a score of 1, and Mystagogues of the many
different House cults typically have scores of 4 or 5.

**Specialties:** Initiating others, Initiating self, modifying an
Initiation Script (Arcane)
