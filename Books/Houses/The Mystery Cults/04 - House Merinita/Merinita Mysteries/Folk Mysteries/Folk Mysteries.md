The common people have collected a great deal of wisdom about faeries
over the years, and Merinitae have adapted that folklore into a unique
set of Mysteries. These explore faerie magic associated with charms (see
above), including charms that focus spells on particular targets, charms
that invoke archetypal events, and charms that harness the power of
symbols and sympathetic magic in the lab.
