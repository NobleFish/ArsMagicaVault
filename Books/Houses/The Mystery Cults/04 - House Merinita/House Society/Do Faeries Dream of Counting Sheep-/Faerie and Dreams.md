Many Merinitae believe that there is a connection to Arcadia in every
person’s mind. This is because they think dreams somehow influence and
are influenced by the Faerie realm, which they consider to be the
collective dreams of all humanity. Thus, faeries may occasionally be
found to inhabit a person’s imagination, accessible with Mentem magic
and the specialized <a
href="The_Mysteries:_Revised_Edition_Chapter_Twelve:_The_Mysteries_of_Dreams"
class="wikilink" title="Dream Virtues">Dream Virtues</a>.

Characters with the Merinita Mystery Arcadian Travel can open trods into
Arcadia through their dreams, if they wish. The maga produces a charm
designed to lull the travelers to sleep, and they then journey to a
dream-based Arcadia in spirit instead of flesh. While away, sleeping
characters do not age or suffer ill effects of going without food or
water, though they can die through other means — and if they do, their
spirits remain in Faerie.
