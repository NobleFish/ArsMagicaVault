Gods and goddesses of the ancient world can be associated with either
the Faerie or Magical realms, though those that have a magical aspect
tend to be focused on themselves more than on their worshipers, and many
of them are thought to have taken physical forms or retreated far from
human society. Those who still seek followers and have an interest in
mortal affairs are considered powerful faeries, and it may be difficult
to distinguish them from other great lords and ladies of the faerie
courts. Still, dedicated ceremonies designed to gain their attention and
favor could plausibly have an effect, and many pagan Merinitae have
claimed to have the blessings of their ancient deities at one time or
another.
