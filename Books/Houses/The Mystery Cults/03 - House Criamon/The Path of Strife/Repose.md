Followers of the Path of Strife cannot ascend into Final Twilight. They
are so impure that if they attempt to do so, they die and create
terrible adulterations. Many choose, near the end of their lives (or as
a Twilight experience begins), to use the Golden Cider upon themselves.
They relinquish The Gift and choose to die, beginning a new cycle of
redemptive reincarnations. Many believe this selfl ess act makes good
much of the fault of their lives.
