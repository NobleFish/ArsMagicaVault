A key fi gure in some Mystery Cults is the Mystagogue. This charismatic
person commands a probationer, and grants him Initiation. Criamon magi
believe that wisdom itself Initiates, and that the traveler on the path
to wisdom does not need a master so much as he needs a guide. The game
mechanics governing the roles of guide and Mystagogue involve the same
numbers and formulae. The roles differ in how the characters behave in
stories. Criamon magi have a non-hierarchical approach to spiritual
development. Tasks based on impressing the guide are not usually signifi
cant — one cannot impress the Enigma.

Two exceptions are the Paths of the Mirror and Strife, which do have
formal Initiation by superiors. The Path of the Mirror, followed only by
the living Prima under the instruction of her predecessors, is a series
of Initiations for those who choose to serve the worldly House. The Path
of Strife transgresses the mores of the House.
