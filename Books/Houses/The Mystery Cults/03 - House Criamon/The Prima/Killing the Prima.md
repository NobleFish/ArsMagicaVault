When the Prima feels close to Final Twilight, the members of House
Criamon gather at the Cave of Twisting Shadows to kill her. A procession
wends to the ninth level of the regio, where a non-Criamon ceremonially
murders her. Her ghost then fl oats free and announces its intention not
to pass through the gate into the Hypostasis. It chooses instead to stay
in the mortal world to tend the House until the Enigma is answered.

Those magi present who have an Enigmatic Wisdom score of 3 or more then
enter into a communal trace fi lled with incomprehensible images. When
one believes the images indicate that he should accept the role of
Primus, he stands and announces this to be so. The magus is acclaimed by
his sodales. The only other formality of investiture is sending letters
to Criamon magi unable to attend the ritual, and to the Primi of the
other Houses.

The House tries to limit the time between the death and ascension of
Primi. They believe the Hypostasis is inaccessible when there is no
living Primus. Thus, they believe that magi who have fallen into
temporary Twilight cannot return while the offi ce is vacant, and that
magi whose circumstances would usually force them into Final Twilight
cannot shelter there during such periods; they simply die.
