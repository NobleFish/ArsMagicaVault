The threshold of repose on the Path of Walking Backwards grants no
immunity to Twilight. However, magi who abstain from all use of magic
and stay out of powerful supernatural auras can avoid entering Twilight
by avoiding gaining two or more Warping Points at once.

Islamic magi tend to dwell on the threshold because they believe they
have an appointed task to complete. Their Sufi faith is based on the
love of God, not on the rewards he offers after death, so they see no
hurry to die. They just live on in the simple way approved by their
faith, perhaps for centuries, until they complete their assigned task.
