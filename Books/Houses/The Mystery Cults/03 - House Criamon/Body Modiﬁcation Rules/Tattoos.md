Tattoos do not have wide favor in Mythic Europe. The Book of Leviticus
states that having tattoos is a sin, and the Romans used them to mark
slaves and criminals. Pilgrims, however, are beginning to popularize
tattoos. A cross inside the wrist, made by the Coptic priests of
Jerusalem, is seen as proof that a pilgrim completed his journey.
