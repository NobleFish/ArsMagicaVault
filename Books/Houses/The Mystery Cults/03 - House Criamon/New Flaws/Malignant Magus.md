*Minor, Story, Supernatural*

A piece of the magus’s psyche has been broken loose by a Twilight
experience, and now manifests physically as an adulteration. This allows
the magus to be a pleasant and upstanding person, because his negative
impulses do terrible things out of the magus’s sight. Eventually the
character’s adulteration must be resolved, but in the interim, his
enemies are attacked be a magical monster of mysterious origin, without
the magus’s conscious consent or approval.
