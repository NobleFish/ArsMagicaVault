Magi on this path do not find repose. They simply cease to distinguish
between Twilight and reality, and so fade away, like illusions. Some
have suggested that these magi have withdrawn into prolonged, temporary
Twilight, and will return to the world when they wish, probably to hear
the answer when it is discovered, but any of them might, theoretically,
return at any time.

Some magi of this path flee death or Twilight by wandering through an
infinite number of memories in moments, living untold lifetimes in mere
weeks. These magi descend into comas and eventually float into Final
Twilight. What happens to the minds of these magi afterward is unclear,
but many suggest that they will live in psychodrama until awakened by
the answer to the Enigma.
