Criamon magi do not all agree on a single cosmology, but the view
presented in this chapter is typical. Magi from other Houses, it should
be noted, do not agree with Criamon beliefs. The House’s view on how the
universe really works colors the reactions of its members to the
representatives of the realms that they encounter during stories.

Criamon magi have encountered many strange states of being, and
conjectured the existence of many more. They use the term alam for
mystical states that are so divorced from the mundane world that their
alignment to the four realms is debatable. Two alams, the Hypostasis and
counter-cyclical alam, play fundamental roles in Criamon cosmology. \|}
