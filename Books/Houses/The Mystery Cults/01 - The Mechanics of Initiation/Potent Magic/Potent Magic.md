Potent Magic, a Hermetic Virtue available in Major and Minor versions,
was described in
<a href="The_Mysteries:_Revised_Edition" class="wikilink"
title="The Mysteries: Revised Edition">The Mysteries: Revised
Edition</a>, but is reproduced here as it is also used by some of the
Mysteries in this book.

The Potent Magic Virtue describes how a maga’s magic is particularly
attuned to a narrow field, much as in a Magical Focus. The benefits of
Potent Magic are compatible with a Magical Focus; unlike a Magical
Focus, a magus may have more than one area of Potent Magic, though only
one Potent Magic Virtue applies to any single activity.

Potent Magic provides a maga with a bonus in her field of magic, and
permits her to devise Potent spells, which gain a casting bonus from the
sympathetic magic in shapes and materials.
