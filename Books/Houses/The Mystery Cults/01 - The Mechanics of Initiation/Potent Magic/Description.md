Minor Potent Magic covers the same narrow fields as a Minor Magical
Focus, and grants a +3 bonus to Lab Totals and Casting Scores.

Major Potent Magic covers the same, wider, fields as a Major Magical
Focus, and grants a +6 bonus to Lab Totals and Casting Scores.

A maga with Potent Magic may also invent new Potent spells (see below)
within the field of her Potent Magic, and may vary spells she knows. See
Potent Magic and Potent Spells, below.
