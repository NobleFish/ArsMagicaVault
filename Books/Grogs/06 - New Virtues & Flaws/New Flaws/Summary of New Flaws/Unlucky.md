*Minor, General*

The character is exceptionally unlucky, and is frequently dealt a bad
hand by the forces of chance. He gets a –1 to –3 penalty (at
storyguide's discretion) on rolls in situations based on luck rather
than skill or talent, depending on how much luck is involved. It would
be wise for him not to take part in games of chance.
