*Minor, General*

This character has an affinity with one type of terrain, whether this is
at sea, in the woods, the rolling hills, or in the urban landscape of
towns and cities, and feels uncomfortable and awkward when away from
where he wants to be. The character suffers a –1 penalty to all stress
rolls taken outside of their favored terrain and also rolls one more
botch dice than normal in those situations.

Appropriate areas may be Cities & Towns, Villages, Rivers, The Sea,
Mountains, Woodland, Desert, etc.

This Flaw may only be taken once, because taking it more than once makes
it less serious, rather than more.
