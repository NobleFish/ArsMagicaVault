*Minor, General*

The character suffers from an old injury that won't heal properly,
resulting in a –1 penalty to all rolls involving physical activity. If
he botches a physical action, he aggravates the injury and the penalty
becomes –3 (recovery from aggravation is as for a Light Wound). This
condition worsens with age, so multiply whatever the penalty is by 1 +
(Decrepitude Score).
