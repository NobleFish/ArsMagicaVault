*Minor, General*

This character is bland, boring, and simple, with almost no chance of
ever getting noticed or enticing anyone. His Presence and Communication
may not be greater than 0. Any use of Abilities like Leadership, Charm,
Intrigue, Etiquette or similar — as well as Personality Rolls — to stir
or impress people suffers a –3 penalty.
