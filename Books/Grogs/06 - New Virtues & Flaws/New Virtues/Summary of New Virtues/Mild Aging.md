*Minor, General*

The character's aging rolls benefit from a +1 bonus to the Living
Conditions Modifier, in addition to whatever his social standing
normally offers him (**ArM5**, page 170). Furthermore, he receives a +3
bonus to rolls to survive an aging crisis. He may be dedicated to living
a healthy life, or else have been blessed by some supernatural being.
