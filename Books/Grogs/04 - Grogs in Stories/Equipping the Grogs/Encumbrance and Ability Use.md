Encumbrance should penalize most physical activities, such as running,
jumping, hiding, and moving silently. Encumbrance also subtracts from
any fatigue rolls. However, under certain circumstances, a heavily
encumbered character should be at an even greater disadvantage
particularly for climbing, swimming, and balancing. In such situations,
use Burden rather than Encumbrance as a penalty to Ability rolls, to
represent the additional penalty imposed by carrying a lot of extra kit.
