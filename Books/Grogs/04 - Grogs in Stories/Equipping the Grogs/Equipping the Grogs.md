Grogs are expected to be ready for anything, and carrying the correct
equipment is the essence of good preparation. However, the benefits of
equipment are often downplayed in **Ars Magica Fifth Edition** — this is
not a game in which you load up on dungeoneering gear, since magi are
considered to be above such things. They assume, as do their players,
that the grogs have everything they might need. This section discusses
the use of such equipment, and expands on the concepts of Load, Burden,
and Encumbrance.
