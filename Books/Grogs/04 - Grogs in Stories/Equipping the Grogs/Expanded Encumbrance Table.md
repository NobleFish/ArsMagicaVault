All four-footed beasts increase their Strength by half again for the
purpose of determining Encumbrance and weight. Note that pack animals
and horses usually have Strength scores beyond the normal human range: a
courser has a Strength of +4, and a destrier has a Strength of +6 (see
Realms of Power: Magic, page 142, or the Book of Beasts, freely
available from Atlas Games' website). A destrier can therefore take its
rider (Load 21), his armor (Load 6), his lance, mace, and heater shield
(Load 7), and a full grog pack (Load 4), and still be unencumbered
(Burden 8, effective Strength 9).

**1 Point of Load = 7 pounds**

**Maximum Practical Weight:**\
**Burden equal to (Strength + 5)**

**Lift off Ground:**\
**Burden equal to (Strength + 7)**

**Push or Drag:**\
**Burden equal to (Strength + 12)**

**Using a Pulley:**\
**+3 to effective Strength when lifting**

**Using a Lever:**\
**+3 to effective Strength when pushing**

**Pack Animals:**\
**1½ x Strength when calculating Encumbrance**
