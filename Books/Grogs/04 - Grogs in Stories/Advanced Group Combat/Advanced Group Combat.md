Group Combat is an important concept in **Ars Magica Fifth Edition**,
and the principal role of grogs in many stories. The rules in this
section expand the Group Combat rules in **ArM5** to add a bit more
tactical depth. Feel free to use group combat when it makes sense, or
have characters fight independently whenever drama or troupe preference
dictates. These rules were first printed in Chapter 9 of *Lords of Men*.
