Every character has 7 points to spend on buying positive
Characteristics, and can take up to 7 negative points to gain an equal
number of additional points for positive Characteristics (**ArM5**, page
30). Various Virtues and Flaws can alter the number of points gained, or
affect the final Characteristics once the points have been spent. The
nearby insert has several arrays of Characteristics bought with the
standard number of points; simply pick one for your character.
