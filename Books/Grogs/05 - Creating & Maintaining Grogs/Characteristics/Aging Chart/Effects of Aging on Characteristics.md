All Aging Points must be applied to the Characteristics of the character
in the usual fashion. The Aging Table (**ArM5**, page 170) is designed
to affect certain Characteristics in favor of others. Just under half of
all Aging Points gained (excluding those from crises) are applied to any
Characteristic (that is, a roll of 10–12 on the Aging Table). Of the
remaining Aging Points, approximately half are gained in Quickness or
Stamina, 30 percent in Perception or Presence, 15 percent in Strength or
Dexterity, and the remainder in Intelligence and Communication. Players
should bear these distributions in mind when applying Aging Points to a
character.
