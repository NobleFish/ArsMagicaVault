Most of the methods by which Virtues can be gained (see earlier) can
also rid a character of Flaws, but not all methods are applicable to all
Flaws. A character with a Missing Hand cannot grow it back through
Initiation, but must instead resort to supernatural means. However,
characters may be able to take part in stories that rid themselves of a
curse, for example.
