A lay brother works at a monastery, but has not taken Holy Orders. This
package is also suitable for novices preparing for ordination. In the
latter case, some years of Student are appropriate. The Profession
Ability could be Scribe, Gardener, Brewer, or any other suitable trade.

**Five Years:** +50 Organization Lore: Church, +10 Folk Ken, +15
Profession (see earlier)

**Three Years:** +30 Organization Lore: Church, +15 Profession (see
earlier)
