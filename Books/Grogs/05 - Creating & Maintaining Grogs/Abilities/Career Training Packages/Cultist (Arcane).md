A cultist has spent years worshiping an evil force in secret. If the
character has the Ceremony Virtue, he can apply some of his experience
points in Infernal Lore to that Ability instead. This package might also
represent a pagan (in which case substitute Faerie Lore for Infernal
Lore), or a devotee of a mystery cult (substitute Organization Lore:
Cult and Magic Lore for Infernal Lore).

**Five Years:** +15 Guile, +50 Infernal Lore, +10 Stealth

**Three Years:** +15 Guile, +30 Infernal Lore
