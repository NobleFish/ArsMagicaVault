The character must have the Failed Apprentice Virtue to take this
package, which permits her to take Arcane and Academic Abilities. She
probably has several years of Student to account for her Academic
Abilities. It is assumed that she has no Art scores; either she has not
been Opened to the Arts, or else her ability to work Hermetic magic has
been destroyed.

**Five Years:** +5 Artes Liberales, +5 Code of Hermes, +5 Concentration,
+5 Finesse, +10 Latin, +25 Magic Theory, +15 Organization Lore: Order of
Hermes, +5 Penetration

**Three Years:** +5 Concentration, +10 Latin, +20 Magic Theory, +5
Organization Lore: Order of Hermes, +5 Penetration
