The player characters are grogs who hold influential positions at a
university, probably one of the larger institutions like those at Paris
or Bologna. While contending with the usual challenges of academic life
— heresy, rowdy students, antagonism of the locals, and so forth — they
also become gradually aware of an insidious secret society among the
masters and students of the school. It becomes clear that this cabal is
attempting to discover some dark mystery hidden in the bowels of the
university, and that the player characters must race to discover it
first.

The nature of the secret society is up to the storyguide. It could be a
cult of diabolists trying to uncover some rite to raise a demon, or a
group of churchmen trying to keep the very same hidden. The society
might consist of faeries, or even a hidden covenant of magi. When the
players discover the secret, they may have to re-evaluate their
preconceptions about their antagonists, and may even find themselves
working with them.
