Last but not least, grog stories can feature the achievement of an
individual's or group's personal goals. This can be as prosaic as
detailing a grog's courtship of his true love, or as melodramatic as a
quest by a blood brotherhood to exact revenge on the monster that slew
their beloved leader. Personal stories occasionally result in changes to
the character, in the form of transformation (see Chapter 5), but this
should always be the focus of the story, rather than incidental.
