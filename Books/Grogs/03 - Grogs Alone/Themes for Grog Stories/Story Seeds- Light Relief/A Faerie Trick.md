A faerie decides to play a trick on a village, and everyone wakes up one
morning looking like Simon the Basket-Weaver. While they struggle with
the confusion this brings, several enterprising villagers decide to
perform petty acts of mischief and revenge on one another, since this is
a day without consequence — no one can be sure who did what. The grogs
might be passing through and be affected by the curse, or they might be
left to try to disentangle who is responsible for stealing their goods.
