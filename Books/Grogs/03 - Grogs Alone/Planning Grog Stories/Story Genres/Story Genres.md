There are seven genres that lend themselves well to grog stories:
Action/Adventure, Comedy, Creature Feature, Drama, Exploration, Horror,
and Mystery. However, one should note that these broad genres are not
mutually exclusive, and a story can always switch genres midway through
to help keep the players on their toes.
