Stories can end in one of two ways: a happy ending or a disastrous
ending — the original meanings of comedy and tragedy, respectively. Some
stories are specifically intended to end well or end poorly, regardless
of the actions of the grog characters. This may seem harsh, but if used
sparingly, it reinforces the idea that grogs are support characters,
rather than the main cast.
