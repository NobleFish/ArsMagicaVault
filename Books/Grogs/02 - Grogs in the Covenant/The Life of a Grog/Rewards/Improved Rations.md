The fare of a grog is usually pretty plain, so a simple and effective
way of rewarding a grog for a well-done job is to give him a one-off or
temporary elevation in his provender. Typical improvements in rations
include a private cellar of salt, wastel bread (the best bread
available), fresh meat, or a double ration of wine or ale. This
improvement in rations might last a day, a month, a season, or a year,
depending on the magnitude of the rewarded behavior. A grog spending a
year on improved rations receives a –1 improvement to his Living
Conditions Modifier when making aging rolls. A grog receiving one of the
other rewards listed here often also receives improved rations as well.
