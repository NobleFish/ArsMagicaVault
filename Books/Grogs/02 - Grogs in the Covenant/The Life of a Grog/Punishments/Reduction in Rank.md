Rank has its privileges: the wages are higher, the ration of food is
better, and the accommodation is superior. Furthermore, rank has
implications for one's pension once one is too old to serve the covenant
(see later). Reduction in rank is permanent; all grogs have the capacity
to re-earn the rank they once held, but a captain will be reluctant to
promote someone who has lost his rank through punishment.
