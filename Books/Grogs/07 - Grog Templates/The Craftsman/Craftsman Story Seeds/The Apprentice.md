A Craftsman approaches the covenant with something to sell: an
apprentice. He considers the boy to be useless to him, but a wise woman
told him that there was a touch of the magical about him. He wants five
pounds for the boy to cover his lost earnings and his trouble. The
apprentice doesn't have The Gift, but it is quickly apparent to the magi
that his talents are linked to the Magic Realm, and that the covenant is
the only place in which he will reach his potential.
