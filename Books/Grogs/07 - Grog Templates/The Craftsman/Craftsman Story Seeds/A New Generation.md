An aging covenant Craftsman, ailments weighing him down with each
passing year, regrets never having taken an apprentice. These feelings
are mirrored by one of the covenant magi. While the Craftsman and a few
friends venture outside the covenant to find a youngster with an
aptitude for his craft, the magus sets out to find a Gifted child. After
false hope given by faeries, does the Craftsman finally find his
apprentice? And can the magus overcome Infernal deception to rescue the
Gifted child? The stories of both the magus and the Craftsman are
mirrored in their searches, but perhaps the resolution lies in the same
place.
