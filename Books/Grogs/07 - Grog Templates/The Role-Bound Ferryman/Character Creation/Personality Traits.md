Unless affected by other effects, characters bound to their role possess
and express Personality Traits just as other grogs, but years of being
restricted to one role, often unable to live a normal natural life, may
take its toll on their personality. They may be resentful or resigned,
bitter or accepting, searching for release or loyal to their covenant.

The Role-bound Ferryman discussed earlier has the following Personality
Traits: Loyal to the Covenant +3, Taciturn +2, Reliable +1
