The covenant awakes one morning to find something unheard of in living
memory: the ferryman is not at his post. Not only that, but his boat is
gone too. A magus of House Verditius, who visited the covenant some
seasons before, has stolen the ferryman away. He recognized that the
ferryman is bound to his role, and bound to his boat. The magus has
taken him in order to experiment upon him, to find the secret of binding
devices to the living, or binding a living mind into a device. Can the
covenant find their man before the Verditius experiments tear him from
his boat?
