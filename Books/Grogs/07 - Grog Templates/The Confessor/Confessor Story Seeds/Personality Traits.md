If the Confessor has taken the new Uncertain Faith Flaw, he has a
Personality Trait of the same name with a score of at least 1. As for
other traits, he is likely to be a Good Listener, which helps further
define his role in the saga. A third trait says more about the Confessor
as a person, who may be Compassionate, Duty-Bound, or Strict.
