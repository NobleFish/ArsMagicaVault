A popular and influential magus, rumored to wield holy magic, comes to
the covenant looking for material to help him with a tractatus he is
writing. The book is a penitentiary, or a manual of confession, designed
to help penitent and confessor alike to correctly identify their sins
and the appropriate penance to be undertaken. While mundane manuals of
confession derive penances for an individual based on the four humors,
the seven sins, and the seven virtues, the magus is attempting to add
the fifteen Hermetic Arts. Are the magi happy to have this magus poking
around their sins, penances, and studies? What happens if his notes fall
into the wrong hands? Come to that, as the magus visited a rival
covenant the previous season, how can the magi get a look at his notes
themselves?
