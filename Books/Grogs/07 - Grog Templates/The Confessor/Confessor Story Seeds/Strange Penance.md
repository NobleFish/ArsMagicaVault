An elderly Tytalus staying at the covenant suddenly falls into Twilight.
Although he recovers, he has seen that his end is near, and calls for a
confessor to hear his many and numerous sins. Uncertain what penance to
give so old and so powerful a man, the inexperienced Confessor seeks the
advice of the magi of the covenant, careful not to divulge the nature
(or quantity) of the old man's sins.
