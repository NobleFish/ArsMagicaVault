The following lists of Virtues and Flaws are appropriate for various
religious leaders who might be cast in the role of Confessor.

**CHRISTIAN CONFESSOR:** Priest; Relic, Clear Thinker; Uncertain
Faith\*, Vow (Celibacy), Weakness (Drinking)

**ISLAMIC CONFESSOR:** 'Alim\*\*; Educated (Islamic), Clear Thinker;
Uncertain Faith\*, Vow (Celibacy), Temperate

**JEWISH CONFESSOR:** Rabbi\*\*; Educated (Hebrew)\*\*, Clear Thinker;
Outsider (Minor)\*\*, Uncertain Faith\*, Meddler (Minor)

\\ See Chapter 6: New Virtues and Flaws

\\\* See *Realms of Power: The Divine Revised Edition*
