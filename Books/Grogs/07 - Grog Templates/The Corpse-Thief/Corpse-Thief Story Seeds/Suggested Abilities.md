A Corpse-Thief needs the same Abilities that a regular thief benefits
from: Stealth, Awareness, Athletics, and Legerdemain. Knowing what to
steal is covered by Magic Lore and Penetration; the latter is also
useful for any magical abilities the character possesses. In the
preparation of material for sale, the Profession: Apothecary and the
Craft: Poppets Abilities are also highly useful. Area Lore and
Organization Lore are important Abilities in developing a client base
from which to earn a living.
