A Corpse-Thief who works for the covenant has a good professional
knowledge of the local hedge wizards, and perhaps knows some of them
personally. He may even be aware of their powers and weaknesses. When
the magi hear of a particularly troublesome non-Hermetic wizard, they
come to the Corpse-Thief for information. He can give them a list of
names who it might be, and where they each can be found. The magi may be
surprised — dismayed, even! — by how many minor magical practitioners
live in the region right under their noses. Why are there so many, what
are they up to, and how did they remain unnoticed for so long?
