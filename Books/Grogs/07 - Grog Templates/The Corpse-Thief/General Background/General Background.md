It takes a special sort of person to be a Corpse-Thief. Even the most
inveterate, hard-bitten criminal generally shows respect for the dead.
Furthermore, the knowledge of forbidden things hints at a dark past. A
Corpse-Thief could have been the child of a witch or warlock, but showed
no talent for sorcery at an early age, and so was put to work in a
useful trade to support his parent. Alternatively, the Corpse-Thief
could have been an initiate of a diabolic cult, who practiced his trade
at the bidding of his infernally tainted masters. The indoctrination
that accompanies either of these backgrounds sets a twisted sense of
morality that allows the character to do the horrible things that he
does.
