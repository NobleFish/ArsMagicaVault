An animal grog could easily become a companion by being transformed by
powerful supernatural effects. As a result of warping, the creature
could become infused with powers of its own, or else become corrupted by
some hellish power. The most extreme form of this sort of transformation
entails gaining a Might Score. The four *Realms of Power* sourcebooks
include details for creating creatures that are either aligned to or are
members of a given supernatural Realm.
