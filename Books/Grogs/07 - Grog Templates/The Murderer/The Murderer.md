*I know they say what I done was wrong, but I'd do it again, I would.
An' I'd look into 'is eyes while doin' it too.*

The covenants established and managed by magi of the Order seem to
suspend some of the normal rules of the world around them. Whether by
negotiated charter or the power to impose their will, covenants manage
their own affairs within their own walls. And this de facto sanctuary
attracts those that the outside world might dispose of, those like
thieves and murderers.
