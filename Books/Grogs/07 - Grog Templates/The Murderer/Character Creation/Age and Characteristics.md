A man may be driven to murder at any age, and *any* man may be driven to
it. Because of this, there is no particular age or set of
characteristics that define him. However, a man looking to extend his
sanctuary will find a way to make himself useful around the covenant,
probably taking on any menial tasks that present themselves. So he'll
probably have a high Dexterity so that he's good with his hands, and at
least a positive Strength and Stamina, representing his determination to
work hard.
