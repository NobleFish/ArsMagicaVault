A magus has reached a plateau in his study of the Art of Mentem, or the
knowledge of Philosophiae. To push his work onwards, he has decided he
needs to examine a damaged mind, and he has heard of a murderer given
sanctuary in a nearby covenant. Can the magus gain access to the
murderer? And how does the season of study affect both the magus and his
subject?
