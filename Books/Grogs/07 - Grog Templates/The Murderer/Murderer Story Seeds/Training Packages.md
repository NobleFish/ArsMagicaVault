The following packages are appropriate for hard-working covenfolk with
particular trade: Brawler, Covenfolk, Craftsman, Laborer, Local,
Servant, Tinker.

However, the Murderer, with his Master-Of-None Flaw, must apply his
experience points to a different Ability each season of the year. This
means that the packages must be adjusted so that they each put
experience into four different Abilities rather than the more usual
three.
