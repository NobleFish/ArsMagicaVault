You can also play an Almogavar as a companion. The character could be
the Almogaten leading the unit, or perhaps he is a specialist of some
sort. Optionally, he might hire himself out individually as a
specialist. He will lack the support and camaraderie of a unit, but his
fortunes are his own and he may be Poor or Wealthy.

As a companion, the character may have Story Flaws, and these could
apply to himself or his whole unit. Enemies is an obvious example. Close
Family Ties could represent a tightly bonded unit, and maybe they *are*
all related. Dependents may be unusual, but some bands of Almogavars
include the women and children of the warriors in addition to the usual
camp followers.
