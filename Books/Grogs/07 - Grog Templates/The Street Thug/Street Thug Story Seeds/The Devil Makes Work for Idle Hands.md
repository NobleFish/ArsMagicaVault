The Street Thug has a price on his head and is forced to lie low while
the tempers cool and memories fade. The trouble is, he's hiding out in
the covenant. It doesn't take long for his presence to become
disruptive. The magi need to find a job for him to do. It's either that
or turn him into a tree for the next month. But as it happens, the magi
have heard that a tax collector of ill repute is arriving in town in the
next week, and the taxes he's collected will be stored in the sheriff's
keep. There might be a way to keep the Street Thug occupied and make a
little money after all.
