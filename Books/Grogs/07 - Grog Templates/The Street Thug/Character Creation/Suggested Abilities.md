The Street Thug seeks experience in anything that helps him get through
another day on the street. This is likely to be Brawl, Guile, Area Lore,
and for intimidation, Leadership. Brawl is the Street Thug's combat
Ability of choice as people in cities are rarely armed and even more
rarely armored. He is also backed up by a gang and will almost always be
in a trained combat group when with his own people. When not using his
fists, a bludgeon, or a knife, he prefers a hatchet for its small size
and brutal simplicity.

As the Street Thug grows older, he gains more authority over his gang,
learning more about its activities and members, represented by his
growing Leadership and Gang Lore Abilities.
