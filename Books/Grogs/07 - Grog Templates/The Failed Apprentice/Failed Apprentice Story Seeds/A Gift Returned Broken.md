A magus Bonisagi has fostered his apprentice out to another magus, just
as House Bonisagus tradition dictates. But the apprentice suddenly
returns to the covenant with a letter from the fostering magus. It seems
a magical experiment has robbed the boy of his Gift. What does the magus
do now? Can he force compensation for the loss? How can he replace the
apprentice? And what becomes of the young student with so much stolen
from him?
