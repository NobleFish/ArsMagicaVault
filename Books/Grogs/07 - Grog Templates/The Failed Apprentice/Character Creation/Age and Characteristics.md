Apprentices can be taken at any age, though magi work hard to find them
in their youth, when they can be molded into the image of a magus. A
Failed Apprentice joining the covenant upon losing his Gift will
probably be younger than 25, while one who has traveled before finding
his place will be older.

The Failed Apprentice is normally intelligent, so as to take the mental
rigors of a learned life, and hardy to withstand the toll of working
magic, so Intelligence and Stamina should be his primary
characteristics. Positive Perception or Dexterity will also benefit him,
depending on what Supernatural Abilities remain.
