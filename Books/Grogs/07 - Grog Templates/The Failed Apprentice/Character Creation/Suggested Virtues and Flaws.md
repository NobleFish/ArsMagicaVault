The Failed Apprentice has a number of Virtues and Flaws that reflect his
magical past, and perhaps hint at a magical future.

Failed Apprentice; Latent Magical Ability, Magic Sensitivity; Broken
Vessel\*, Missing Eye, Offensive to Animals

\\ See Chapter 6, New Virtues and Flaws

The Failed Apprentice Social Status Virtue is apt and gives the
character access to both Academic and Arcane abilities. Latent Magical
Ability and Magic Sensitivity are the remnants of the apprentice's Gift.
The Flaws also represent these remnants, with the Missing Eye a lasting
physical reminder, Offensive to Animals echoing the effect his Gift once
had, and Broken Vessel a continuing threat of his magical powers
slipping away from him. Alternatively, a Personality Flaw representing
his continued bad feelings about the events that stripped his Gift from
him would be entirely appropriate in place of a physical defect.
