This enchanted buckle allows the wearer to move with freedom in any
direction, even unsupported into the air. It was designed for and
awarded to the librarian of a nowdissolved covenant and became a symbol
of the office, passed down through generations of librarians. That
covenant had a library built in a tall narrow tower, with the most
valuable books and scrolls stored in a chamber near the roof that was
inaccessible without using the buckle.

(Base 5, +1 Touch, +1 Concentration; +5 for 24 uses per day; +5 levels
to allow the device to maintain concentration)
