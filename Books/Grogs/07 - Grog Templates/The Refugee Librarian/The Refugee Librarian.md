*Listen to me, we're here now and there's no going back. Just be
thankful we have a home again and serve these new masters as they
deserve, for they have taken us in when no one else would.*

When a magus or a covenant falls, the loss is not only one to the Order
but is most keenly felt by the covenfolk. Suddenly, they find themselves
without their masters, without protection, and without income. Those
serving a fallen covenant face finding new mundane lords, or trekking
out to join another covenant.
