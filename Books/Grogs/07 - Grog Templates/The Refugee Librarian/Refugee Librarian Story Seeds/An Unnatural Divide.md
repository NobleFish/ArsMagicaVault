Ever since the refugees arrived at the covenant all those years ago, the
community has been on the brink of division. Over the years, the
covenfolk have split into those who live in the Upper Village and those
who live in the Lower Village. And when a girl from the Upper Village is
murdered, the magi must find the killer before revenge is taken against
the Lower Villagers.
