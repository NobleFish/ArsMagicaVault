The Misguided Mystic can be made into a companion character by putting
her in touch with a higher power. The Visions Story Flaw is an excellent
way to achieve this; not only are the visions true, but they also drag
the character into stories. True prophetic power only comes from the
Divine (*Realms of Power: The Divine*, page 54), but there are
non-Divine powers that could be useful to an oracle, such as Divination
and Augury (*The Mysteries Revised Edition*, page 58) and the Dream
Power (*Realms of Power: Faerie*, page 126). Even without overt powers
of prediction, there are several Supernatural Abilities that fit very
well with this character concept, and a companion character could take
two or more of the Abilities listed earlier.
