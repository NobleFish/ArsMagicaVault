Characters who have worked as prostitutes, and seek employment with
covenants, usually come from the lower classes of Mythic European
society. Women of higher social standing tend to act as mistresses to a
single powerful lord, and are supported by his arrangements, or by their
own families. Choose Social Status Virtues appropriately.

Many of the other Virtues and Flaws appropriate for characters working
in the sex trade — Gossip, Protection, Social Contacts — are lost before
play begins, when the character leaves her hometown. Players should only
select Virtues and Flaws that the character will use in play.
