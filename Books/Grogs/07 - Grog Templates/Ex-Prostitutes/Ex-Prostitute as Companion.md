Ex-Prostitutes who become companion characters differ from their sisters
not so much in background or Ability, but in their fortune in their
later career, and their influence in the troupe's stories. Characters
who rise to a station that they could lose if their past were revealed
may select the Dark Secret Flaw. Characters without this Flaw may
instead have another Story Flaw, fleshing out the character's background
further.
