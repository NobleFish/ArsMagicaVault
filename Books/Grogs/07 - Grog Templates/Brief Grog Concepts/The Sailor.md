Many covenants earn income from the sea, through fishing or trade, and
each ship needs a crew. While crews for these vessels have little to do
with the Order, magi who undertake sea voyages would do well to retain a
turb of Sailors.

The chief Ability is Profession: Sailor, but Area Lore for coastal areas
is important. And for covenants that have built or bought enchanted
vessels, Finesse is useful as it helps the Sailor to control the ship's
enchantments. But the Sailor's loyalties are to his captain first, his
ship second, and then to the covenant. Appropriate Virtues and Flaws
include Cautious with Profession: Sailor, Wilderness Sense, and Craving
for Travel\*.
