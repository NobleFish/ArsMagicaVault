Every fighting turb needs a leader, and most have one. Depending on the
size of the turb and the level of organization, the Officer may be
anything from the most-senior fighter to a knight or formally trained
officer.

Custos is a recommended Virtue, as is Warrior. He needs access to
Martial Abilities, and a higher officer needs to know Latin and be
literate, in addition to basic arithmetic. Good Teacher or Puissance in
favored abilities would help, or Inspirational for a captain. Key
Abilities include Folk Ken, Leadership, Martial Abilities, Riding, and
Teaching. Etiquette and various Organization and Area Lores apply as
well.
