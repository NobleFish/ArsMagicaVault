Always unnoticed and never catching the attention of the masters, the
Loyal Watchman will never amount to much and he'd be surprised if the
scholars even knew his name. He simply takes his pay, feeds his family,
and raises the next generation of Loyal Watchmen.

The Loyal Watchman defends the covenant. He does not venture out with
the magi and is nobody's custos. But he knows the covenant like the back
of his hand; it is all he has known. Every covenant has these
downtrodden nameless grogs, but once in a while they come into their own
and show their worth. Awareness is the key Ability of the Watchman, and
he may well have a good local Area Lore, thanks to long months of
intense exploration. Having spent a long time at the covenant, he may
have developed a good Organization Lore: Covenant. Appropriate Virtues
and Flaws include Puissant Awareness and Nocturnal.
