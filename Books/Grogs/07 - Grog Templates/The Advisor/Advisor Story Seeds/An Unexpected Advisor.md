Nobody is entirely sure how long he has been at the covenant, who his
parents were, or how he came to know so much, but the reserved and
confident young man was right. Investigating the young man's reasoning
and examining his information proves it: that trouble with the Church
*was* stirred up by a Tytalus known to the covenant. How had this young
man spotted what is now so obvious when all the magi of the covenant
failed? What are they going to do with their unexpected benefactor? And
what are they going to do about the Tytalus? Their new Advisor may have
an idea …
