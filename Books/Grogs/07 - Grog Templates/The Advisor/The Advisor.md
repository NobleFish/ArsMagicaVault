*My lord, if I may, the magus Bellatorius is reputed to be a man not
given to either forgiveness or forgetfulness. If you really must
challenge someone to certamen, perhaps Benedic ex Miscellanea might make
for a less resentful target.*

This specialist within the covenant has found a niche for himself by
learning all he can about his covenant, the Order of Hermes, and the
supernatural realms with which his masters so frequently come into
contact. This encyclopedic knowledge of the world in which his magi
operate makes him an essential companion to the more cloistered magi.
