The Order of Hermes supports a vibrant book trade with two main
branches: sale of finished books, and scribal hospitality. Sale of books
occurs through the Redcap system, supplemented by exchanges that occur
at Tribunal meetings. The use of magic to support the Redcap network is
considered more fully in
<a href="Houses_of_Hermes:_True_Lineages_Chapter_Three:_House_Mercere"
class="wikilink" title="Houses of Hermes: True Lineages">Houses of
Hermes: True Lineages</a>. Scribal hospitality occurs when a magus is
allowed to copy from the library of a covenant to which he does not
belong. Hospitality is usually offered in exchange for a payment or
service.
