Any member of the Order of Hermes may designate an area as their
"sanctum," and most choose to designate their laboratory and living
quarters. A decision of the First Tribunal means that a magus in
another's sanctum has no legal protection from the owner of the sanctum
and may be attacked or killed without legal consequences. There is a
strong convention against entering a sanctum without permission, but the
Peripheral Code on these points differs between Tribunals. See Houses of
Hermes: True Lineages for more details—particularly pages 46–47, 62, and
71. A ruling of the Grand Tribunal of 997 requires a sanctum to be
marked by a circle inscribed within a square, with lines joining the
square's opposite corners.
