Maria, a young woman living in a village not far from the volcano, feels
a strange empathy for fire, and she can predict the activity of the
volcano through her dreams (she has the Visions Flaw). Her pregnant
mother was the only survivor when the volcano destroyed the village
during its last eruption a generation ago — only she heeded the warnings
and made a miraculous escape. Maria, born with the same talent as her
mother, makes dire predictions of an imminent eruption and begs the
villagers to assuage the fire spirit to prevent it. They, however, scoff
at her heathen mumblings, and so it looks as if the fire spirit will
indeed return with a vengeance.
