A far-reaching perception of strange and baffling phenomena that helps
you understand their nature, though you may seem strange or even
ludicrous to the uninitiated. Your score in this Ability is added to
rolls to interpret dreams and riddles, and to understand phantasms and
arcane or mysterious situations. Thanks to this Ability, Criamon magi go
into Wizard's Twilight (see page 228) more often, but survive it better
than most.

*Specialties:* interpreting signs, explaining the Enigma, Twilight.
