This is a big book, as it needs to be to explain all the rules and
background for **Ars Magica**. The first chapter, this [[Books/Core/01 - Introduction/01 - Introduction|Introduction]],
provides a quick overview of the game, and explains the basic
die-rolling mechanic.

Chapter 2, [[Books/Core/02 - The Order of Hermes/02 - The Order of Hermes|The Order of Hermes]], describes the order of wizards that is
central to the game.

Chapters 3, 4 and 5, [[Books/Core/03 - Characters/03 - Characters||Characters]], [[Books/Core/04 - Virtues and Flaws/04 - Virtues and Flaws|Virtues and Flaws]], and [[Books/Core/05 - Abilities/05 - Abilities|Abilities]] contain all the information necessary to create a character. 

Chapter 6, [[Books/Core/06 - Covenants/06 - Covenants|Covenants]], deals with creating the place where the characters live.

Chapters 7, 8, and 9, [[Books/Core/07 - Hermetic Magic/07 - Hermetic Magic|Hermetic Magic]], [[Books/Core/08 - Laboratory/08 - Laboratory|Laboratory]], and [[Books/Core/09 - Spells/09 - Spells|Spells]], describe
Hermetic Magic. 

Chapter 10, [[Books/Core/10 - Long-Term Events/10 - Long-Term Events|Long Term Events]], covers things that happen to the characters over months or years, including character improvement, aging and death, and Warping by supernatural power. 

Chapter 11, [[Books/Core/11 - Obstacles/11 - Obstacles|Obstacles]], details the things that might harm the characters, most
notably combat.

Chapter 12, [[Books/Core/12 - Realms/12 - Realms|Realms]], explains the four different sorts of supernatural
power in Mythic Europe, the Divine, Faerie, Infernal, and Magical
Realms. 

Chapter 13, [[Books/Core/13 - Bestiary/13 - Bestiary|Bestiary]], provides a sample of creatures from each
of these Realms. 

Chapter 14, [[Books/Core/14 - Mythic Europe/14 - Mythic Europe|Mythic Europe]], provides hints on making
your game feel like it happens in the mythic world.

Chapters 15 and 16, [[Books/Core/15 - Stories/15 - Stories|Stories]] and [[Books/Core/16 - Sagas/16 - Sagas|Sagas]], provides advice on using the preceding material to actually make a game that the troupe enjoys.
