R: Eve, D: Mom, T: Ind\
You can detect the conflicting motives behind one target's actions. Thus
you might learn that a guard feels conflict between fear and duty. This
spell is often used before application of a Muto or Rego spell, since it
is usually easier to change a target's emotions after you've sensed them
than it is to create emotions.\
(Base 10, +1 Eve)
