R: Voice, D: Mom, T: Ind\
Cast on another spell while the other spell is being cast, this causes
the targeted spell to have the exact opposite of its normal effects.
This effect works on spells up to half the (level – 5) of the Mirror of
Opposition spell. The effects of the inversion are up to the storyguide
to determine, but the target of the inverted spell usually remains the
same as that of the original spell. The inverted spell cannot overcome
any limitations of Hermetic magic. For example, if a Momentary Perdo
Corpus spell is cast, the inversion is probably a Creo Corpus spell of
Sun duration. There are ten versions of this spell, each affecting
spells of one of the Hermetic forms.\
(Base effect, +2 Voice)
