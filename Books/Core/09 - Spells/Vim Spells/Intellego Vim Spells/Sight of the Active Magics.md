R: Per, D: Conc, T: Vision\
You can see spells as "auras" around things and people. The color of the
aura indicates the Form of the spell working on a subject, the color
matching the appropriate one described in Sense the Nature of Vis (page
367). The Technique of a spell working on the subject is recognized by
the aura's shape, not color. Creo and Rego are both very orderly auras.
Muto is constantly shifting, and Intellego usually is as well, but more
slowly. A Perdo aura is usually in fragments. Thus, a magus with both
Endurance of the Berserkers and The Invisible Eye Revealed upon him has
a very controlled, orderly aura of deep red around his or her entire
body, and the magus's eyes are surrounded with slowly shifting auras of
a silvery hue. You should be warned: Imaginem or Vim spells may be used
to disguise auras. Also note that this spell will detect the residues of
some spells.\
(Base 5, +1 Conc, +4 Vision, +2 Techniques and Forms)
