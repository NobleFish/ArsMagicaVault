By Paul Tevis

High in the mountains, fed from melting snow, lies a stream of crystal
clarity. As it rushes down the mountainside, it collects neither dirt
nor leaf. The village at the base of the mountain enjoys considerable
fame for the beer that the villagers brew from its sparkling water.

Magi are less interested in the villagers’ beer than they are in its
source. Halfway up the mountain, the stream pours over a small
waterfall. On a ledge at the top the fall lies a stone inscribed with a
Hermetic rune for Aquam. On auspicious nights of the year, a magus who
sits upon this stone may thrust his hand into the water and cast
Distillation of the Purest Stream (see below) to recover Aquam vis. This
requires a Concentration + Strength roll against an Ease Factor of 15,
due to the frigid water and the strong current. If the magus is
protected against the cold, the Ease Factor drops to 9. If the
Concentration roll fails, no vis may be gathered that night.

For a day after the vis is collected, the water from the stream (and any
beer brewed with the water) tastes brackish and foul.
