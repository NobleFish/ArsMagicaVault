The covenant’s buildings need to be properly maintained, decorated, and
furnished to prevent them from falling into disrepair. For example,
damaged roofing and stonework has to be patched up, old furniture needs
to be replaced, and there are often plenty of minor repair jobs for a
carpenter to do.

Add two pounds for any relevant Minor Fortifications Boon, and five
pounds for any relevant Major Fortifications Boon (see Chapter 2: Boons
and Hooks, Fortification). Only Boons that add to the size,
magnificence, or quantity of the covenant’s buildings or fortifications
count.
