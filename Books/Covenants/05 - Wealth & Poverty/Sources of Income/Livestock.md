Farmland owned by the covenant might be devoted mainly to animal
pasture, instead of crops, and income gained from the sale of livestock,
hides, or wool. Just as with arable farming, this is not the most
glamorous source of income, and usually requires at least a small
holding of peasants to tend the herds. A few covenants are known to
breed sheep and calves in order to produce parchment and vellum.

Pros: Animal herding is less labor intensive than arable crops; use can
be made of more marginal land (such as forest or heath).

Cons: Animals are often smelly and noisy; the risk of animal pestilence.

Magical Options: Creo Animal to make cloth, hides, or parchment, or
enhance fertility; Rego Animal to control herds; Herbam to nurture crops
for grazing.
