A circle twenty miles across, as a rough estimate, is the area that the
mounted warriors from a castle can impose its lord’s will. The circle is
distorted by geography, political considerations, and mystical
influence, but the twenty miles around a covenant are its home ground.
The majority of the covenant’s food, income, and emergencies are
generated within this radius, and these Boons and Hooks describe its
contents.
