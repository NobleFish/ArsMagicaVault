These covenfolk live at a great fortification that’s the envy — or
nightmare — of every noble for miles and miles. Such a covenant might
profitably employ a book-binder, cook, percamenarius (parchment-maker),
and silver-smith — these can be based on the craftsman template above.
The castle is also very likely to have a castellan or steward, which can
be based on the lord of the manor or lady of the manor templates. The
templates below describe additional covenfolk that might be found at a
grand castle.
