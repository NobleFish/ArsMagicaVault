CrIg 15

R: Touch, D: Ring, T: Ind

This spell creates a steady, cool, smokeless light that does not
flicker. Variants of this spell are enchanted into devices in many
libraries. Some are handheld, while others float by their user, are
embedded in the ceiling, or overlook the work desks of individual
scholars.

*(Base 4, +1 Touch, +2 Ring)*
