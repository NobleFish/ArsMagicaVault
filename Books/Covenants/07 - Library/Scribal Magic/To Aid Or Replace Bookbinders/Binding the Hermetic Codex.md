ReAn 15

R: Touch, D: Mom, T: Group Req: Vary

A variant of Binding the Mundane Codex, this spell allows magi to
incorporate resonant materials into their bindings. Binding a book with
resonant materials is extremely difficult and requires an Perception +
Finesse roll against an Ease Factor of 12 + 3 per resonant material, to
a maximum of three materials. This spell allows readers of the book to
claim the +1 quality bonus for sound binding.

*(Base 1, +1 Touch, +2 Group, +3 complexity)*
