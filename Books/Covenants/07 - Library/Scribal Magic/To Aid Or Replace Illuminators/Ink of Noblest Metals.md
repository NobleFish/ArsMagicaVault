MuTe 20

R: Voice, D: Dia, T: Ind Req: Aquam

This spell transforms gold into a liquid that is applied as ink. When
the spell expires, it turns back into solid metal, allowing magi to
place it on a page without developing skill with gold leaf. By pouring
the golden ink into molds, the magi can set it into shapes.

*(Base 3, +2 metal, +2 Voice, +1 Dia)*
