A magus seeking a fact in a library makes an Intelligence + Ability
roll, with a bonus modified by the library’s holdings. Sample library
bonuses are as follows.'

- +5 for a peerless collection on a subject
- +4 for a collection that is specialized in the subject but not unique
- +3 for a collection like that of a powerful Autumn covenant with no
  particular interest in the subject
- +2 for a collection like that of an average Summer covenant with no
  particular interest in the subject
- +1 for a library with only a few books on the subject

A library may also have a bonus due to the presence of a significant
magical item. For example, a library lit using a lamp made from the
skull of Chiron, the centaur who taught humans medicine, might have a +3
on all medical research rolls.
