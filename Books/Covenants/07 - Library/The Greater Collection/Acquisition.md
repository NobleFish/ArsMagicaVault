The Order of Hermes supports a vibrant book trade through two main
branches: sale of finished books, and scribal hospitality. Books are
sold through the Redcap system, supplemented by exchanges that occur at
Tribunal meetings. The use of magic to support the Redcap network is
considered more fully in Houses of Hermes: True Lineages, page 101.
Scribal hospitality occurs when a magus is allowed to copy from the
library of a covenant to which he does not belong. Hospitality is
usually offered in exchange for a payment or service.
