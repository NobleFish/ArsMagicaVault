Accession is the process of preparing books for use by readers, so that
they become part of the library. Two processes that comprise accession:
the physical preparation of the book and the classification of the book.
In Hermetic libraries, physical preparation is performed first.
Sometimes, when a book is scribed for sale to a library, much of its
shelf preparation is performed before its transfer, since shelf-readied
books are more durable, and so travel better.
