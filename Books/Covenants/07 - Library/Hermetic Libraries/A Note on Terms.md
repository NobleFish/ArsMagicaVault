In this book, an attempt has been made to keep obscure medieval terms to
a minimum. The term “armarius” has been used here because the English
term for these book closets is “book press.” To avoid confusion with a
printing press or binder’s press, the Latin term has been used,
although, in Mythic Europe, an armarius is any sort of locked cupboard.
A related, modern, English term is armoire, borrowed from the French.
