Many magi have protective enchantments of their sancta. Most have a ward
that prevents casual visits by those who might accidentally damage the
magus’s experiments, for example curious covenfolk. Many others have
spells that kill intruders. Seen objectively, these are often excessive.
Any intruder facing these enchantments must already have passed through
the covenant’s fortifications and Aegis of the Hearth. The person most
likely to be killed by complicated sanctum defenses is the magus’s own
apprentice. Traps are, however, common in those covenants where
amicability within the council of magi has broken down. Some magi employ
them in the defense of their keep, their place of final refuge in siege.
