A yellow-flowered plant that grows close to the ground. This plant
contains a single pawn of Herbam vis, and once in its life it can be
triggered to transfer this pawn into one of its seeds. The plant that
grows from the seed likewise inherits the same power.

(Base 10, +1 Part, +1 special effect)
