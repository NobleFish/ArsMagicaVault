Harvesting vis from a magical aura can potentially reduce the strength
of that aura (*Realms of Power: Magic*, page 10), and Hermetic
Viresculture is no different. If you are using the rules for Aura
Variation, then subtract one fifth (rounded up) of the total number of
pawns produced by Hermetic Viresculture in that aura from the Yearly
Aura Modifier.

**Yearly Aura Modifier for Viresculture: – (total pawns / 5)**
