The island is not obviously distinguished, at a distance, from any of
the other myriad small islands that clutter the coastal regions of the
Mediterranean; it is so small and undistinguished that if it has a
mundane name, only local fisher folk know it, and avoid it as an area
with bad weather.

The approach to this island is protected by Tempestas, a powerful spirit
of storms. If a ship or swimmer approaches the island, Tempestas raises
a storm sufficient to drive away or wreck any vessel not protected by
powerful magic. It is likely that those who come sailing up expecting a
quiet anchorage will find themselves cast upon the shore with minimal
equipment, separated from each other.

Tempestas is bound to serve as guardian by a Spell Binding in a carved
agate head in Fortunata's library.
