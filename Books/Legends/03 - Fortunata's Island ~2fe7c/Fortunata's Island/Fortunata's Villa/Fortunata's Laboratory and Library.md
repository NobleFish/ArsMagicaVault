Her laboratory is hidden in the core of the building, surrounded by
other rooms, with neither door nor window — magic is needed to enter.
The layout of the other rooms is such that the hidden chamber is not
obvious, though careful study or mapping will reveal the missing space.
Monocaudus is no help, never having entered; only Lucida knows the
secret.

The laboratory also contains her library, and is surprisingly sparse —
much of her work took place outdoors among spirits. If cleaned up, and
perishable stocks replaced, it could be used as a standard laboratory.
