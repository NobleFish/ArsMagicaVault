The Imagines, spirits of Illusion that haunt the island, such as Error
and Cantus (see this chapter, Cantus) often congregate in the villa,
enjoying the aura and teasing the mirrors; strangers draw attention as
play-things.

Lucida wanders the villa, passing through the library, laboratory, and
Fortunata's bedroom, and chastising Monocaudus wherever he can be found.

Monocaudus lurks in the kitchen by the fire, or resentfully makes fires
or token gestures of cleaning; he spends much of the day wandering
outdoors on the pretext of gathering firewood or food.
