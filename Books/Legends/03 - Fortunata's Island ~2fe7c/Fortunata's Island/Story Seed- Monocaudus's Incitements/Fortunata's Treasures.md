The laboratory hides the Spell Binding containers (see this chapter,
Bound Spirits) which maintain the *Coerce Spirit* spells which command
the guardians of the Island, including Tempestas, Lapidosus, and Fax.
The commands given when the *Coerce Spirit* spells were cast mean that a
possessor of the Spell Binding object can claim to be the lawful ruler
of that spirit.

The containers are sealed into the solid stone of the floor, by use of
Rego Terram to re-shape the stone around them (Intellego Vim magics
reveal their presence.)
