Scripts created by a "first generation" Mystagogue will often reflect
the Presence and Ability scores of that magus, and a new maga seeking to
follow in their footsteps may find the Script does not provide enough
Initiation Points. In this case, the script may be extended. To add a
further Quest (+3 Script Bonus) to an existing Initiation Script, the
Mystagogue must succeed in a test of Intelligence + (Mystery Cult) Lore
against an Ease Factor of 9 (see The Mysteries: Revised Edition, page
17).
