The castle contained a magician's laboratory, private library, and
sufficient supplies for his servants to live luxuriously. Many of
Thomae's possessions were destroyed in the crash or by the elements
afterward. His materials will not equip the multiple laboratories of
most covenants, but may provide useful pieces of enchanted laboratory
equipment, a collection of valuable books, or sufficient mundane wealth
to purchase useful supplies for the covenant. Storyguides might use
magical items from other scenarios in this book as treasures belonging
to Thomae.
