- Thomae was young, for a magus, when he disappeared, and many suspect
  he is still alive, abusing the natives in some distant part of Africa
  or Asia.
- Thomae was disinterested in politics–his obsession was flight.
- His castle was made of bronze.
- He was renowned as a slayer of magical beasts, and a magical
  mercenary.
- He was accused of provoking the mundanes by displaying his flying
  castle.
- Thomae traveled widely in his quest to find vis to pay for his castle,
  spending much time in the Holy Land and Africa.
- Thomae settled his tribunal case by agreeing not to fly his castle in
  civilized areas, but there was some question regarding his later
  compliance.
