Female hatchlings may be found in the lair of the griffons, or hatched
from intact eggs, at the discretion of the storyguide. Griffon
hatchlings are the size of a human, but much of that mass is taken up by
their wings, which are disproportionately large when compared to the
wings of mundane birds.

Juvenile griffons may be added to those faced during the combat section
of this scenario. Griffons may alloparent, like lions and some birds,
with older siblings aiding in the protection, feeding and education of
young animals. Female griffons might, in high fantasy campaigns, serve
as flying mounts for magi. Faeries may also use juvenile female griffons
as templates for their own mounts.

Adult female griffons are strongly territorial, so only one should
appear in any scenario.
