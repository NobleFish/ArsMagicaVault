R: Touch, D: Sun, T: Individual.\
This power is activated by the medallion's wearer speaking the word
"Thomae".

This effect allows the user to move at running pace through the air in
any direction he wishes. It can be reactivated an unlimited number of
times per day. Thomae's bodyguards developed a strange,
three-dimensional martial art based on the use of this power. The scent
of frying onions—sigil of the Verditius who created the medallion—
accompanies the character for the duration of the effect.

(Effect: Base 10, +1 Touch, +2 Sun; Modifications +10 unlimited uses per
day.)

The other medallions provide flight for two minutes, but at the speed of
a running horse.

(Base 15, +1 Touch, +1 Diameter; Modifications +10 unlimited uses per
day.)
