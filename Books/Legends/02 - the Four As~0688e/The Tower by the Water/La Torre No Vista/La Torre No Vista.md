Conciatta's sanctum occupied the upper levels of a tower near the banks
of the river Ebro. The tower fills a regio that hangs high in the air
with seemingly no way to reach it. As was revealed in Currito's book, he
always had a trouble with the birds that seemed to find his sanctum
windows.

At the covenant's founding, having acquire mundane title to the land,
Currito used ritual magic to raise a tower hidden inside the
already-present regio. At the same time, in the mundane world, he helped
his agents build wharfs and a warehouse to both disguise the entrance
and provide an important income.
