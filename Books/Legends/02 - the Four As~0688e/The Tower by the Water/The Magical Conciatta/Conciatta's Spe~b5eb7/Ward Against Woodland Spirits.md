ReHe(Vi) 41\
R: Touch, D: Ring, T: Circle

This experimental spell is a ward against all creatures with a
Herbam-based Might Score, irrespective of their supernatural realm, of
10 or less. The casting tablet has a penetration score built in, but
magi recreating this spell do so without this and at a level of 30.

Recreating this spell provides 9 points towards integrating *True
Understanding of the Realms*.

(Base 10, +4 Vi representing each of the Realms; +11 for penetration)
