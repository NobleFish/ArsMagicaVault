InVi 30\
R: Personal, D: Diameter, T: Vision

On casting this spell within a Magic aura, the maga sees a light
emanating from any natural or supernatural tether currently feeding and
binding the aura. The light is brighter the stronger the tether is,
allowing the maga to estimate the relative importance of that tether to
the aura.

(Base 5, +1 Diameter, +4 Vision)
