InVi 20\
R: Touch, D: Momentary, T: Room

Cast within an Infernal aura, this spell reveals whether actions taken
within this room were responsible for drawing in the Infernal. This
could be through a personal evil or sin, or an Infernal ritual. Any
event that induces a corruption roll (*Realms of Power: The Infernal*,
page 11) taints the location and is equivalent to a tether. This spell
cannot provide information about the evil that took place, nor even how
long ago it was committed.

(Base 5, +1 Touch, +2 Room)
