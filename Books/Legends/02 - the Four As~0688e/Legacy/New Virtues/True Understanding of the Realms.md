*Major, Hermetic*

When the maga achieves true understanding of the realms, represented by
this Virtue and through the four Realm Lore Abilities, she is able to
manipulate and use auras to better serve her magic.

On entering a supernatural aura foreign to the maga, the effective level
of that aura is reduced by the score in the appropriate Realm Lore
Ability to a minimum of 0. This reduces both the penalty associated with
that aura and the number of additional botch dice activities are subject
to. Note that some auras provide a bonus to spells and powers. In such
cases, the bonus is retained but the number of botch dice is reduced.
