The caves are ancient and contain deep fissures and domed chambers all
interconnected by mundane tunnels and a network of regiones. Most
regiones are linear in that they emanate from a single point and tend to
localize on that point while growing in strength. The Narixa regiones,
however, bleed into each other just as cave tunnels do.

The caves are home to swarms of mice, scorpions, and snakes, as well as
packs of hyena whose laughter can be heard echoing off the walls from
somewhere deep inside. Here and there, human bones jut painfully from
the walls, victims caught between a regio and the mundane world. The
caves are covered by a level 3 Malevolent Infernal aura and filled with
Tartaran regiones (*Realms of Power: The Infernal*, pages 15 and 16).
