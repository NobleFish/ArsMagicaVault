Assuming none of the four spirits have been destroyed in the journey (in
which case all perish at the same moment), the final chapter in
Conciatta's story is the reuniting of the four aspects.

On returning to the tower, the player characters should have the
scholarly Conciatta waiting for them, her Divine spirit subsumed within
a host, her Infernal aspect bound or captive in some form, and her
Faerie self in the form of a book.

The Infernal Conciatta is resentful and hateful towards the magi and her
other selves. She curses them with every other word and struggles to be
free. This shocks the Magical spirit, unaware she had such feelings
within her.
