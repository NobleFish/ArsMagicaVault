InVi 54<br />
Constant Effect<br />
R: Personal, D: Sun, T: Vision<br />
Each lens can be used to see through the apparent aura into the subsumed aura of the appropriate type, if such an aura is present. The image seen by looking through the lens shows the world as it would look if that aura were dominant. So a subsumed Infernal aura shows the potential for unnerving shadows, while a Magic aura may appear somewhat cleaner or more regular.

Although subsumed auras of any level can be detected, the enchantment is only strong enough to penetrate a level 3 apparent aura. Only the lowest level of each subsumed aura type is detected, so regiones cannot be directly perceived using this device.

(Base 20, +2 Sun, +4 Vision; +1 for two uses per day, +3 triggered at sunrise and sunset)

Recreating this device provides 11 points towards integrating ''Confluence of the Realms''.
