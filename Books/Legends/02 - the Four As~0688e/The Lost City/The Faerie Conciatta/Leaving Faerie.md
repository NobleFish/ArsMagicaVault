With agreements in place, the faerie Conciatta is ready to leave for the
mundane world. After an emotional yet dignified farewell to her King,
she begins the long walk back to the Threshold. The group meets Bruja
again and she gives the magi a book with a warning not to open it until
on the other side.

Bruja summons the Threshold and in an instant the Faerie Realm becomes a
memory. And Conciatta has vanished. In fact, her essence is now within
the book, a quality 7 tractatus on Faerie Lore written in somewhat
archaic Hermetic Latin.
