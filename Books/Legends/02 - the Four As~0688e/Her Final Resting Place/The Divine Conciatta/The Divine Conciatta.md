*Grant (Enfeebled)*, 0 points, Init 0, Vim: This power, used

Major Flaw (**ArM5**, page 53) on the target. (Ritual Power x2)

also operates just below the surface of the mind, beyond conscious
control. A character choosing to carry the spirit of Conciatta within
her does not have conscious access to that knowledge, but does suffer
the effects of her Personality Traits. She also gains the protection of
Conciatta's Divine might.
