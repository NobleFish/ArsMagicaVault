A magical frog asks a child apprentice for help in stopping the nearby
bulls from battling over control of their herd. The frog is worried that
the losing bulls will invade his pond and destroy his habitat. The bulls
are battling over one particular cow, a recent addition to the herd.
Removing the cow will stop the battle but anger the herd's owner. The
children can try to stop the battle or erect means to protect the frog's
pond from the vanquished bovines.
