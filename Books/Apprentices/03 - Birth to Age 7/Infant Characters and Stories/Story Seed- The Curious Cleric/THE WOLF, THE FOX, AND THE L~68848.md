A wounded wolf comes to the children characters seeking justice. He and
his companions, a fox and a lion, had agreed to go hunting together. He
caught a large rat, the fox caught a goose, and the lion a scrawny crow.
When it came time to divide the catches for dinner, the wolf suggested
that each predator eat what he caught. The lion responded by striking
the wolf. The fox then suggested that the lion take it all, and leave
the other two leftovers. The lion agreed. The wolf slunk off, and now
asks the characters to punish the sycophant fox and retrieve the large
rat from the lion.
