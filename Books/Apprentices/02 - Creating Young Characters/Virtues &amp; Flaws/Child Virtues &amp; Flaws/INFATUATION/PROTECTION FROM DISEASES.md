*Major Virtue, Child, Supernatural*

Some supernatural agent — Divine, Infernal, Faerie, or Magic has granted
the character immunity from all diseases. The character can still suffer
wounds and other damage, but diseases do not affect him. The reason for
such protection could be benevolent, malicious, or accidental, depending
on the creature bestowing the protection and its future plans.
