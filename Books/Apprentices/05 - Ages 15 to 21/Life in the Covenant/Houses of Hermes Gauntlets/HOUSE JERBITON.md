Apprentices of House Jerbiton participate in a Grand Tour before their
Gauntlet, visiting a large city and viewing its beautiful artifacts. In
anticipation of this event, a Jerbiton apprentice would be interested in
visiting nearby monasteries, nunneries, or noble houses to view any
available objects of art. Rumors of a beautiful chalice or woven
tapestry could easily divert a Jerbiton apprentice from his current
studies, and make him gather his friends for an overland journey.
