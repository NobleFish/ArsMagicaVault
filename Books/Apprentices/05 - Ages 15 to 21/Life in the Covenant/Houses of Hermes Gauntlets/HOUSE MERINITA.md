The Gauntlets for House Merinita magi are as varied as the individual
members. In broad strokes, the House is interested in either faerie or
nature. An apprentice's Gauntlet typically requires her to wander
ancient faerie roads and lonely forests, looking for specific faeries,
links between the Magic and Faerie realms, and penetrating deeply hidden
faerie regiones.
