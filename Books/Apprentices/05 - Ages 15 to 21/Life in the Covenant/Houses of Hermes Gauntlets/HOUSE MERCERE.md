Redcaps of House Mercere, as well as the few actual spell casters, must
carry a message from one location to another to pass their Gauntlet.
This is never straightforward, and the master always includes some trick
or dangerous element in the task. For example, the apprentice must carry
a faerie to a nearby covenant, since only the faerie knows the message.
The faerie is devious and quickly disappears, forcing the apprentice to
find it if she wishes to pass her Gauntlet.
