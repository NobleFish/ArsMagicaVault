Once the Arts are Opened, the apprentice character has a 0 in each of
the five Techniques and ten Forms. She can immediately cast spontaneous
spells. She can read any casting tablets the covenant may have,
providing she has a score of 1 in Artes Liberales and 4 in Latin.
Barring some logical limitations — the apprentice can't cast formulaic
spells because she doesn't know any — an apprentice can undertake any
magical activity a magus can. Simply use the applicable formula based on
the current Arts scores of the apprentice for any magical activity she
undertakes.
