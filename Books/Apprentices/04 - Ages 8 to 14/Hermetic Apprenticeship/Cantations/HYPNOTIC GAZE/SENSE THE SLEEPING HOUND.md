R: Voice, D: Mom, T: Ind

This spell indicates to the caster if an animal within range is asleep.
Care must be taken that the vocalization of this spell doesn't wake the
beast, which is good practice in determining how loudly a caster has to
speak in specific spell-casting situations.

(Base 3, +2 Voice)
