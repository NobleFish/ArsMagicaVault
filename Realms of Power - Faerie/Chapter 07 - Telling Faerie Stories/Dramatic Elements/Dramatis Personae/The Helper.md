The helper assists the hero; unlike the donor, the helper is always well
inclined towards the hero, and his motives are pure. The help provided
is always timely and perfectly suited to the story. The helper assists
the hero in traveling between locations, reverses his ill fortune,
rescues him from pursuit, or assists him in his difficult tasks.

Examples: Hermes who shows Odysseus the moly plant; the Grey Wolf (in
Prince Ivan)
