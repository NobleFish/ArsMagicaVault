The donor grants the hero a magical gift or power that is vital to the
execution of his task. However, the donor may not necessarily be kindly
disposed towards the hero or acting out of altruism; the hero often has
to force the donor to provide the magical aid by completing a task or
defeating him in combat.

Examples: Fafnir (in the Saga of the Nibelungs); Ceridwen (in the story
of Gwion Bach, the witch whose cauldron gives him magical powers)
