The hero is sometimes not recognized for his deeds. Instead, a false
hero (who may also be the villain) claims credit for the ordeals passed
by the hero. However, in the predestined manner of the fairy tale, the
hero always has some means to positively identify himself as the hero,
and expose the false hero to receive the punishment he deserves. This
identifying token may be the wound he suffered in his struggle against
the villain, a token he took from the villain, or a token he received
from his princess.
