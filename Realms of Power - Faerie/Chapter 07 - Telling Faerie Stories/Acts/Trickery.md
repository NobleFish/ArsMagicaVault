The villain deceives his intended victim in order to take possession of
him or of his belongings. In fairy tales, such trickery is almost always
fallen for. The villain may employ a number of means to gain control of
the hero — persuasion, magic, or force. But there need not be any active
trickery on behalf of the villain; for example, the hero may fall asleep
and thereby put himself into the hands of the villain.
