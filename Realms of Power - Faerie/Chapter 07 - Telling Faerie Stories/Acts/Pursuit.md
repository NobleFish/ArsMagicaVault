The journey home is not always a simple one for the hero; sometimes he
is pursued. The villain, if not killed during the struggle with the
hero, is usually the pursuer. However, it might be some guardian or
compatriot of the villain who takes the role of pursuer. The hero who is
pursued never simply outruns his pursuer; rather, he is rescued from
pursuit by employing the magical agent received from the donor, by the
helper, or by the object of his quest.
