One of the favorite elements of the fairy tale is the requirement that
the hero perform some difficult task. The setter of the ordeal is not
always the villain, though. For example, a hero might have to prove his
worth to a king to obtain permission to marry his daughter. Some typical
examples:

- Riddle setting or guessing;
- Choice (the hero must select his prize from among twelve identical
  objects);
- Ordeal by food and drink (the hero must consume a vast amount of food
  or drink);
- Hide and seek (the hero must hide himself from the task-setter);
- Test of strength, adroitness, or fortitude;
- Supply or manufacture (the hero must make a complex object in a single
  night, or obtain a mythical prize).
