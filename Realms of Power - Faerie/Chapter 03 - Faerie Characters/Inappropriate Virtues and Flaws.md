Faeries may not have supernatural powers associated with the Divine,
Infernal, or Magic realms.

Faeries may not have The Gift, or any Virtue or Flaw specific to
Hermetic magi.

Faeries do not have human Social Status virtues. If the faerie has a
role that makes it appear part of human society, it should take the
Infiltrator Virtue.

Faeries do not have any human Virtue linked with having an appropriate
upbringing or life experience.

Many faeries are religious, and many more are amoral, but Virtues and
Flaws linked to the Divine and Infernal should be taken only after close
consultation with the Troupe. However, as noted above, Virtues and Flaws
that grant Divine or Infernal powers are not permitted.
