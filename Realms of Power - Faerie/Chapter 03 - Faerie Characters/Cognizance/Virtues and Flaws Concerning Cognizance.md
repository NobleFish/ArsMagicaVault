All fairies must have one of the following levels of cognizance.

<span id="Incognizant"></span>**Incognizant** Minor Flaw, Supernatural

The character is not aware that it can change its role, although it may
develop additional Pretenses by assisting humans in stories. Incognizant
faeries do not, however, notice that they are more skilled than they
once were, and cannot consciously seek developmental opportunities.

<span id="Narrowly Cognizant"></span>**Narrowly Cognizant** Free Choice,
Supernatural

The character is able to improve itself in ways traditional for
characters of this type. A dwarf may seek to become a dwarf king, for
example. Or a faerie maid may wish to steal a child to raise as her own.

<span id="Highly Cognizant"></span>**Highly Cognizant** Minor Virtue,
Supernatural

The character is aware that creative humans can generate change in
faeries, and can actively seek out such changes.
