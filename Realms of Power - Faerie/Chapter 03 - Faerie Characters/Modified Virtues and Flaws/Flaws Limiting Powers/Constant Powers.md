A Greater, Lesser, or Personal Power can be made constant, triggering
automatically at both sunset and sunrise and continuing perpetually as
long as the character has a Might Score and the Might Points needed to
activate it. The effect must be designed with Sun duration, and costs an
additional magnitude. If the power has a Might cost, it is subtracted
from the character’s Might Pool each time the sun rises or sets, whether
the character is aware of it or not. If the character does not have
enough Might Points, the power is temporarily interrupted until the next
activation. \|}
