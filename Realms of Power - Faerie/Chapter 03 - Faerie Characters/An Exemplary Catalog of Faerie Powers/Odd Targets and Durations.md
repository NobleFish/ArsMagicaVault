There are few examples in this book, because it is intended to stand
alone, but faeries may use any of the Ranges, Targets, or Durations
available to Merinita magi in Houses of Hermes: Mystery Cults. One that
is used repeatedly in this book is Until, a Duration that makes a spell
last until a certain thing occurs. In this book it is treated as being
equivalent to Year, but not requiring a Ritual. This is substantially
more powerful than Merinita magic, and is a field of interested study by
magi of that House. In some examples, two Durations are given. For
example, a power might be marked as Sun or Until. In that case, the
power’s spell level is treated as Sun duration, and the power expires if
either the Sun Duration is completed, or the condition of the Until
duration is met.

The conditions of Until durations designed by players need to make
narrative sense, and troupes should sternly enforce them.
