Manifests the faerie’s consciousness among animal and plant life its
glamour touches, temporarily controlling their actions. This power
allows the character to simulate any Creo Animal, Rego Animal, Creo
Herbam, or Rego Herbam effect with a value of 25 spell levels or less,
targeted at the animals within its glamour and suiting its motif. A lord
of a wild hunt, for example, may manifest through the wolves of a
forest, but would not manifest through squirrels or trees.
