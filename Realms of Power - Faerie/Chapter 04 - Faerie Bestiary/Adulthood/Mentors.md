A faerie mentor allows the character to move from his immature,
adolescent self to his potent adult self. Mentors are difficult to find,
and often initially reject the character, forcing him to prove his
suitability for training through arduous tasks. The mentor then provides
the wisdom and power necessary to complete the task that allows the
character to take his adult place in society. The mentor may also
provide the student with a symbolic gift that suits the challenge faced,
like a sword, or magical fruit that produce jewels when broken open.
