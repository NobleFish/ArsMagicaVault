Many faeries expect humans to kill them. The faeries gain vitality from
this process, but for the story to be complete, they must not use the
same role until sufficient time has passed for their actions to
constitute a new story. So, a faerie knight known for doing battle every
midsummer’s day may die in combat once a year, but the giant from Jack
and the Beanstalk cannot return to Jack’s country in its original form.
Perhaps it returns as the giant’s wife, or son, or brother, or chooses
to go to a different kingdom, and tell the beanstalk story again with a
new boy.''
