Lesser faeries can be designed as player characters. Many have the Place
of Power Virtue. These faeries have the roles of minor nobles, or their
attendants. Courtly faeries also often have faerie animals as servants.
Most courts have animals that suit mortal nobility, like horses, hounds,
and hawks, but the court of the Lady has horses, eels, and gulls. Some
ancient Roman writers talk of “mimic dogs,” which were creatures that
could be taught to repeat human actions. These were probably a type of
servant faerie that has been destroyed by the coming of the Dominion.
