For a faerie to challenge the power of a group of senior magi, unaided,
it requires a Faerie Might score of around 60, and the Penetration
Pretense, to allow it to project its powers through the Parma Magica.
Monarchs of this highest degree of power are rare, and many have a Might
score of 40. These can challenge magi in their middle years, if aided by
a court of less-powerful beings.
