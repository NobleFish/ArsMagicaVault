Ancient sites of pagan worship often have faerie auras, because the
faeries that dwell in them refuse to leave, even when humans ignore
them. These faeries may be incognizant, and therefore unable to
understand that they can leave. Some trapped faeries are narrowly
cognizant, and are only waiting for a human to come and conclude their
story. A few are highly cognizant, but enjoy their current role so much
that rather than relinquish it, they are willing to wait for a human to
arrive so that they can attempt to modify it.
