Anchors are mundane objects into which faeries have settled their
glamour — the cores around which faeries agglomerate bodies. When a
faerie’s body is destroyed unexpectedly, it is this anchor that magi
prize for its vis. Some anchors are distant from the body they motivate,
while other types allow the bearer to access one of a faerie’s powers.
Each of these unusual anchor types is found as a Virtue in Chapter 3:
Faerie Characters. Highly cognizant faeries understand what anchors are,
and may bargain for them with magi.

A faerie remains conscious, and in its current role, while trapped in
its anchor. But it is unable to use its powers, other than the ability
to draw matter to produce a new body. Characters may communicate with a
faerie trapped in its anchor using Intellego Vim spells.

The connection between the faerie and its anchor is enforced by glamour,
and can be broken if the faerie’s glamour is damaged. For example, if
the faerie’s anchor breaks a taboo — represented by a Ward Flaw — the
faerie’s role breaks and it fades from the mortal world. This is
particularly fortunate for some faeries, who are forbidden from straying
from their lands or are unable to enter the Dominion, because it allows
them to escape to Faerieland before magi destroy them permanently.
Anchors whose faeries break taboo become accrued vis.
