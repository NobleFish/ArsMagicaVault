An interesting twist on the Elysian story is for the characters to adopt
a role other than that of the hero. For most stories, the only
appropriate dramatis personae other than the hero are the roles of
villain and princess — the remaining minor roles do not offer sufficient
scope for stories.

For example, in the story of Orpheus and Eurydice, the characters could
take the role of Hades. They must ensure that Orpheus is sorely tested
but succeeds, and also make sure that he looks back at just the right
moment so that Eurydice is still lost to him. \|}
