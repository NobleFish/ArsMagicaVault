The transmission of ancient Greek legends to the medieval period is far
from complete, and only educated men are familiar with them. And even
then, such stories are only commonly encountered as counter-examples to
a good, Christian life. The later history of Rome — that is, outside of
the legendary period and into the history books — also provides a wealth
of stories.

- The Sorrows and Labors of Hercules
- The Sack of Troy
- Theseus in the Underworld
- Prometheus, Epimetheus, and Pandora
- The Flight of Icarus
- Romulus and Remus
