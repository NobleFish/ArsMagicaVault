Path of Chance: The literal interpretation of the story element, as most
commonly discovered in Arcadia. Each story element has a link to the
previous or next one.

Path of Destiny: The mythological or metaphysical interpretation of he
story element, as found along the many roads of Elysium.

Forking Path: The figurative interpretation of the story element,
leading to one of two (or more) choices.

The story elements have been divided into three separate categories —
Actors, Props, and Scenery. Any element in one of these three categories
can take one of the roles described later (see Chapter 7: Telling Faerie
Stories, Dramatis Personae), and/or become involved in one or more of
the scenes (see Chapter 7: Telling Faerie Stories, Acts).
