Greater Faerie Power, variable points, Init 0, Vim

The faerie can add Threshold points to the total accumulated by a mortal
individual or group, at a cost of 1 Might Point per Threshold point. The
characters affected by this power must already have a Threshold total;
the faerie cannot be the only source of Threshold points. The characters
must still face the Guardian of the Threshold (see later) — who may
indeed by the faerie with this power — but if the faerie wants them to
enter the Faerie Realm, then it may well indicate the right path. This
power can also be used to send characters away from the Faerie Realm as
well; this costs 3 Might Points per character. \|}
