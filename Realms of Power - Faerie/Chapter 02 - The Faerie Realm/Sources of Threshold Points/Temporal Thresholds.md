Certain times of year mark Thresholds in time, such as the turning of
the seasons. At these times, the border between the mundane world and
Faerie becomes thinner and more easily traversed. Such times are worth a
number of Threshold points determined by the table. Note that if the
characters are potentially crossing into Faerie from a Faerie Aura, they
may benefit twice from the time of year, since the Faerie aura may be
increased, thus decreasing the Threshold strength as well as adding
Threshold points.

| Time            | Threshold Points |
|-----------------|------------------|
| New Moon        | 1                |
| Full Moon       | 3                |
| Pagan Holiday\* | 3                |
| Solstice        | 6                |
| Equinox         | 9                |

\* If observed locally''
