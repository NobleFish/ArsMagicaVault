The huldra (plural huldrene) is a variety of nymph native to
Scandinavian lands, also known as a skogsrå or tallemaja. The rarer male
form is called a huldu or huldrekall. They are often exceedingly
beautiful from the front, although their backs are hollow like a canoe.
Huldrene never wear clothes, and have the tail of a cow. Their most
distinctive feature is their power to disappear from sight and remain
hidden when no-one is watching. A man can unknowingly marry a huldra and
raise children with her, but she always eventually leaves him when he
discovers her secret. Some huldrene are sexual predators, rewarding
those who please them and killing those who disappoint. Others are
guardians of morality; if a man is unfaithful to his wife with a huldra,
then the faerie punishes him by causing him to father huldrablooded
children.

- Benefits: When she remains still, the character cannot be seen. She
  must concentrate to maintain the absolute stillness this requires;
  make a Stamina + Concentration roll against an Ease Factor of 9 every
  fifteen minutes. She cannot use this power if anyone is aware of her
  location, or if in a Divine aura.
- Legacy: As above, except that the character can disappear even when
  being watched.
- Sympathies: Concealment, Fidelity, Secrets (negative).
- Other Virtues and Flaws: Ways of the Forest; Great Presence, Puissant
  Stealth; Reclusive.

Appearance: Cow’s tail, long blonde hair, unearthly beauty, hollow from
behind.
