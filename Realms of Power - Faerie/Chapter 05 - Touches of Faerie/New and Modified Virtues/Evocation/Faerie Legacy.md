Minor, Supernatural

This Virtue can only be taken by characters with Faerie Blood or Strong
Faerie Blood. Your Faerie Blood is particularly potent, and the usual
benefit deriving from it is replaced with a more powerful version. See
Varieties of Faerie Blood (earlier) for details on how the benefit is
affected. Note that the legacy benefit from this Virtue is instead of
the normal benefit, not in addition to it.
