Major or Minor, Supernatural

The character receives a positive Sympathy Trait. As a Major Virtue,
this has an initial score of +3; as a Minor Virtue it is +1. This Trait
may be increased with experience points, up to a maximum score of (the
initial value + the character’s Warping Score). For example, a character
with Warping Score 1 and the Major Virtue could begin with a +4 Trait.
Players with this Virtue may freely increase their character’s Warping
Score during character creation, though they should have some idea of
what in the character’s past caused this Warping to occur. This Virtue
may be taken more than once. See Sympathy, earlier, for more information
about Sympathy Traits.
