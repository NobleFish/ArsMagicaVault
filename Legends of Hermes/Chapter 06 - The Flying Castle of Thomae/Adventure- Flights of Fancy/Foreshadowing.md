For the adventure in this chapter to be slotted seamlessly into a saga,
the storyguide should prepare the players by foreshadowing two elements
in earlier stories. Thomae himself might be mentioned, in passing.
Characters might find art depicting his castle, or he may be referenced
in a legal case. The players should also know a little about griffons.
They may, for example, visit an ancient covenant that possesses the
talisman of an archmage, a staff that ends in an agate the size of an
ostrich egg, and learn that this was laid by a griffon. Griffons also
appear in artwork and so may be slipped into other stories as decorative
elements.
