The wrecked castle is made of enormous amounts of bronze. Thomae chose
bronze because a superficial layer of corrosion stops bronze
deteriorating further, unlike iron or steel. Bronze can be sold for
between 200 and 300 Mythic pounds per ton, but if reworked into
high-class brassware (a term which includes bronze, in Mythic Europe) it
can sell for as much as 1,500 Mythic pounds per ton. Characters wanting
to sell the bronze will encounter problems of weight, remoteness and
market flooding, rather than scarcity.
