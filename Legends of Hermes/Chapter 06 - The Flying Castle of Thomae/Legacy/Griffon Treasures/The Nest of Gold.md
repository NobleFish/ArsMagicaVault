The nest of gold is worth a fortune, but to a skilled enchanter, it is
even more valuable as a focus for enchantment. It adds +7 to
enchantments which favor the defense of home, or the protection of
treasures. To be used as a focus for enchantment, the nest — which
contains pounds of gold, griffon feathers, and the woods of exotic trees
— must be moved into a laboratory without damaging its basic structure.
Careful manual handling, skilled goldsmithing to solder the pieces
together, or magic is required to keep the nest intact until it can be
stabilized and enchanted. An intact nest may also be required for
incubated griffon eggs to hatch.
