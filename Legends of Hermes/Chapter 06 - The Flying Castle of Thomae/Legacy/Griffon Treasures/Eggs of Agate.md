Griffons' eggs are perfect agates the size of ostrich eggs. Agates are
semiprecious stones that may be used for a variety of purposes. Ancient
writers note that an agate placed in the mouth prevents thirst.
Sorcerers have, since Roman times, used agates in the control of rivers
and storms. Agate is linked to Auram magic (+3), protection from storms
(+5), and protection from venom (+7).
