Boundary effects are not, by their nature, mobile. This means that, for
example, if the castle had a Boundary ward against magical creatures, it
would leave the effect behind as it flew. Similarly, an Aegis of the
Hearth ritual cast around the edge of the castle would also be left
behind as the castle moved. This lack of effective wards for huge,
moving objects was a known risk. Thomae believed few creatures would
attack a magical flying castle filled with offensive magical items and
well-trained soldiers.
