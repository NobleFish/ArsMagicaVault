When Conciatta's husband Currito left the covenant he took a number of
useful texts and devices. It is possible that some of her work has made
it to the troupe's covenant over the years. Such books or devices can be
presented to the troupe as obscure items in their library or stores.

Also, works by others may contain Conciatta's own glossing, and these
hint at her greater understanding of the realms, or individual
correspondences may be found within other volumes. Any time a character
studies the Art of Vim from a book there is an opportunity to include
Conciatta's name in the experience.
