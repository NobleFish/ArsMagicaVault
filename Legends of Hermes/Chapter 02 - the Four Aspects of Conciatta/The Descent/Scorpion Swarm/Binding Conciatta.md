Conciatta will not willingly surrender and is prepared to fight to the
death. Her high Might Score means that binding her through wards is a
difficult prospect. There is no easy answer to capturing and binding
Conciatta and troupes are encouraged to be creative. But there are some
options:

- Holy relics affect Conciatta as she is a demon.
- Reducing her Might through Perdo Vim magic will allow her to be bound
  more easily.
- As a being of fire and heat, intense cold will cause her harm and may
  force her to submit.
