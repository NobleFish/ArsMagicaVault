A small fleet of fishing boats launch daily from the white beaches,
while teams of fishermen cast their nets from the shores. Moving inland,
the markets are full with fresh fish, livestock grazed on the hillsides,
and fruits, grains, and other foodstuffs brought in from neighboring
towns. In the mercantile quarter, all manner of merchants can be found
trading Narixa silks, oriental spices, and goods from across Europe. The
sounds of commerce mingle with street players and the periodic chants of
the *mu'adhdhins* calling the faithful to prayer.

This far into Al Andalus, there are few *dhimmis* (non-Muslims living
under Muslim rule) but peoples from across the Islamic world are
represented here. And the skyline is filled with the minarets of the
many mosques that serve the town.
