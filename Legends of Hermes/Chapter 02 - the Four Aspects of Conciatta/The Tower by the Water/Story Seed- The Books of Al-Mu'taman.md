Yusuf ibn Ahmad al-Mu'taman ibn Hud ruled Zaragoza between 1082 and
1085. Aside from his secular concerns he was an accomplished
mathematician, writing extensively on geometry. However, those reading
his works also discover references to magic.

Al-Mu'taman was a Learned Magician (*Hedge Magic: Revised Edition*, page
79) and his lineage schooled Conciatta in their ways of overcoming the
deleterious effects of auras. Modern magi might also learn much from
this line of magicians, if they can be found and bargained with.
