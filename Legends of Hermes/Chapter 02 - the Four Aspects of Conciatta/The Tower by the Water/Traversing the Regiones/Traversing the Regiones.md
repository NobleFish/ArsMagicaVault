As per the rules in **ArM5**, page 189, it is possible for a character
to see from an aura of one realm into a regio of different realm. To
enter the level 6 Magic regio directly from within the level 2 Dominion
requires a Perception + Second Sight simple roll against an Ease Factor
of 5 + (2 x (2 + 6)) = 21. The Dominion aura also directly penalizes any
non-Divine power used to detect the boundary.

Once the ritual has been cast and the subsumed Magic aura has been made
apparent, it is much easier to traverse the regiones, requiring a
Perception + Second Sight simple roll against an Ease Factor of 5 + (2 x
(6 – 1)) = 15.
