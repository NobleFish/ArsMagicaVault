Leaving Conciatta's spirit within Immaculada is good for neither of
them, and if Prudencia can be persuaded of this she allows the magi to
try to separate the two; the abbess will not allow suffering to continue
if there is a way to end it. It is Immaculada's fear of being alone that
binds Conciatta to her. If this can be abated, the spirit can freely
move into another host. It is left to individual troupes to decide how
this can be achieved.

The new host suffers both the Enfeebled and Visions Flaws while
possessed by Conciatta, though the storyguide is free to use the Visions
to provide further information and/or direction in later stages of
Conciatta's story. These visions could include seeing Conciatta as a
queen, or wreathed in serpents, or sitting in the heart of a great fire.
They may also include visions of a great shining city sinking beneath a
mighty river.
