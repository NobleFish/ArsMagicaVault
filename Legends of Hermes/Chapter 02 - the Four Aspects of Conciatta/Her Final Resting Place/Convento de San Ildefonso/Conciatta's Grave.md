Conciatta has a small simple grave in the convent's cemetery,
identifiable by a small headstone bearing her name. However, according
to convent tradition, upon her death, the novice who last carried
Conciatta's spirit is buried here. Conciatta's bones, like those of all
subsequent hosts, have been moved to the ossuary. There is no record of
which bones are hers.
