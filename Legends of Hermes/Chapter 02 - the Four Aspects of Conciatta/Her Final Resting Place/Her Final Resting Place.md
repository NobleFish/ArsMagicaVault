A short distance from the city of Toledo sits a convent dedicated to
Saint Ildefonso of Toledo. It was here that Conciatta came looking for
the Divine when engaged in her studies so many years ago and it was here
she returned when her magic overcame her. But the spirit that now dwells
within the convent may not be what those who come looking for Conciatta
expect.

Characters searching for Conciatta here face two distinct challenges.
The first is to gain access to Conciatta, either persuading the
protective abbess or by taking more direct actions. The second is facing
up to what they find and deciding how best to deal with the spirit.
