ReVi General\
R: Touch, D: Year, T: Boundary, Ritual\
Causes the apparent Infernal aura of strength equal to (ritual magnitude
– 9) to abate. The Infernal aura, though still present, is held at bay
and its effects are not felt. Other auras present that may have been
suppressed are now expressed.

(Base, +1 Touch, +4 Year, +4 Boundary)
