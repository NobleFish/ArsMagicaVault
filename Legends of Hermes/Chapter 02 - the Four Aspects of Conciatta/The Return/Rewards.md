Storyguides who wish to add linking stories into the larger quest should
set the rewards as appropriate. But it is suggested that 5 experience
points are awarded for the events surrounding each of the four spirits
of Conciatta, for a total of 20 across the story.

Sections set within the Magic and Faerie Realms also carry their own
rewards. Experience earned in the Magic Realm manifests as vis (see
*Realms of Power: Magic*, pages 20 and 26), which can be used to
transform the character, allowing them to gain or exchange Virtues.
Similarly, characters leaving the Faerie Realm may also acquire new
Virtues. In this case, the character given the book gains one of
Conciatta's breakthroughs as a charm (see *Realms of Power: Faerie*,
pages 39 and 104). The Virtue can be used while the character carries
the book, so long as he keeps the charm secret.
