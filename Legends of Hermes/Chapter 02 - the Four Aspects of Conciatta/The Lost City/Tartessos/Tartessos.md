Tartessos was once a powerful trading city of the ancient world. It
supplied Greece with silver to fund defenses against the Persians,
traded with the Tin Islands to the far north, and with the Phoenicians
in the east. And through this time, Arganthonios was its absolute king.
But a great calamity befell it and the city was lost, sinking beneath
the sea in a single day.

The city can now only be found in the Faerie Realm, accessible from the
marshlands at the mouth of the Guadalquivir river.
