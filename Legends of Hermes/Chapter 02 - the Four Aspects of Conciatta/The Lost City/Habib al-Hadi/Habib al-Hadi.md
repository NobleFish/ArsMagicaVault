Habib is a scholar, cleric, astronomer, translator, and teacher. In
fact, given his perpetually poor finances, he takes on any job that is
asked of him so that he can maintain a roof over his head, food in his
belly, and parchment for his studies – but not always in that order. He
has an inquisitive mind and is fascinated by the sights he has recently
seen. He may be tempted to part with the magical device in his custody,
but only if his study of supernatural Qadis can be supported in other
ways.
