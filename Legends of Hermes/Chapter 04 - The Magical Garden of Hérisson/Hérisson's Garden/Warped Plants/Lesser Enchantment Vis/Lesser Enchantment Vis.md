All these types of Lesser Enchantment Vis were made into Stirpes by
Hérisson's Hermetic Viresculture. However, they have exhausted their
invigoration, and now only produce a single Stirpes each generation.
Characters must duplicate Hérisson's Minor Breakthrough before they can
be Invigorated again.
