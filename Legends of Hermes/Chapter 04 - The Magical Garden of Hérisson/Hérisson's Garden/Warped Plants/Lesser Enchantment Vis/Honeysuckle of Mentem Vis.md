Rego Mentem 24\
Pen 0, constant effect\
R: Per, D: Sun, T: Scent

A vine covered in hooked yellow flowers with a strong scent. This
plant's fragrance affects everyone who smells it with an effect
identical to *Confusion of the Numbed Will*. The perfume extends about 6
paces from the blossoms of the vine.

(Base 4, +2 Sun, +2 Scent; +1 2/day, +3 environmental trigger)
