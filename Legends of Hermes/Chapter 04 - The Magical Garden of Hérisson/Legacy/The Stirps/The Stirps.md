The foundation of any vis-growing enterprise using Hermetic Viresculture
is the Stirps. This is the first individual that both contains usable
vis and is capable of reproduction, and is the founder of a given
cultivar. The Stirps is usually a living being, but Hérisson
successfully created a Stirps from a tiny fire elemental, since fire is
an element that naturally spreads. The Story portion of this chapter
culminates in the characters discovering several Stirpes created by
Hérisson, but anyone who develops a score in Hermetic Viresculture can
make her own.
