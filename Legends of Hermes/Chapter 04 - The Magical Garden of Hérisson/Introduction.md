# Chapter Four: The Magical Garden of Hérisson

**Lifetime:** c. 857 AD – 1002 AD

**Overview:** An advocate of peace slain on the eve of the Schism War,
whose pacifist teachings changed the philosophy of House Bjornaer. His
magical garden hosts a war of its own even today, as well as the secret
of growing vis.

One of the legends of House Bjornaer was the magus Hérisson. Quite apart
from his political importance to his followers, Hérisson was a theorist
and experimenter who discovered a method for growing and multiplying
vis. When the player characters stumble across the results of his
experimentation, they can follow a trail of clues to his garden, a
magical paradise filled with remarkable magical plants and the means to
recreate his method of Hermetic vis production.
