Cantus is an example of one of the many Imagines that haunt the island. It wanders freely, not bound by Fortunata — it may be one of the native &quot;sounds which fill the island&quot; which Fortunata mentions in her writings.

'''Magic Might:''' 5 (Imaginem)

'''Season:''' Spring

'''Characteristics:''' Int 0, Per +3, Pre –2, Com +4, Str –3, Sta +1, Dex 0, Qik +2

'''Size:''' —

'''Virtues and Flaws:''' Magic Spirit.

'''Magical Qualities and Inferiorities:''' Greater Power (Music of the Spheres).

'''Personality Traits:''' Guileful +3

'''Abilities:''' Awareness 3 (alertness), Finesse 3 (illusion), Music 3 (illusion), Island Lore 2 (inhabitants), Magic Lore 2 (spirits)

'''Powers:'''

''Music of the Spheres'', 0 points, Init +1, Imaginem<br />
R: Voice, D: Conc, T: Group<br />
Cantus can create music (or less complex sounds), from one to ten voices. It is fond of bell chimes, and plaintive melodies.

CrIm 25 (Base 1, +2 Voice, +1 Conc, +2 Group, +2 move at command, +1 intricacy), Greater Power (25 levels, -3 Might, +2 Init)

'''Vis:''' 1 Imaginem, in a chime which falls to the ground.

'''Appearance:''' To those with Second Sight or similar abilities, Cantus appears as a set of chime bells or as some specific instrument it is sounding. It always sounds sweet and comely.
