ReVi Gen<br />
R: Touch, D: Special, T: Ind, Ritual<br />
Req: Form or Forms of the spell to be sustained.<br />
Allows another spell to be entwined with this spell and bound to a physical container. The base level of this spell must equal or exceed the level of the spell to be sustained. Casting of the sustained spell must begin while this spell lasts, or the next round for Momentary duration, after which the two spells are entwined and only end after both durations expire. If a spirit of Might at least equal to the sustained spell is forced into a container while the spell is still active, then the spell is sustained indefinitely while the spirit is bound.
