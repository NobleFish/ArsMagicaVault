Even if not following an ordained sequence of Initiations, it is
important to record the Ordeals suffered by the Initiate. These not only
provide a Script Bonus to the Initiation specifying the Ordeal, but also
reduce the Initiation Total required for the next three Initiations, no
matter what they are.

This in turn reduces the needed Script Bonus, which makes experimental
scripts and similar activities easier, so some scripts are designed to
follow previous Ordeals. This is shown in Fortunata's use of two
separate Scripts to obtain the Virtue Major Magical Focus, Initiating
the Minor version as preparation.
