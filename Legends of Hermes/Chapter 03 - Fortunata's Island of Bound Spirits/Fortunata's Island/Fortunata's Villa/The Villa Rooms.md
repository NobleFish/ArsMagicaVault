Most rooms stand open — entrance hall, kitchen, and stores, and the many
store-, function- and bed-rooms, including Fortunata's own. A corridor
snakes through the building, interrupted by some of the rooms; many
rooms have connecting doors. External doorways and those of larger rooms
are decorated with pillars molded with Hermetic motifs.

The finest rooms, such as Fortunata's bedchamber and dining room, have
mosaic floors and painted plaster walls, according to designs from
classical Rome recounted by summoned ghosts. Sadly, many of these have
been damaged by time and careless treatment.
