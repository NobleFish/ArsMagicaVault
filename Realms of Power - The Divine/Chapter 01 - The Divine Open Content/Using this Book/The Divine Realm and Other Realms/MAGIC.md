If the erudite Hermetic researchers are correct, then the Magic realm is
intimately connected to the power and goodness of the Divine. Certainly,
some powerful magical beings resemble angels, and may have once even
been Divine. Divine beings, such as angels, tend to have little to do
with Magic; relationships are distant but not necessarily hostile. Due
to their great power, the sovereigns of the Magic realm are often
associated with the sin of Pride, and occasionally encourage
sacrilegious worship of themselves or their auras. For these reasons,
the Divine seeks to remind the Magic realm of humility, restricting its
might while in holy areas.
