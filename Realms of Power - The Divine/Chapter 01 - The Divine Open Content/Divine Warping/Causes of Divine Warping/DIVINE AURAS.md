Large churches and cathedrals are the most common causes of Divine
Warping in Mythic Europe. However, many of the people who live and pray
in these places also possess Faith Points or mystical abilities that
prevent them from gaining Warping Points from the church's powerful
aura. Auras of any kind with a level over 5 are very rare in Mythic
Europe, and the majority of them are located around the small area of a
church's altar or similar holy place.
