*Minor, General*

You are a non-Muslim living under Muslim rule. If you are adult and
male, you must pay the *jizya* head tax, and all *dhimmis* must abide by
certain social restrictions (described above), in return for which they
receive tolerance and protection from the Muslim authorities. The actual
amount a *dhimmi* has to pay in *jizya* varies, but rarely exceeds a
*dinar* (a gold coin) per year.
