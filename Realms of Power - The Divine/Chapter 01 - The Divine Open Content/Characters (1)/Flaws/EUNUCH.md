*Minor, General*

You are a eunuch, and so are unable to procreate. Regardless of when the
operation was carried out, you display outward characteristics
identifying you as a eunuch, which may make you seem odd in the eyes of
other characters. You enjoy a certain freedom of movement among the
sexes, and it is likely you were made a eunuch for work at a harem or a
military school. This Flaw is only available to male characters.
