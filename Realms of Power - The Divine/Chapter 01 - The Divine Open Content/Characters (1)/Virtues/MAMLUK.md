*Minor, Social Status*

You are a former military slave, bought as a child, trained at a
military school and educated in Islam, and then freed upon reaching
adulthood. You are probably Turkish, but you may also be Mongol or even
Greek. You are likely to have been trained in the use of lances, bows,
shields, chainmail, and lamellar armor (same statistics) and one or two
swords, both on foot and on horseback, though this does not necessarily
mean that you will own these at the start of the game (storyguide's
option).

You may take Martial Abilities at character creation, and as a special
case you may also take the Ability Theology: Islam, even if you do not
have the Minor General Virtue Educated. This Virtue is only available to
male characters, and (for Companion characters only) is compatible with
both the Emir and *Muqta*' Virtues, as many Mamluks have climbed high in
the Muslim hierarchy.
