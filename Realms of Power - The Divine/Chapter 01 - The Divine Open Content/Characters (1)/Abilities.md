**ISLAMIC LAW\***

*Academic*

This is the Muslim version of Civil & Canon Law. The major sources of
Islamic Law are the Qur'an and the Sunna. **Specialties:** any one of
the sources of Islamic Law, particular topics, customs of a particular
area.

**THEOLOGY: ISLAM\***

*Academic*

This is the Islamic equivalent of Christian Theology. The major sources
for Islamic theology are the Qur'an and the Sunna. **Specialties:** the
Qur'an, debates, history, prophets.
