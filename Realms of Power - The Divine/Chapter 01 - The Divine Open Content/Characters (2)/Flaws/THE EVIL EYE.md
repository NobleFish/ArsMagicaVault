*Minor, Supernatural*

You character bears the Evil Eye, the uncontrolled potential to bring
harm to others around her. Characters making stress rolls in her
presence are subject to an additional botch die. Characters may protect
themselves through the use of an amulet or a ritual, decided upon by the
troupe, which does not need to be magical or supernatural in nature. A
magic resistance of 0 or more, including the effect of the Aegis of the
Hearth, is also sufficient to guard against this effect.
