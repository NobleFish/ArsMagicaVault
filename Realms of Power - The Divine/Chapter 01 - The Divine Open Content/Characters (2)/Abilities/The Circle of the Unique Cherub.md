The Circle of the Unique Cherub is a small school of Jewish mystics and
philosophers, numbering no more than a dozen, based in Provence and the
Rhine. Membership of the circle is by invitation and its existence is
not widely known. With the *Sefer Yetzirah* as their foundation text,
they seek to understand creation through mystical analogies, which leads
some to concentrate on Dominion Lore, while others have become
proficient with the mysteries of the Kabbalah. In particular, the Circle
is well-practiced with the creation of golems, of which there are
currently three.
