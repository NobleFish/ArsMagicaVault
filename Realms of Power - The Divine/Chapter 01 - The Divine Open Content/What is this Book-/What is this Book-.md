*Realms of Power: The Divine* supplements, expands, and updates the
section on the Divine realm and its human institutions in **Ars Magica**
Fifth Edition.

Inside this book are details on the history, beliefs and practices of
Christianity, Islam, and Judaism in Mythic Europe, as well as expanded
information on the Dominion, True Faith, saints, angels and miracles.
There is entirely new information on the powers of holy wizards and
pious mages. It includes a complete system for divinely aligned magic
and King Solomon's *Ars Notoria*. New character options are also
available to give more detail and flavor to your characters.
