The Jewish Radhanite merchants from southern Iraq are known to be gifted
with languages and to travel extensively across Mythic Europe and the
East. They are the perfectly placed to trade spices, silks, and other
exotic Eastern goods with the West in exchange for Western wool, tin,
and timber.

But notoriously, some Radhanites also trade in slaves. Much of the
Muslim world permits slavery and the Radhanites, with their far-reaching
trade routes, are able to bring black slaves from the Sudan as well as
fair-skinned slaves from the West. These slaves are usually given to the
Radhanites by Christian raiders in trade for other goods and the
merchants rarely capture or abduct slaves themselves.
