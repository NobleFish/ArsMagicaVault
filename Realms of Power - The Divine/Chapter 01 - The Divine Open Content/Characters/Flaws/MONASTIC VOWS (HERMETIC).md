*Major, Hermetic*

You have taken vows of poverty, chastity, and obedience to a religious
superior, which means that you cannot own vis and must only possess
functional magic devices. You cannot marry, and many magi might
interpret that as prohibiting binding a familiar. You must do what your
master commands in service of your order, though in return you can
expect aid and assistance when needed.
