*Minor, Status*

You were once a member of a cloistered order, but were cast out for some
great sin or gross incompetence, or perhaps you ran away and your abbot
might allow you to come back after a suitable punishment. Because of
this, you have a poor reputation at level 2 in the local area and within
the Church. You no longer need to observe your monastic vows of poverty,
chastity, and obedience, though you may still practice them as they
might be ingrained in your nature. You may take Academic Abilities
during character creation.

Female characters may take this Flaw as Failed Nun.
