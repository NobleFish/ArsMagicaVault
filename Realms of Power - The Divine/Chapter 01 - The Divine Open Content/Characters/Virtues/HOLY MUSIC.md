*Major, Supernatural Method*

This Virtue gives you Holy Music 1, a Supernatural Ability that works
like Meditation, allowing you to gain divine insight through music. You
must perform a song using your Presence + Music, and then use your
Perception to interpret the feelings you experience. This Ability is
primarily taught by the Cantores (see Instruments of God, Holy Methods
and Traditions).
