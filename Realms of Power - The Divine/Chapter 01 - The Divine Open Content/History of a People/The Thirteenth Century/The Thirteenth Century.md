With the fall of the Second Temple, the Jews are exiled from their
homeland, dispersed across Mythic Europe and the East in what is termed
the *diaspora*. This dispersal has given rise to two broad Jewish
cultures: the **Ashkenazim** found within Christian lands, predominantly
France and Germany, and the **Sephardim** of Muslim and Mediterranean
lands. While these communities are united in a common faith, many
cultural differences have arisen.
