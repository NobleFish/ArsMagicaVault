*It is a very different thing to foresee the changes of the temporal
order in the eternal and unchanging laws of God, which live eternally in
his wisdom, and, by participation in the Spirit of God, to know the will
of God, which is supremely certain and supremely powerful; this
privilege is granted, by a just distinction, to the holy angels. Thus
they are blessed as well as eternal. The good, which renders them
blessed, is God, by whom they were created; and the participation in his
life and the contemplation of his beauty is their never-failing joy.* -
St. Augustine, *City of God*, Book X, Chapter 22
