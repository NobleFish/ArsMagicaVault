*Major, General*

You own an unusually powerful relic with a Faith score of 3. The relic
also has one power, which should be agreed upon with the storyguide (see
Relics, below). As with the Minor General Virtue Relic, the item may be
built into any other item that you possess, like a sword or a pendant.

Owning such a powerful relic is a great responsibility, and your
character should behave in an appropriate manner. If you ever behave
impiously (as judged by the storyguide) your relic will cease to
function until suitable penance is made.
