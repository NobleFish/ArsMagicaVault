In order to have a holy person in Mythic Europe, you must first design
the character. To this end, many new Virtues, Flaws, and Abilities are
presented here for you to use when creating a character, or to modify
existing characters to bring them more in line with your vision of them
in the game. There are more of these in later chapters, for character
qualities that are generally associated with a particular faith; the
ones below are intended to be universal, available to any character with
an interest in the Divine.

Many of these Virtues and Abilities are associated with special
Supernatural Abilities called Holy Methods and Holy Powers, and rules
for how these may be used to work miracles can be found under Miraculous
Abilities, later in this chapter.
