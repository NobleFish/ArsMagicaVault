Tractatus, Dominion Lore (Quality 8); Tractatus, Theology: Islam
(Quality 8)

**Author:** Farid al-Din 'Attar (d. betw. 1190 and 1230)

**Availability:** Popular among Sufis (see below).

**Description:** A compilation of biographies of Muslim mystics by the
Persian mystical poet, pharmacist, and doctor, Farid al-Din 'Attar.
While an inspirational work for Sufis, the author has altered the
evidence of some of his sources to match his own religious ideas.

**Special Rule:** Make a note when the character reads this work. The
first time after doing so that the character makes a roll involving the
Knowledge studied, he or she should also roll a botch die, even if the
roll is being made on a simple die.
