Tractatus, Theology: Islam (Quality 7); Tractatus, Islamic Law (Quality
7); Tractatus, Dominion Lore (Quality 7)

**Author:** Ibn Ishaq (d. betw. 761 and 767)

**Availability:** Wide. However, some scholars, both earlier and
contemporary, regard parts of it as being of debatable veracity.

**Description:** A biography of the Prophet.
