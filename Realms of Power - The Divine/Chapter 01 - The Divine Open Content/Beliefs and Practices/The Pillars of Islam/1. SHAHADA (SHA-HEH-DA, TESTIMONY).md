Muslims witness to their faith through two statements, which signify
conversion to the faith. These are "**la ilaha illa Allah**" (there is
no god except God) and "**Muhammadun rasul Allah**" (Muhammad is the
messenger of God). These are repeated regularly as part of the ritual
prayers.

The *shahada* is a public declaration of divine supremacy, and as such
is unwelcome to the ears of agents of the Inferno. Any creature
belonging to the Infernal realm who hears someone say the *shahada* out
loud suffers momentary discomfort. Some, rare Infernal creatures are
more vulnerable to the *shahada*, in that it causes them actual pain.
Needless to say, these creatures do not publish their vulnerability, but
research can reveal this weakness.
