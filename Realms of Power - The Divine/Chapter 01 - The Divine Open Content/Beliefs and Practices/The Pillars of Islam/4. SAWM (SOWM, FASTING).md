All Muslims are required to fast during **Ramadan** (ra-ma-DAWN), the
ninth month of the Muslim year and the month when Muhammad first began
to receive the revelation. Fasting is undertaken from sunrise to sunset,
and includes abstention from food, drink, and sexual activity. Muslim
women who are having their period and the sick are exempted from
fasting. Travelers are also exempted but must make up the fast later.
The end of the fast is marked by a celebration known as the **'Id
al-Fitr** ('EED-ul-FITR, feast of the breaking of the fast), which is
one of the two major feast days of the Muslim year. Muslims may also
choose to fast at other times in atonement for certain sins, including
killing a Muslim by mistake or breaking an oath.

The score of Muslim Dominion Auras increases by one during the month of
Ramadan. See the boxed section on the Muslim calendar for more details.
