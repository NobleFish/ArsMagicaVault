**Powers:** *Acknowledgment of Evil, Resist Temptation, Grant of

Rabi'a was an early Muslim mystic and preacher, regarded by many as a
model for Sufis and particularly female Sufis. She was a particularly
strong advocate of the belief that one must truly love God, and it is
said that she was once seen running through the streets of her home town
of Basra with a torch and a pitcher of water, intending to burn Heaven
and douse Hell so that people would love God for Himself alone and not
from any desire for the first or fear of the second. While she lived
much of her life in Basra, after she died (in 752 or 801) she was buried
on the Mount of Olives outside Jerusalem.
