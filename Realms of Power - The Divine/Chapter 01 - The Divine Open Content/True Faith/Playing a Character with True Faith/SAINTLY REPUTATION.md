Legends of saints are larger than life — they are the superstars of
Mythic Europe. As a saintly character performs miracles and overcomes
adversity, he gains a reputation that can increase startlingly. Each
miracle he successfully prays for counts as one noteworthy action per
Faith Point he possesses, so long as there was a witness. So long he has
a True Faith Score, the character cannot gain a negative Reputation.

A fallen saint is a despised person, unwelcome in any circle other than
the darkest levels of Hell.
