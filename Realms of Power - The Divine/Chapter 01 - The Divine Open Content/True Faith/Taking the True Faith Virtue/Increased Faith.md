Players may wish to create a character with a True Faith Score of two or
more. Such characters should be made as Mythic Companions, since people
with a True Faith Score of more than one in Mythic Europe are very rare.
Each additional increase to the character's True Faith Score above that
granted by the True Faith Virtue is considered a Major Virtue. Thus,
having a True Faith Score of 2 requires two Major Virtues. Most saintly
characters are quite old although exceptions, such as Francis of Assisi,
of course exist.
