Holy men and women from the Bible were constantly tested by God. They
were terrorized by devils or cowed by angels, and Mythic Europe in the
Middle Ages is no exception. Contests with demons and monsters, or
frightening instructions from angels are common divine tests of faith. A
character on the cusp of gaining True Faith is likely to be diabolically
tempted with every imaginable pleasure of the flesh, while angels may
demand that the character give up his earthly belongings or renounce his
family.

*Story Idea*: A demon in the guise of an angel demands that the
character accuse an innocent midwife of diabolism.
