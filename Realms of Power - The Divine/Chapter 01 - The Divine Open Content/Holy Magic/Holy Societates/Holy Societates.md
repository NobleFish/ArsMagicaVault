Some traditions teach Holy Magic as one of their favored Abilities;
these are called **Holy Societates**. They are typically associated with
the Order of Hermes, and might assist a Gifted character down the
humbling path of magic aligned with the Divine. They can also accept
unGifted characters like any holy tradition, train apprentices in holy
magic, and are an ideal opportunity for players to create a holy magus
during character creation. A sample societas is below, and others may be
found in the setting chapters later in the book.
