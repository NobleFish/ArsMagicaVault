Unless the covenant and its inhabitants are particularly sinful, most
agents of the Divine will leave them to their own devices. The Divine
stays firmly in its own lands — around cities, villages and holy sites —
while faeries play in the woods and magi battle the elements on the
edges of civilization. In this option, the Divine only enters the story
when the characters seek it out; the war between Heaven and Hell is
largely irrelevant and only comes into play when the characters directly
act in its domain, such as proud magi casting spells in a cathedral or
throwing a *Ball of Abysmal Flame* at a mosque's Imam. Even in this
scenario, the Divine is not necessarily hostile to magic, but it *is*
protective of its flock.
