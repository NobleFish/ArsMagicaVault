The next set of orations are the second level of Ars Notoria and signify
a greater step toward divine knowledge. A successful recitation bestows
theological knowledge as well as the acumen to understand God's work on
earth.

With a successful recitation of the second ring, you gain 100 experience
points in the Academic Ability: Theology. Your Intelligence
characteristic also increases by 3 points, to a maximum total of +5.

This second level of Ars Notoria must be recited during the Winter
season.
