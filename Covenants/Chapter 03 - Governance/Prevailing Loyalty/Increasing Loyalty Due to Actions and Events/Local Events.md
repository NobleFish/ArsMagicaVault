Prevailing Loyalty is not affected if the covenant prospers unless this
is clearly due to the direct action of the magi, such as casting ritual
spells on the crops, publicly intervening in a war, or curing a plague.
In such cases, calculate the penalty that would have been imposed if
these events had occurred and grant it as a bonus to loyalty points
instead. (This may, of course, simply cancel out the effects of a
penalty that arose when the events occurred in the first place.)
