# Play Aids

The pages that follow contain a variety of materials useful in founding,
defining, and administrating covenants of the Order of Hermes according
to the new rules presented in this book.

The first four pages contain a sample covenant charter. It can be used
for your own covenant, and blank spaces are provided for including your
covenant’s name, its magi, the year, and so forth. The text of the oath
and charter are the same as those of the covenant Arae Flaviae. Because
it is such a commonly used and well-respected document in the Order, if
adopted, it will be automatically ratified by the Quaesitors. For more
information, see page 33.

Beginning on page 129 are ten pages of record sheets created
specifically to accommodate the new rules presented throughout this
book. You will find record sheets to track covenant statistics, mem
bership, wealth, income, laboratories, libraries, books, vis, and to
summarize a covenant’s annual activities. The pages of this appendix may
be photocopied for your personal use, but are copyright 2005 by Trident,
Inc. d/b/a Atlas Games. They are also available for PDF download from
the Ars Magica web site at www.atlas-games.com/ArM5.
