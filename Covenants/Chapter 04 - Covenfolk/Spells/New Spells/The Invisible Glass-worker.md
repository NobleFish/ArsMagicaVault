ReTe 4

R: Touch, D: Mom, T: Ind

The magus may transform a piece of glass into a finished form, as if a
glassworker had shaped and formed it. The magus must succeed with a
Perception + Finesse roll substituting for Craft: Glassworker against an
Ease Factor at least 3 higher than the mundane Ease Factor. Failure
reduces the glass to a slumped lump as if an incompetent beginner had
worked on it.

Note that in the thirteenth century, most glass is not particularly
clear — making clear glass is an alchemical process, beyond a
craft-spell. Glass often has bubbles and distortions unless a real
expert works on it, with a proportionately high Ease Factor.

*(Base 2, +1 Glass, +1 Touch)*
