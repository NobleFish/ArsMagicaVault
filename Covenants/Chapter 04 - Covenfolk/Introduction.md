# Covenfolk

Covenfolk surround a covenant’s magi, supporting them and aiding them.
For the storyguide they are bit-parts and extras who fill out the saga,
and provide roles in play for players whose magus or companion is
elsewhere. They provide players with variety and a chance to explore
alternative roles from their main characters; they also provide
opportunities for low humor and soap opera.

The covenfolk best-knownto most magi are the familiar shield grogs who
accompany and defend them in the heat of battle, providing the brief
calm needed to cast spells. There are many more covenfolk than just
these, however; indeed, covenfolk fill all the other places in a
covenant. This chapter discusses their roles and provides grog templates
for many, also discussing spelland magicbased substitutes for some
roles.

The rules allow covenants to purchase specialists with Build Points, but
these are blanks-with-Abilities, lacking even character sheets. They
cost Build Points because they allow the players to sketch the covenant
without effort. Characters created as grogs or companions, however, need
not be paid for with Build Points. This chapter encourages you to flesh
out the covenant with real characters — companions and grogs — rather
than simply purchasing specialists with Build Points, and it includes
character templates as examples for customization.

This approach is best-suited to the slow saga speed (two or more game
sessions per season) and medium saga speed (one to four seasons per
session), where the lifespans of magi extend beyond the scope of the
saga and individual grogs can be expected to survive for all or most of
it. But even in fast sagas (one or more years per session), grogs are
still worth creating and playing even as they age and die. Indeed, their
mortality and succession may be a theme of the saga. As a practical
matter, however, in such sagas some roles are likely to be filled with
paid-for specialists without character sheets.''
