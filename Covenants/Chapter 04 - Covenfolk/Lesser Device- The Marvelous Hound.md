Muto Animal (Corpus, Mentem) 34 Pen +0, constant effect

R: Touch, D: Constant Effect, T: Ind

This collar transforms a normal hound into an ideal servant, who walks
erect and has hands; has the intellect of a grog; but retains the
devotion, willingness to please. and loyalty of a hound. The creature’s
overall appearance remains hound-like, but its limbs and intellect are
transformed by the device.

One defect is that the hounds suffer Warping from the constant effect,
but over the lifetime of a dog, this is generally not too much of a
problem.

*(Base 15, +1 Touch, +14 levels always on, requisites free)*
