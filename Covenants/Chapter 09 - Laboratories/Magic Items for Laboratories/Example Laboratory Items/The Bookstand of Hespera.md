This item is a wooden lectern with enough space on its top for a Lab
Text, as well as an inkhorn, a small knife, and several quills. The
stand moves around to follow the magus so that he is never far from his
Lab Text. He only need turn to consult his text or make further notes.
The Bookstand adds two points to a Specialization in Texts. ReHe 15
(Base 10, +1 Conc), item maintains concentration (final level 20)
