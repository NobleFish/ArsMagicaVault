**Greater Guardian**: A powerful supernatural creature (Might 20 or
higher), which is not the familiar of the owner, resides in the lab and
acts as its protector, being well-disposed to the owner. Woe betide any
who should intrude. +(creature’s Intelligence) Safety, +1 Warping, +1
Aesthetics; 3 points on appropriate Art specialization(s).

**Greater Horde**: A large community of resident minor supernatural
creatures are at the owner’s command. They are intelligent enough to
serve a protective function and assist with simple lab tasks. +2 General
Quality, +1 Upkeep, +(creature’s Intelligence) Safety, +2 Aesthetics; 2
points on Rego or other appropriate Art specialization(s).
