Many magi believe that the Creator, or similar, placed a layer of
occluded meaning under the phenomena of the real world, and that magic
works by tapping into this hidden series of connections. In the same way
that rubies can affect blood because they are linked mystically, many
mundane things are linked mystically to other phenomena by their nature.
These links are more overt in magical objects, so rubies which naturally
contain Corpus vis are simply expressing a link that all rubies have.
Magi can collect objects to study these links, have unusual experiences,
or watch them in action.
