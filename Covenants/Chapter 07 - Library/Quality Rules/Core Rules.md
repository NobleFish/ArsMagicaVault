Summa Source Quality: Author’s Communication + 6 + bonuses due to
Virtues + bonus for writing to level less than half of Art score.
Quality may not exceed 2 x (Communication + Virtue bonuses + 6).

A summae’s level may not exceed half its author’s score in an Art at the
time of writing. It may instruct a magus until his Art equals the book’s
level.

Tractatus Source Quality: Author’s Communication + 6 + bonuses due to
Virtues.

A tractatus can only be read once for benefit.
