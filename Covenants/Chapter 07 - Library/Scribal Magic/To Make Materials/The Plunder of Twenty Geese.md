MuAn 15

R: Touch, D: Sun, T: Group Req: Rego (optional, raises level to 20)

This spell transforms up to 100 feathers, usually gained by plucking a
chicken, into the wing feathers of geese, which are the preferred source
of pens. A version of this spell, with a Rego requisite and level of 20,
strips and tempers the pens.

*(Base 1, +1 Touch, +2 Sun, +2 Group, +1 size, +1 optional requisite)*
