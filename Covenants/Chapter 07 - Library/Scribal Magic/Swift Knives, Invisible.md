PeAn 40

R: Eye, D: Mom, T: Ind Req: Rego

This spell reduces an animal to its components. A dead sheep, for
example, becomes a pile of unwashed wool, a hide, a collection of boned
joins of meat, a stack of organs, and a skeleton. The contents of the
alimentary tract vanish.

*(Base 30, +1 Eye, +1 destruction of alimentary contents, +0 cosmetic
requisite)*
