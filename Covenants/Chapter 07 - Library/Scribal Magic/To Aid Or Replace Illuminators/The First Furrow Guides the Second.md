CrIm 25

R: Voice, D: Sun, T: Group Req: Intellego

This spell lays the image of a book’s page over the top of a blank
sheet, so that a scribe can trace the page’s contents. This creates
identical copies of books. This is particularly useful when a book has
lost a page, but can be made complete with a page copied from one of its
sisters in another library. Used repeatedly over the course of a season,
it can assist in the creation of copies of Hermetic books, allowing an
unskilled magus to avoid the three extra botch dice for scribing without
Profession: Scribe. This spell is also known as The Principal Aide to
the Forger’s Art.

*(Base 1, +1 changes as pages turn, +2 Voice, +2 Sun, +2 Group, +1
requisite)*
