Knowledge-based libraries use the teaching rules rather than the book
study rules. They are purchased using the covenant creation guidelines
for teachers. An example of a knowledge-based library is found at the
Cave of Twisting Shadows , where the ghosts of Criamon magi teach
visitors their Arts. Spirits, demons, faeries who learned them from
humans, familiars that outlived their masters, and — according to myth —
angels can also teach the Arts in this way.
