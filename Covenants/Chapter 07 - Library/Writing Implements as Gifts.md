Hermetic magi offer the tools of literacy as gifts to honored sodales,
even those from militant Houses. To give a gift of stationery to a magus
is to acknowledge that his thoughts are important to future generations
of magi, and to encourage the magus to record them. This is particularly
true of magi from militant Houses, who cannot be certain to live the
fifteen years required to raise an apprentice. It is usual, however, to
give violent magi penknives, rather than rare inks or parchments.
