This young covenant has been founded deep in the woods to escape the
attention of the Church, nobility, and senior covenants of the Tribunal.
It has managed to develop good relations with the Court of the Bees, but
has, at the same time, aroused the ire of the minor faerie that used to
raid the beehives. This faerie has found a way past the Aegis and now
lives in the covenant, causing minor trouble.

Hooks: Faerie Aura (Minor), Faerie Court (Minor), Resident Nuisance
(Minor), Small Tower: Wooden (Minor)

Free: Gerontocracy Boons: Hidden Ways (Minor), Local Ally (Minor),
Unnatural Law (Minor), Useful Curse (Minor)
