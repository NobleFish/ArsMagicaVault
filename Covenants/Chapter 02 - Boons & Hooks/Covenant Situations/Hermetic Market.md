This covenant lies at the center of Europe, and holds Ceremonies of
Welcome each year, which allow apprentices to become magi. Many magi use
these gatherings to trade and gossip, and the covenant charges a small
fee for setting up a stall. It also controls surrounding businesses that
provide food, accommodation, and alcohol. Its servants act as tailors,
saddlers, and blacksmiths, to repair the equipment of travelers.

The covenant’s Unnatural Law refers to powerful truth magic that affects
the place. Here, broken oaths cause the tongue of liars to bleed, forged
items reveal themselves through chance, and light or heavy measures
break or refuse to weigh. This effect has Penetration 15, but is
believed by many to be higher.

Hooks: Ceremonies (Major), Hermetic Services (Major), Castle (Major)

Boons: Curtain Walls and Mural Towers: Courtyard Castle (Major),
Minority (Minor), Road (Major), Outbuildings — Fairground (Minor),
Unnatural Law: Truth Magic (Minor)
