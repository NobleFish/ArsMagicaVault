This is a classic Summer covenant. Set amidst cropland and supported by
serfs, it has a castle it can’t quite explain and a few faeries it has
annoyed, but no major problems, when the saga begins.

Hooks: Castle (Major), Faerie Court (Minor)

Free: Criminals , Faux Feudalism

Boons: Curtain Walls and Mural Towers: Octagonal Keep (Major),
Informants (Minor)
