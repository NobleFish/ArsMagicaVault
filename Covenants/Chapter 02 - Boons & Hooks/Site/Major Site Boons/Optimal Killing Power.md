Players familiar with other roleplaying games are sometimes tempted to
select the Boons that give their characters the greatest power, or
capacity to kill. It is easy to juggle the possible combinations of
Boons and Hooks to create covenants powerful enough to massacre
thousands. Troupes should select those combinations only if they are
interested in stories about the consequences of unbridled power. Ars
Magica offers a richer palette of stories than this.

Rather than optimizing a covenant’s battle strength, troupes should
select those Boons and Hooksthat favor the sorts of stories the troupe
enjoys. Young magi are already very powerful. The Storyguide isn’t going
to assault the player characters with waves of increasingly powerful
monsters unless that’s what the players want. Players more familiar with
games in which the characters kill and loot should consider what their
characters wanted to do after they became rich and powerful.
