A lot of Ars Magica’s flavor is lost if the players select a generic
covenant like the prototypical castle on a hill by a faerie wood. The
broad array of options this chapter provides, and its many examples,
encourage players to experiment. While new players may be most
comfortable with sagas set in a bland, tidy Middle Ages, this chapter
encourages troupes with a little more experience to explore the
intricacy and foreignness of Mythic Europe. Telling stories in Mythic
Europe should be like a trip to a foreign country: to enjoy it fully,
you need to embrace and take pleasure in how different it is.
