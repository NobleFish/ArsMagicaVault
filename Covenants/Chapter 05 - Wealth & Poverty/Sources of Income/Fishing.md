It is possible to make a living harvesting from seas and rivers, instead
of the land. This requires ready access to a river, or (preferably) a
lake or sea, and one or more crewed vessels, replete with nets. Fish
supplements the diet of many folk, and is usually eaten on Friday.
Whales may also be hunted, for their oil and meat, although this
requires a larger and more skilled crew.

Pros: You are unlikely to ever run out of fish.

Cons: Storms may damage or wreck fishing vessels; fish smell.

Magical Options: Enchanted boats, nets, sails, or oars; Rego Auram, Rego
Aquam, or Rego Animal to control the winds, the waves, and the catch,
respectively.
