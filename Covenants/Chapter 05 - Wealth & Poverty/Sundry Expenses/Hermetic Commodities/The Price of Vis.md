It is almost impossible to put a fixed monetary price on a pawn of vis.
Magi prize vis, their currency of choice, far more highly than silver.
Most consider that mundane things should be bought with silver, and
magical things with vis, and that is indeed usually what happens. There
are many wealthy covenants that would not even consider selling a pawn
of vis for less than a hundred pounds, since vis can be employed
effectively in various magical wealth-creation schemes. Yet at the same
time, plenty of covenants would not be able to spare more than a few
pounds to actually buy vis, since their silver is needed to run their
covenants. For this reason, the trading of vis for silver is rarely
undertaken, although it is not unheard of, especially amongst House
Mercere, who have a more practical sense of the relative worth of vis
and silver (see Houses of Hermes: True Lineages, page 87). In the cases
where vis is sold for silver, the price varies wildly between a couple
of pounds to a hundred or more pounds per pawn, depending on such
factors as geographical location, the particular Art of the vis, the
wealth and whims of local covenants and magi, and the availability of
vis relative to other Hermetic commodities in your saga. Having said
that, a base guideline of roughly ten pounds per pawn is a reasonable
rule of thumb for a typical saga. \|}
