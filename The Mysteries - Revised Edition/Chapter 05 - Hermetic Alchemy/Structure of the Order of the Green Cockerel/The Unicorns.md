This degree covers the alchemical principle of fermentation. The
symbolic planet and metal is Mercury, and the color is the purest white.
Unicorns are the ambassadors and emissaries of the cult, and watch over
mundane alchemists with a kindly eye, guiding experiments and helping
research through veiled hints and cryptic allusions. Now the Great Work
begins in earnest, as the Initiate learns the secret of the Virtue
Lesser Elixir.
