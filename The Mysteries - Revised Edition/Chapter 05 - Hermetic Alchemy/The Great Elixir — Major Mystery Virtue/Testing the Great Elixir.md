The Lab Texts for the previous Great Elixir enchantments give a similar
spells bonus of (effect magnitude) to the new Great Elixir effects, but
no other benefit. In addition, each attempt at the Great Elixir grants a
cumulative +1 bonus to the roll, to a limit of the maga’s Magic Theory.

*Example: Desperata has studied alchemy for many years, She is 157 years
old. She has Stamina +2, Magic Theory 10, and 53 Lesser Elixir Points —
an Elixir Level of 9. Her player rolls 4 + Stamina 2 + Lesser Elixir
Level 9 = 15 vs. Ease Factor 16 — failure. Desperata curses her bad
luck, and plans several seasons more to enchant different effects into
her Great Elixir, and then to test the new formula.*
