# Curious Common Magics

There are certain arcana known with the Order generally, yet of special
interest to certain of the Mystery Cults: the mysteries of the memory
palace, how to render spells permanent by evoking powerful magical
spirits, and the secrets of Hermetic Alchemy, Planetary Magic, and other
curious and potent lore known to magi who study these magical paths.
These Virtues are available to all within the Order of Hermes without
Initiation, yet only the mystae fully comprehend their significance and
potential, and understand the deeper secrets to which they provide a
cryptic introduction.
