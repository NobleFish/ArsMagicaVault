*Any magus may have a Minor Magical Focus or Minor Potent Magic covering
effects governed by a single astrological house.* *Spells cast with a
benefit from Potent Magic or a Focus in a sign, planet, or house are
Astrological spells. Other spells cast by the maga need not be
Astrological. Astrological spells are discussed further in Chapter 6:
Hermetic Astrology.*
