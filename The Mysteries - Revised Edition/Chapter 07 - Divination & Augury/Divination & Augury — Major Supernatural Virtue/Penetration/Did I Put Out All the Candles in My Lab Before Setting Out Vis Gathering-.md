This can be formulated either as a Group target (the candles) or a Room
target (the laboratory). The guideline is level 1 “locate a fire.”

As a Casual Divination, standing on the threshold of the room before
leaving, the Ease Factor is 4. If considering the matter en route to
dinner, holding an Arcane Connection to the laboratory, the Ease Factor
is also 4 (Base 1, +1 touching AC, +2 Room).
