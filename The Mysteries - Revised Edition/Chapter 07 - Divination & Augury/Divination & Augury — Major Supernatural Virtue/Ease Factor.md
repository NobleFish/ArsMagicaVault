Any question that can be posed as a non-Ritual Hermetic Intellego spell
(of any Form), of any range and target (other than Sense targets), and
of Momentary Duration can be attempted using Divination.

In Divination, an Arcane Connection held in the hand may be interpreted
as if the diviner were Touching the target (rather than at Arcane
Range).

Divination may answer questions about Boundary Targets without the
normal ritual requirement.
