*Once upon a time, I, by my power, turning air into water, and water
again into blood, and solidifying it into flesh, formed a new human
creature — a boy — and produced a much nobler work than God the Creator.
For He created a man from the earth, but I from air — a far more
difficult matter; and again I unmade him and restored him into air, but
not until I had placed his picture and image in my bedchamber, as a
proof and memorial of my work.* — Simon Magus, from the Clementine
