Cr(In)Te 25

R: Touch, D: Mom, T: Ind, Ritual

This spell creates an exact duplicate of an existing mechanism. An
armillary sphere itself is complex and fiddly, but nonmagical. The
precision of the mechanism is limited by the precision of the duplicated
source.

Status-conscious magi may prefer the alternative Level 45 ritual
Duplicate the Noble Mechanism, which creates a precious metal (silver or
gold, or both) mechanism.

*(Base 5 create metal, +1 Touch, +2 Group, +2 for finesse, +1 for
Intellego requisite).*

A similar spell without an Intellego requisite, Craft the Mechanism, can
create a new mechanism, but is limited by the magus’s understanding of
what he is creating and requires Finesse to match design to instant
magic.
