A periapt was originally a small amulet with astrological symbols, which
contained a simple one-use enchantment; Hermetic theory has allowed magi
to generalize this to make astrological amulets as any form of Hermetic
charged device.

By creating a special Horoscope for the season, the magus is able to
infer the correct aspects of the heavens to create amulets with greater
efficiency than normal: the charges resulting equal those that the magus
would produce if he already had a Lab Text for the enchantment.
