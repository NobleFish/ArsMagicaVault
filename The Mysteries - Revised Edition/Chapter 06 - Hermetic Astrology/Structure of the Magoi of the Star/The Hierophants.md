The greatest astrologers of all, the three Hierophants who lead the
Magoi of the Star are said to know many Virtues concerning Astrological
magic, and be able to Initiate a large number of astrological Virtues on
those who attain this position of cult leadership. The three current
Hierophants’ true identities are mysterious, and only when one of them
dies or passes into Final Twilight will a new opening become available.
