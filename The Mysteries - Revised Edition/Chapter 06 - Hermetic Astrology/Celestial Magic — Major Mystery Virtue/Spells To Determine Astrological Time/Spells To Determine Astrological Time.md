These spells do not require Astrological Virtues to learn or cast, but
the information is most useful to magi using Astrological Durations.

To cast a spell relying on a measured astrological time it is sufficient
to measure the astrological time near the target, provided the
measurement point and the target of subsequent spells are close enough.
As such, these measurement spells are normally indirect and Aimed rather
than Targeted (see ArM5, page 86).
