Some would say of the dreamscape that it is all an extended dream. No
physical form has ever crossed from a dream into the dreamscape, and
no-one has ever taken real-world vis through; likewise the normal Arcane
Connection to the entranced body of the spirit traveler is lost while in
the dreamscape. Thus, no permanent change can be made real in the
dreamscape, although dream forms and dream vis suffice for almost all
purposes.

If one opens a portal from the dreamscape to dream, the traveler can
take no real-world vis, and so cannot make any real changes to the
remote dream. Does that remote dream actually exist — or is it in fact a
dream of a dream? Dream magi claim it is real, but it may not be so.
Some even claim to have arranged rendezvoused with other dream travelers
equipped with real-world vis on the body of the remote dreamer.
