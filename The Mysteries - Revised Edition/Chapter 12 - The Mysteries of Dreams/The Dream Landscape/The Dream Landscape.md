There are natural portals from dreams to the imagination and memory of
the dreamer. These portals are rare and hidden, but some magi claim to
know spells to help find them. Memory and imagination both have portals
to each other, and back to dream, and the return portals are usually
much easier to find than the dream portals.

Physical travelers entering dreams also leave portals that lead back to
the places they entered from.

Any travelers in dreams — spiritual traveler, physical traveler, demon,
or faerie — may pass through dream portals if they can find them.
