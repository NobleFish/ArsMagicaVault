Mu(In)Me 30

R: Touch, D: Dream, T: Dream, Mystery

The magus touches a dreaming sleeper, and the target dream changes to
include the caster’s spirit; the caster slips into a trance while in the
dream.

The spiritual traveler appears in the dream with his clothes, a dream
copy of his talisman (if any), and dream vis equivalent to any vis left
on his body. The magus has an Arcane Connection to his own body, and may
use this to leave dream before the spell ends.

*(Base: 20, +1 Touch, +1 Dream duration)*
