This Mystery deals with the substance of dream, giving the magus the
power to enter into dreams, becoming part of the stuff of dreams. Once
in a dream, the traveler may move and interact with the dream world
around her. If the dream comes to an end, any spiritual traveler in the
dream is evicted and wakes up in the real world.

in spells.
