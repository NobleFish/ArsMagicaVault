Dreams of all things can be found, including dreams of raw vis (even if
the dreamer has no idea what the substance is). A traveler in a dream
can use dream vis like normal vis, except that it can have no effect
whatsoever outside dreams. It is quite easy for magi to find dream vis
in dreams. Dream vis is nearly always Imaginem vis (the general Form of
all within dream).

To have any effect beyond a dream, including persistence between dreams
and moving dream objects into the real world, real-world vis is needed.
To use real-world vis within a dream, it must either be on the body of a
traveler (linked by Arcane Connection), or brought in by a physical
traveler (see The Greater Dream Grimoire). \|}
