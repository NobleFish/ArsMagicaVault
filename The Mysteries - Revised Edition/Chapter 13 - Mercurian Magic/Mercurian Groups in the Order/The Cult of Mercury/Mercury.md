The Roman deity Mercury, often represented as a youth wearing winged
sandals, was primarily associated with trade, profit, and commerce, but
also looked after travelers, roads, and thieves. The Latin words
mercator and merx (“merchant”) are related to Mercury. His main
festival, Mercuralia, was celebrated on May 15th. The Greeks knew him as
Hermes, and his symbol is the Caduceus, or a winged staff with entwined
serpents.
