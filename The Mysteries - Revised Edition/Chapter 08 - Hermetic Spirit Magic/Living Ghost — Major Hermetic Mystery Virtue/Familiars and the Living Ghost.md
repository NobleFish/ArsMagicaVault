If a magus have a spirit familiar, then it automatically stays with him,
and requires no extra vis to do so, as it is already a free spirit.

Otherwise, his familiar faces a choice when he chooses the path of
death: it may remain in life and lose the magus, or join him in living
death by sharing the Ritual ordeal, provided the magus expends
additional pawns of raw vis equal to the Familiar Bond Score/5 (round
up). (The Mystery allows for this — see also Appendix A: Immortal Magi,
Becoming Immortal with a Familiar.)
