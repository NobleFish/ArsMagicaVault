All theurgic spells are researched using a modified Lab Total:

If the magus knows Names of Power governing Invocation or the magic of
the invoked spirit, he may add their bonuses to his Lab Total and
Casting Score, to a limit of his Magic Theory score. If a magus works
Names of Power into his Lab Total, they become a fixed part of the spell
design, and he must utter them out loud, in a firm voice — so that the
names’ attention is gained — as he casts the Invocation spell. Those
around him may react to the names even if they do not react to the
Invocation spell itself.
