If a magus has a spirit familiar, then it automatically stays with him,
and requires no extra vis to do so, as it is already a free spirit.

Otherwise, the familiar faces a choice when its master ascends: it may
remain in life and lose him, or join him in ascendancy by sharing the
ritual Ordeal, provided additional pawns of raw vis equal to the
Familiar Bond Score/5 (round up) are expended in the Ritual. (The
mystery allows for this — see also Appendix A: Immortal Magi, Becoming
Immortal with a Familiar.)

Ascended heroes with companion familiar spirits form some of the famous
heroic pairs in the Hall of Heroes. \|}
