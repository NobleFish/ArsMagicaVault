After an immortal magus studies and gains experience points, whether in
Arts or Abilities, his unchanging nature asserts itself and the change
fades over the course of the next season as the spirit “repairs” itself.
The magus can prevent this by spending the next season creating an
enchanted effect to bind the experience into his talisman. This needs
already opened spaces before the enchantment starts, so the magus is
advised to prepare before study begins.

An immortal should be especially careful not to lose the device, as it
contains part of his life.

The immortal transformation informs the magus how to perform these rites
— once a magus is immortal, it seems obvious, but mortal magi lack the
insight, which comes from the Mysteries.
